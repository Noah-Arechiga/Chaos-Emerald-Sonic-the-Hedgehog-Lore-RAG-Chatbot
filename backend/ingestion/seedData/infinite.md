Infinite (インフィニット, Infinitto?) is a character that appears in the Sonic the Hedgehog series. He is an anthropomorphic jackal and the former leader of the Jackal Squad mercenary group, holding the title of the "Ultimate Mercenary" (究極傭兵, Kyuukyoku Youhei?). Infinite originally targeted Doctor Eggman before joining the Eggman Army, only to fall at the hands of Shadow the Hedgehog while defending one of Eggman's facilities. Ashamed of his failure, Infinite chose to wield the power of the Phantom Ruby not only as a means of becoming stronger, but also to abandon his previous identity.

With his newfound power, Infinite aided Eggman in expanding the Eggman Empire to its largest size to date after defeating Sonic the Hedgehog. Near the end of the War to Take Back the Planet, Infinite faced Sonic and the Avatar multiple times, until he was defeated during the decisive battle for the planet, losing the Phantom Ruby soon after. Infinite secretly escaped by faking his demise and eventually met someone who showed him an Astroscopic Lens. Infinite mastered the artifact's abilites and sought to obtain the other Lenses to restore his power. However, he would eventually be defeated again by the combined efforts of Team Chaotix and their allies, though seemingly not for good.


## Creation


During the original drafts of Sonic Forces, Infinite was originally called "Zero". His final name was intended to reference the nature of his ability.

According to the producer of Sonic Forces, Shun Nakamura, Infinite was created with an impressive and strong presence in mind since he would be accompanied by old bosses in Sonic Forces. This is the reason why he was made stronger and faster than Sonic the Hedgehog. His nature likewise inspired the staff to elaborate on his origin story. Sonic Team was also particular about giving him a cool, but evil design, with his mask especially being designed to emphasize his evil. Character artist Shinkichi Tanahashi elaborated that Infinite was designed as a vicious existence that stood in the way of the bonds of friendship that Sonic represents. His overall design motif was derived from the namahage, a demon-like being in Japanese folklore, with focus on key elements like the word "cruel" and the phrases, "clinging to power" and "not believing in bonds". Since namahage look scary, they ensured he had a striking and cool silhouette. Tanahashi noted as well that the design process was difficult, but that the silhouette of Infinite's face logo worked well during development.

Infinite went through several designs. His original design was that of a ghost-like figure, with one of the examples including one with monster-like horns. A common element his early design shared with his final one was his mask and large hands with pointed claws. The team later proposed a design with a buffalo motif, but it was scrapped in favor of the jackal motif, which became the outline for the character's final design. Infinite's species, the jackal, was inspired by Anubis, the Egyptian god of the afterlife who possessed the head of a jackal. These designs included bandages to create an eerie sense of coolness and a prehensile tail. It was also around this time that Infinite's mask began to take on its current form, albeit with scars on it. This mask was given its patterns inside the ears (with one pattern on one side and the inverted colors on the other) because it gave it a geometric eeriness. Late in Infinite's design process however, it was decided that his bandages and the scars on his mask were to be removed because they made him "look weak." Following then an adjustment in colors, Infinite reached his current design.


## Appearance


Infinite is an anthropomorphic jackal with black and white fur. He has a ribcage-like patch of some of his fur's countershading around the upper half of his body, a bushy tail with a quintet of curved tufts at its tip where the tail bears its countershading and long, white and stiff dreadlocks. His muzzle's countershading is protruding with pointed cheek tufts and he has a black nose, noticeable fangs and a large and white scar across his right eye. Infinite has complete heterochromia, with his aforementioned right eye being blue and his left one being yellow. For attire, he wears a white neckerchief and black gloves with silver and "greater/less than" symbol-like designs on the backs, covering his sharp claws. Infinite additionally wears pointed and metal hi-tops which bear a black and silver coloration, triangular straps and his personal infinity symbol engraved on the red soles.

After abandoning his previous identity, Infinite began wearing a silver-colored and metal mask that has a pair of hollow oval-shaped patterns on the insides of the ears, with the right ear bearing a black canal with white patterns and the left ear bearing a canal with the opposite color scheme, and a somewhat polygonal underside. While Infinite's left eye is visible through a red and crack-styled visor, his right eye is covered by a black and lightning bolt-shaped piece akin to an eyepatch that is connected to his mask's right ear canal, which glows red when he uses his powers.

Infinite also once possessed the finalized version of the Phantom Ruby prototypes that was fused to his chest. When Infinite channeled the power of the gemstone, he could surround his figure in a red aura, with particles that resembled forms of pixelated glitching effects scattered across his body. After his first defeat, Infinite lost the prototype and the aura.


## History



## Past


Infinite was the captain of the Jackal Squad, a band of mercenaries raiding others for a profit. Over time, he gained a reputation as the "ultimate mercenary". However, Infinite grew disillusioned with the world, believing other people to be petty and shallow. He came to see the world as worthless and contemptible.

One day, Infinite led his squad on a raid of Doctor Eggman's base in the Arsenal Pyramid in an attempt to steal his devices for a hefty bounty. Using the Phantom Ruby, Eggman created an army of Phantom Egg Pawns to deter the Jackal Squad. Slipping past the Egg Pawns, Infinite ordered his squad to handle the robots while he moved in to slash at Eggman with his sword. However, Infinite accidentally struck the Phantom Ruby instead, causing it to show him an illusion of what he desired the most: a desolate and destroyed world. Eggman, who also saw the vision, took a liking to Infinite and offered to make him the leader of his army so they could accomplish their goals together. Realizing he had grown bored of the world as it was, Infinite accepted Eggman's offer despite his squad's protests.

Months before the War to Take Back the Planet, Infinite and the Jackal Squad were assigned by Eggman to guard his facility in Mystic Jungle. When Shadow the Hedgehog came to raid and destroy the facility however, the Jackal Squad were easily defeated by the hedgehog. Berated by Eggman for his squad's failure, Infinite caught up to Shadow and tried to ambush him as payback and to live up to his title. However, the ultimate mercenary was caught off-guard by Shadow's power and was soundly thrashed by the hedgehog, who brutally insulted him before teleporting away. Reeling in defeat, Infinite realized, much to his horror, that he was shivering in fear from the encounter. He then fell into an emotional breakdown, screaming that he was "not weak".

Spiraling in self-hatred, Infinite began wearing a mask to hide his old face while seeking more power. He agreed to be infused with Eggman's finalized Phantom Ruby replica, undergoing a number of experiments in the innermost area of the Eggman Empire Fortress, which granted him virtually limitless power. Infinite then became Eggman's right-hand-man, the duo plotting to use the Phantom Ruby to defeat Sonic and anyone else in their way to finally conquer the world.

Shortly after obtaining the Phantom Ruby, Infinite was dispatched by Eggman to deal with E-123 Omega, who was wreaking havoc in one of Eggman's bases. Infinite effortlessly defeated Omega with his new abilities. Soon after, Shadow arrived at the facility to locate Omega, and Infinite welcomed him to their reunion. When Shadow revealed that he did not remember him, Infinite proceeded to tell Shadow his backstory, then trap him in a virtual reality. Although Shadow broke free, Infinite was nonetheless confident in the power he now possessed. After hinting at his plan to defeat Sonic, the facility began to explode as he made his escape.


## Sonic Forces


In Sonic Forces, about a month after Infinite's last meeting with Shadow, Eggman's army launched an attack on the City. When Sonic arrived to stop it, Infinite attacked him alongside Phantom Copies of Shadow, Zavok, Metal Sonic, and Chaos that he had created. Overwhelming Sonic with his power and Phantom Copies, Infinite defeated and captured Sonic.

After imprisoning Sonic aboard the Death Egg, Infinite and Eggman continued their conquest unrivaled. Led by Infinite's Phantom Ruby powers, the Eggman Army was able to conquer over 99% of the planet. In the meantime, Infinite worked alongside his Phantom Copies to instill fear into the subjugated population. A rag-tag Resistance formed by Sonic's friends was formed to continue the fight for freedom, but they proved to be of little threat to Infinite.

A few months after Sonic's defeat, Infinite wiped out a Resistance squad in the City, before turning his attention to the sole survivor, the Avatar. Mocking and taunting them, Infinite allowed the terrified Avatar to flee with their life, relishing in their fear.

Six months after Sonic's defeat, Infinite was present at the Spaceport, fighting Espio's forces in the orbital tunnel while the Avatar hijacked a shuttle to the Death Egg. Sometime later, Infinite traveled to a facility in Mystic Jungle to dispose of leftover Phantom Ruby prototypes. There, Infinite encountered Silver the Hedgehog, whom he decided to defeat to further the Resistance's despair. Although Infinite overpowered Silver, he dropped a Phantom Ruby prototype during the battle without noticing and the escaped Sonic intervened before he could finish him off. Intrigued by Sonic's return, Infinite attempted to intimidate him, only to be brushed off, as Sonic was more interested in the secret to his powers. Infinite refused to entertain him and instead attacked. Although Sonic put up a better fight than before, Infinite still beat him, seeing him as no threat and letting him live.

Meeting Dr. Eggman in Green Hill, Infinite destroyed what was thought to be the last Phantom Ruby prototype. He then told Eggman that he let Sonic live; while Eggman was greatly troubled by this, Infinite dismissed his concerns. After noticing but ignoring a nearby sound, Infinite asked Eggman about the plan to wipe out the Resistance, and the two relished in the thought of total victory before Infinite departed.

When the Resistance conducted Operation Big Wave in Metropolis, Infinite arrived to unleash his Phantom Ruby's power. Creating gravity shifts and giant monstrous versions of himself, Infinite cast the Resistance into chaos, resulting in them suffering huge casualties before withdrawing. Watching the mayhem from a rooftop, Infinite wondered aloud if he would ever face a challenge, just as the Avatar from months ago approached. Infinite intimidated them, but the Avatar stood their ground, leading to a battle. After the fight, Infinite finally recognized the Avatar. He proceeded to summon cannons to finish them, but unknown to Infinite, the Avatar had picked up the lost Phantom Ruby prototype, which disabled the attack. Infinite grew confused, but brushed it off and gloated that the Resistance would be wiped out in only two days, before flying away.

Once the Resistance destroyed the primary Phantom Ruby power source aboard the Death Egg, the Eggman Empire was left with only a backup reactor beneath the Eggman Empire Fortress, so Eggman ordered a reluctant Infinite to withdraw to Metropolis. As Sonic and the Avatar cornered them, Infinite opened a portal to Null Space, which sucked them both inside. However, while Infinite and Eggman fled Metropolis, Sonic and the Avatar escaped and caught back up to them. Infinite was only slightly displeased by this, as Eggman furiously ordered a full retreat to the Eggman Empire Fortress.

When the Resistance Army stormed the Eggman Empire Fortress, Infinite faced them with a legion of Phantom Copies alongside the rest of Eggman's army. Infinite easily pushed Sonic and the Resistance back, only to be caught off-guard by a repaired and vengeful E-123 Omega. Growing irritated, Infinite initiated Eggman's endgame: using his Phantom Ruby, Infinite created a second sun that he planned to drop on the Resistance, which would certainly destroy them all. As Infinite was about to drop the sun, however, it was suddenly disabled by the Avatar and their spare Phantom Ruby prototype, causing it to shatter.

Drained by creating the sun, Infinite withdrew to the center of the fortress to recharge his Phantom Ruby, although Sonic soon caught up. Confident that he could still beat Sonic, even in his weakened state, Infinite engaged Sonic in battle until his Phantom Ruby was fully recharged. However, he was interrupted by the Avatar, who joined forces with Sonic to finally defeat Infinite. As Infinite struggled to comprehend his defeat, Sonic informed him that he was really defeated by heart, soul and friendship, all things that he and his Phantom Copies lacked. Unable to control his powers anymore, Infinite was suddenly pulled towards the reactor along with his Phantom Ruby.

As a punishment for his failures, Eggman later forcibly removed the Phantom Ruby from Infinite, leaving him powerless. However, Infinite would use the last of the Ruby's power to fake his demise and escape.


## Otherworld Comedy


In the Otherworld Comedy story, as a result of Dr. Eggman using a weapon still charged with the Phantom Ruby's power to envelop the world in illusions known as Otherworlds, Infinite is put in the role of a J-pop idol alongside Shadow the Hedgehog.

After a successful concert in what was once an Eggman Empire base, Shadow and Infinite have an argument backstage. Shadow believes their performance should be perfect, while Infinite believes his performance is more authentic. As the argument escalates, the amnesiac Shadow's mind briefly reacts to the word "purpose," prompting him to kick Infinite away and tell him never to show his pathetic face again.

Infinite is absent for the rest of the story until after Dr. Eggman's defeat. Beneath the molten ruins of Eggmanland, the doctor holds a spare he made of the modified Phantom Ruby prototype, planning to use it for his next scheme. Suddenly, Infinite appears, still in his costume, and swipes it from his hands. He briefly contemplates putting it on his chest to regain the power he once lost, but his pride makes him suddenly filled with doubt on whether he should really have to resort to using it again. Before he can make a decision, his body begins to disappear into light, which causes the modified prototype to drop into lava, destroying it forever. Infinite smiles and nods, as if understanding it was for the best, and fades away. His ultimate fate is left intentionally ambiguous.


## The Chaotix Casefiles


Following his defeat at the end of the war, Infinite began to wander the world aimlessly, frustrated by his failure and the loss of the Phantom Ruby. Eventually, he met a mysterious benefactor who showed him an Astroscopic Lens, which Infinite took. He learned to master the artifact's illusion abilities, and began seeking the other Lenses in order to restore his powers. Using the artifact, Infinite posed as Knuckles the Echidna to steal an Astroscopic Lens in display from a museum in Station Square, and would later steal another Lens from Miss Edna Knackerworth's house.

Infinite later disguised himself as Miles "Tails" Prower to infiltrate the Mineral Museum in Spiral Hill Village to steal another Astroscopic Lens in display. He would deceive Tangle the Lemur into believing Vector the Crocodile had contacted him for backup. When the lemur questions "Tails" how he was able to enter despite all of the museum's entrances being blocked, Infinite simply punches her and rushes into the building. Once the heroes are alerted of his presence, Infinite turns off the museum's lights, leaving everything in darkness and causing confusion amongst the heroes. Once the lights are back on, Infinite has disguised as Tangle herself, standing next to the real one. When Whisper the Wolf shoots with her Variable Wispon in order to determine which is the real Tangle, Infinite dodges the blast at the last second. He then turns the lights off once again and steals the Lens.

Infinite would later break into the Eggman base Vector and Sonic were captive in and reveal himself before the heroes and the doctor. Noticing Eggman holds the last Astroscopic Lens, Infinite betrays his former boss and steals it, completing the set. Infinite then retreats and proceeds to place the full set of Astroscopic Lenses on a platform in the sky, which causes it to project a beam onto him that amplifies his powers. Infinite then creates duplicates of himself to distract the heroes and begins to explain his motivations for seeking power, his views on the world and his future plans with his newfound power. As Eggman is about to retreat, Infinite channels the energy of the Lenses onto his fist and punches the doctor into the horizon: an act of revenge for attempting to assimilate him along with his Phantom Ruby prototype back in the war.

Infinite then enlarges himself to a gigantic size and procceds to unleash a horde of fake copies onto the heroes, continuing to boast about his plans. However, once Espio and Charmy remove all of the Lenses from the floating platform, and as they fall off the Chaotix Copter and shatter on impact, Infinite loses his powers and returns to normal size. As the heroes try to apprehend him, Infinite pulls out a bomb and detonates it, taunting them about "prolonging the unavoidable" and promising to meet Sonic again in the future "when [Sonic] least expects it", mysteriously vanishing after the explosion dissipates.

## Personality


Before acquiring the Phantom Ruby’s power, Infinite was a selfish and arrogant individual whose Jackal Squad had a vicious reputation. Openly encouraging his subordinates to go for the kill, he had no qualms about taking lives to obtain bounties. He also had a large ego, using the title of the "ultimate mercenary". The Phantom Ruby revealed that Infinite's deepest desire at this time was a desolate world: this is because he hated the people of the world, calling them shallow and petty, and wishing to tear it all down. He then ignored his comrades and chose to join the Eggman Empire to achieve his goal. While working for Doctor Eggman, he held little respect for his employer.

However, Infinite's ego was completely shattered when he was so easily beaten by Shadow the Hedgehog. Infinite was furious about Shadow defeating his subordinates, but he completely detested his own terrified reaction to Shadow's power in this encounter, conflicting with his preconceived views of himself. This sent him into a spiral of fear and self-hatred, leading him to cast away his previous identity and wear a mask so he no longer had to see his own cowardly face.

Once his pride was crushed and he was faced with true fear, Infinite comforted his ego by using the Phantom Ruby to grow stronger so he could inflict fear in others instead. Infinite again arrogantly believed he was unbeatable, his pride returning a hundredfold. He showed great pleasure in destruction and sadistically tormented those in the Resistance as he crushed their forces, relishing in his own ability to make others fear him, the same fear and despair that he once felt. At his core, Infinite has a selfishly individualistic outlook on the world and a sadistic fascination with fear, a trait that is emphasized by his eerie design; especially by his inspiration, the namahage, a Japanese monster known for frightening children. His goal to trap the world in a "never-ending nightmare" as revenge also indicates his love of tormenting others with fear.

Infinite speaks in a theatrical and self-important manner, with Shadow noting that he had a "big mouth". He also proved to have a darkly dry sense of humor, often mocking his enemies with cruel jokes. Unlike Dr. Eggman, who excitedly reveals his plans while gloating to his enemies, Infinite never revealed anything about himself, remaining aloof and mysterious. He claimed that it was natural for the strong to vanquish the weak, placing himself at the top of the hierarchy.

Though his confidence was not without validity, Infinite's hubris was so great that he would sometimes leave his enemies to grovel in fear rather than finish them off, believing fully that they were no threat to him; this may also be inspired by his encounter with Shadow, who did the same to him. Similarly, his confidence was such that he even rubbed Eggman in on the latter's failure to stop Sonic repeatedly especially compared to his defeating him on the first try. On some level, Infinite also desired a challenge, as he wondered if the world had any challenges left after foiling Operation Big Wave. He was, however, able to acknowledge attributes of his opponents he deemed commendable, as he acknowledged that Omega was "spirited" before defeating him and during his rematch with Sonic, he begrudgingly acknowledged that Sonic had improved since their last fight. In the Japanese version, he uses the impolite pronoun "ore" (俺) and often calls his opponents trash.

Despite his illusory power, Infinite was clearly still scarred by his encounter with true power. He remained highly insecure and loathed his "old self", assuming that Shadow didn't remember him because he was too pathetic back then. His illusion of Omega also descended into a mantra of "I AM NOT WEAK", seemingly reflecting Infinite's inner torment. Infinite also refused to count on anyone but himself, forming illusory Phantom Copies as little more than drones in his army. This was ultimately his downfall, as the teamwork of Sonic and the Avatar was capable of defeating his lone wolf mindset. Both Sonic and Amy noted that Infinite's existence was ultimately a sad and lonely one that he imposed on himself.


## Powers and abilities



## Base abilities


Before obtaining the finalized Phantom Ruby, Infinite was apparently a strong fighter in his own right, his skills having earned him the title as the "ultimate mercenary". However, he was nowhere near as powerful as the likes of Shadow the Hedgehog.

On his own, Infinite was shown to be fairly fast and agile in his movements, being capable of high-speed leaps and lunges across several meters, although his speed was easily outclassed by Shadow's Chaos Control abilities. He has also demonstrated above-average durability by taking a sound thrashing from Shadow and then getting back up. He was also skilled at swordplay.

In The Chaotix Casefiles, after losing the Phantom Ruby, Infinite adopts a more cunning and methodical approach. He operates covertly to steal the Astroscopic Lenses, disguising himself as others to infiltrate secure locations. He demonstrates quick thinking under pressure, dodging attacks and escaping using misdirection, and shows strategic patience, waiting until his opponents are distracted before striking.


## Phantom Ruby abilities


After being fused with the Phantom Ruby, Infinite gained powers superior to those of the Master Emerald's. In his own words, Infinite had acquired "unrivaled" power and the "ultimate strength" through his gemstone. Infinite's newfound strength proved so great that he was able to effortlessly defeat figures like E-123 Omega and Silver the Hedgehog, and ultimately best even Sonic the Hedgehog twice, the first time with great ease. He was even able to single-handedly overwhelm the entire Resistance army during Operation Big Wave. His power was such that not even the Miles Electric was able to give an accurate reading of his capabilities.

After his fusion with the finalized Phantom Ruby copy, Infinite acquired the ability to generate, destroy, and manipulate virtual realities, which he could turn into virtual reality projections to interact with reality. Said virtual realities are illusions, which Infinite creates by exercising control of one's visual and depth perception by feeding the brain false information. He was able to use his projections to enable his virtual realities to interact with the real world in order to bestow powers on himself. Having no limitations to the illusions he could create, Infinite had, within the influence of his Phantom Ruby, unlimited abilities.

Within his Ruby's virtual realities, Infinite was able to make himself faster and stronger than even Sonic. His speed was so great that he was able to outpace Sonic while he was boosting. Similarly, his strength became potent enough to knock away someone of Sonic's size with enough force to crack a concrete arch upon impact. He also used his Ruby to give himself the power of flight and levitation, deadly energy beam emission, energy ball projection, self-replication, size enhancement, shockwave manipulation, gravity distortion, object materialization and teleportation. He was even able to manifest giant monsters in his likeness, trap beings in virtual realities, create a whole sun that moved according to his will and mimic the special techniques of others, such as Metal Sonic's exclusive V. Maximum Overdrive Attack.

In battle, Infinite primarily used his finalized Phantom Ruby copy to generate crimson cubes that he could skillfully and willingly move around. If an opponent got struck by these cubes, they would be sent into a virtual reality where they were assailed by objects manifested by his Ruby's power. He could generate a large number of these cubes and arrange them into a variety of different shapes and forms.

Infinite could also use his Ruby's power to create Phantom Copies that cause his victims to falsely perceive the presence of other characters. These copies are described by Shadow as having "mass and form, but no heart or soul". These copies, so long as Infinite desired, could also carry the memories, powers and personalities of the originals. Accordingly, Infinite could create an unlimited number of them. He could also alter them to make them stronger than the originals, such as enhancing the size of a Metal Sonic Phantom Copy granting him the ability to use electrified versions of his energy cubes as well as to materialize objects to use as a shield.

Using his finalized Phantom Ruby copy's power, Infinite was also capable of manipulating nothingness itself, allowing him to create Null Space, which is a seemingly inescapable void in space-time where absolutely nothing exists. When opening a portal into Null Space, Infinite could reinforce such portals to expand and suck everything nearby into them.


## Astroscopic Lens abilities


After losing the Phantom Ruby, Infinite acquired an Astroscopic Lens from a mysterious benefactor and quickly mastered its abilities, using it to track down the remaining lenses in the set. With a single lens, he could disguise himself perfectly imitating the appearance, voice, and mannerisms of others as well as create illusions and evade detection. After securing all six lenses, Infinite arranged them on a floating platform designed to continuously channel their power into him. With the full set amplifying his abilities, he could fly, move at incredible speeds, deliver powerful physical strikes, and create multiple clones of himself. However, when Team Chaotix removed the lenses from the platform, causing them to shatter, Infinite lost the amplified power and returned to his normal state.


## Weapons


During his time as the leader of the Jackal Squad, Infinite possessed a red sword. This sword was evidently very sharp, being able to cut through robots with ease.


## Weaknesses


While Infinite's illusions are formidable, they can be neutralized by someone who has a Phantom Ruby prototype. Likewise, his finalized Phantom Ruby copy's illusions only work if the target is within the Ruby's field of effect. Also, his finalized Phantom Ruby copy requires a tremendous amount of energy to function. Should its energy supply be cut off, his finalized Phantom Ruby copy would be rendered inert. He also appeared to be vulnerable to the original Phantom Ruby's influence, though the extent of this is unknown since he managed to escape using an illusion to deceive his foes at the time.

Another weakness of Infinite's is his extreme sense of confidence and constant need to satisfy his sadism. When Infinite destroyed the Avatar's squad, he willingly let them run away unharmed as he enjoyed the sounds of their screams. Later, when he had Sonic at his mercy a second time, rather than finishing him there and then, he just left, seeing Sonic as something he need not bother himself with. Both these actions proved to be Infinite's downfall when Sonic and the Avatar later ended up beating him together. To a lesser extent, his insecurities over perceived weaknesses he might have also interfered with his ability to channel Phantom Copies, which is demonstrated by how his facsimile of E-123 Omega ultimately started to break down when declaring he never knew defeat by going on a repetitive rant of "I am not weak!" (Heavily implied to be Infinite's feelings over being beaten by Shadow bleeding into the facsimile).


## Relationships



## Shadow the Hedgehog


Shadow the Hedgehog is responsible for breaking Infinite's ego, leading him to use the Phantom Ruby. When a furious and arrogant Infinite tried to stop Shadow in Eggman's Facility, Shadow easily defeated him, making his disgust clear and demanding to never see his "pathetic face" again. Infuriated by his own pitiful reaction to this encounter, Infinite spiraled into self-loathing and subjected himself to Eggman's experiments in order to gain power and avoid ever feeling so weak again.

With the Phantom Ruby, Infinite's behavior reflected his encounter with Shadow. He loved inflicting the same fear that he once felt, and even began concealing his face with a mask, possibly reflecting Shadow's threat. Infinite was eager to show his new powers to Shadow and was completely unoffended when he didn't remember his old self. He belittled Shadow as an "insect" and believed himself to now be stronger than him. Although Shadow escaped his false version of Green Hill, Infinite was satisfied with his torment and changed focus to conquering the planet, indicating that he no longer deemed him a threat nor a priority. He later used a Phantom Copy of Shadow to instill fear into the Resistance during Eggman's reign.


## Sonic the Hedgehog


Infinite knew that defeating Sonic the Hedgehog was key to the Eggman Empire's world domination. In their first meeting, Infinite took Sonic seriously, quickly defeating and capturing him, which indeed led to the Eggman Empire's victory. This gave Infinite's ego yet another boost, as he frequently taunted his Resistance enemies that they were hopeless without Sonic.

When facing Sonic again in Mystic Jungle, Infinite was undeterred by his sudden return. He attempted to intimidate Sonic like he had done with countless others, but this failed, as the hedgehog wittily deflected all of his remarks. Similar to Erazor Djinn, Infinite insulted Sonic by calling him a "filthy sewer rat" during their first fight. However, he admitted that Sonic had improved and expressed surprise that he posed a challenge despite previous data indicating otherwise. Regardless, Infinite chose to abandon the fight, declaring Sonic not worth killing. Eggman later criticized this choice, though Infinite remained confident that he could easily defeat Sonic if need be.

In Sonic and Infinite's final encounter, the two continued to trade witty remarks; Infinite argued that Sonic's friends were useless, while Sonic asserted that Infinite's powers were hollow and paled in comparison to the bonds of friendship. Infinite was shocked when Sonic and the Avatar beat him after all.

They would meet again some time later, during the events of The Chaotix Casefiles. By this point, Infinite's hatred for Sonic increased drastically, after being defeated by him at the end of War to Take Back the Planet, and he questioned why he continued to challenge him and the power he believed he was entitled to. Then use the Astroscopic Lenses to create illusory clones of Sonic and his friends to destroy them, but after Espio and Charmy destroy the Astroscopic Lenses his newfound powers vanished completely. As he prepared to set off a bomb and escape, he swore to Sonic that they would meet again.


## Avatar


Infinite had a personal connection with the Avatar, as he was responsible for destroying their friends in the six months between Sonic's defeat and the Avatar joining the Resistance. Infinite had only spared them to satisfy his sadistic desire to watch them flee in terror. They later reunited at Metropolis, where Infinite was surprised to see them again and tried to intimidate them. When this failed due to the Avatar feeling encouraged by Sonic, Infinite was shocked that they put up a fight. This shows that Infinite and the Avatar are foils: when Infinite was faced with overwhelming fear as his allies were destroyed, he turned inwards to selfishly increase his own power, but when the Avatar was in a similar situation, he turned outwards to find inspiration in friendship with others such as Sonic. Infinite continued to underestimate the Avatar, even letting them live despite their immunity to his powers thanks to their secret Phantom Ruby prototype. Ultimately, Infinite was brought down by the teamwork of Sonic and the Avatar, proving his selfish and individualistic outlook wrong.


## Dr. Eggman


Infinite initially sought Dr. Eggman's technology to sell for bounties, but upon witnessing the power of the Phantom Ruby, accepted his offer to join him. He promised to help the doctor change the world, mostly for his own selfish amusement. However, his relationship with Eggman proved to be a rocky one, as Infinite grew exasperated with his overbearing orders. After his crushing defeat at the hands of Shadow, Infinite became a willing subject in Eggman's Phantom Ruby experiments, ready to do anything to achieve more power.

Following Eggman's domination of Earth, Infinite would continue to prove loyal to the doctor, serving as his right-hand man and enforcer, though he did not hesitate to voice his displeasure with Eggman's choices. He also openly acknowledged himself as the better of the two, giving him somewhat more of an equal footing with the doctor than most. After Eggman recalled the Phantom Ruby's power from Infinite due to his failures, the jackal grew resentful of this action. Though Eggman tried to order him around as he did before, he quickly betrayed the doctor by stealing the last Astroscopic Lens, then using its power to punch Eggman into the horizon.


## Jackal Squad


Although Infinite once led the Jackal Squad in battle, he saw them as little more than his underlings. He did not value their opinions, quickly disregarding their pleas to reject Dr. Eggman's deal and instead seeking his own satisfaction. When Shadow destroyed them in Mystic Jungle, Infinite seemed more offended that someone had beaten his subordinates than worried for their safety. After obtaining the Phantom Ruby, Infinite came to regard interpersonal relationships as foolish and claimed that you can't rely on anyone, showing that he no longer cared for the Jackal Squad at all.


## Team Chaotix


During the War to Take Back the Planet, Infinite dismissed Team Chaotix as more of Sonic's "annoying" and "useless" friends. As he began gathering the Astroscopic Lenses for himself, however, he started to loathe the detectives, Vector the Crocodile especially, for trying to interfere with his plans.

## Battle


Infinite appears as a boss that is fought on three separate occasions in Sonic Forces. The first battle is in Mystic Jungle and is against Sonic; the second battle is in Metropolis and is against the Avatar, and the third and final battle is in the Eggman Empire Fortress and is at first against Sonic, but then changes into a battle against both Sonic and the Avatar. During each of these battles, taking damage will result in the player getting thrown into a brief virtual reality with extra hazards. The first and the third battle have the player chase after Infinite, while the second one takes place in an arena.


## Reception


Infinite received a very negative reception from both fans and critics. The most criticized aspects were his personality and motivations (in particular the motivations revealed in "Episode Shadow"), the latter of which became a massive source of mockery towards the character. According to Martin Wood from Game Rant, many players found Infinite to be "one-dimensional and generic, with a weak revenge story and premise."
