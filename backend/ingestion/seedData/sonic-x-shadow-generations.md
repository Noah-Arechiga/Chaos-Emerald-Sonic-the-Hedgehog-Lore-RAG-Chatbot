Sonic X Shadow Generations (ソニック × シャドウ ジェネレーションズ, Sonikku Shadō Jenerēshonzu?) is a bundle video game that features a remaster of the console/PC version of Sonic Generations (2011) and a new game called Shadow Generations. Developed by Sonic Team and published by Sega, the game was released in October 2024 as part of the "Fearless: Year of Shadow" campaign that ran throughout the year. The remastered Sonic Generations features rewritten cutscenes by Ian Flynn and improved visuals, while Shadow Generations stars Shadow the Hedgehog as a playable character.

Sonic X Shadow Generations was first teased during Sony's PlayStation State of Play livestream on 31 January 2024. It was released for the PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X and Series S, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, and PC on 25 October 2024, with consumers who bought the Digital Deluxe Edition having obtained access to the game three days earlier. The game received positive reviews and was a commercial success, selling over 1 million copies on the first day of its release.

Aside from the Nintendo Switch version being backwards compatible on the Nintendo Switch 2, a separate Nintendo Switch 2 version was also released alongside the console's launch of 5 June 2025, with upgraded visuals at the same price of the other versions, being the first game in the Sonic the Hedgehog franchise for that system, as well as being one of the first Sega games for the system (releasing alongside 2 other Sega titles).

## Gameplay


Sonic X Shadow Generations features two games: a remaster of Sonic Generations, retaining the same gameplay style, and Shadow Generations, incorporating elements from the original Generations and Sonic Frontiers.


## Changes to Sonic Generations


The Sonic Generations remaster now uses a new control scheme similar to Sonic Frontiers, though there is an option for the player to use the old control scheme known as "Legacy Controls". For the PC version, it is also possible to customize the button mapping in player's favor.

## Cast


In the Italian dub, this is the only game where Matteo Brusamonti voices Vector.

Due to the credits after beating the game being entirely reused from the original game, the voice actors from the original game in all dubs are credited, despite several of them not reprising their roles in the remake. All of the voice actors who worked on the remaster are instead credited alongside those from Shadow Generations in its credits. Their voice clips are taken from the last two games of the Mario & Sonic series, and even Sonic at the Olympic Games (2020), with a few more characters adding new ones.


## Promotion


On 31 January 2024, Sonic X Shadow Generations was first announced during Sony's State of Play.

On 8 June 2024, a trailer on Summer Game Fest was shown which was instantly released on YouTube. With it, it was announced that there would be a pre-order bonus, as well as a Digital Deluxe version. The pre-order bonus will give the buyer a skin for Modern Sonic based on Sonic's appearance from the original Sonic Adventure game with all his Level Up Items. Physical pre-orders will also include a reversible cover and a physical replica of Gerald Robotnik's journal.

The Digital Deluxe Edition contains a set of Skill Points for Sonic Generations, a bonus skin for Shadow based on his "Terios" beta design, an extra BGM pack, a digital art book and soundtrack, and the Sonic the Hedgehog 3 Movie Pack (which contains an extra skin and level based on the upcoming third Sonic movie). Pre-orders of this version also include three-day early access on all consoles but Nintendo Switch, allowing it to be played on October 22nd as opposed to the 25th. The Terios skin was described in interviews as having a few differences in comparison to Shadow, making him feel like a completely different character.

On 1 July 2024, it was revealed that those who sign up for the website's newsletter will receive a free legacy skin for Classic Sonic based on Sonic Jam, his first 3D appearance. When the game launched, the skin was listed separately on each store's marketplace as a paid DLC.

On 19 July 2024, it was announced on their social media that, thanks to fan feedback, all pre-orders from the physical release will have the Japanese box art as a reversible cover.

On 8 August 2024, it was announced that a brand new serialized manga by CoroCoro Comic would be releasing in October to promote and adapt the game, dubbed The Jet Black Hedgehog: Shadow the Hedgehog. During the same day, it was announced that a demo of the game would be shown in Gamescom 2024, from 22-25 August, where Sonic will be in attendance posing for photos with fans, as well showing off the custom-built Shadow-inspired motorcycle.

The next day, the social media team announced that the original Sonic Generations would be delisted from Steam, Xbox, and Playstation stores, with existing owners still being able to download and play the game. However, in an effort to preserve the game per the fan's wishes, it will still be included in Sonic game bundles on Steam and Xbox, also noting that mods made for the Steam version will not be affected by the change.

On 19 August 2024, it was announced that players could pre-order a special "Collector's Edition" through Limited Run Games, which includes the game, an artbook, the official soundtrack, an individually numbered certificate of authentication, Chao figurines, keychains, a steelbook, a comparative Dreamcast jewel case, a Sonic and Shadow Dreamcast statue, and all other pre-order bonuses. It was available to pre-order from 19 August to 6 October 2024 and was shipped in December 2025.

On 20 August 2024, a trailer for the game showcasing Shadow's new abilities (named "Doom Powers") was released, also showcasing levels that will be featured in the Shadow portion of the game, like Chaos Island from Sonic Frontiers, and the Metal Overlord from Sonic Heroes.

On 11 September 2024, a new piece of key art for the game was released revealing the Black Moon.

A demo of the game was playable during the Thailand Game Show 2024, held between 18 and 20 October 2024.


## Reception


The bundle received high praise, both for the original game's remaster and Shadow's campaign.

Shacknews praised the game for its tight and polished action. Video Games Chronicle praised the game for its abundance of lore information as well replay value.

One major criticism of the bundle was that Sonic Generations did not fit in with Shadow Generations and felt outdated by comparison, creating a stark contrast between the two games. Gamespot opined that Shadow lacked innovation, and enjoyment may vary based on nostalgia. Multiple sources also criticized the Doom Powers for not going together with game's flow and pace naturally, while VGC anticipated the 3D design of White Space in Shadow would prove divisive.

The Nintendo Switch 2 version was largely criticized for not allowing owners of the original Switch version to upgrade to the new version and that the physical editions did not contain the full game but instead provided a download code through a Game-Key Card, requiring an internet connection to access the content.
