Sonic Frontiers (2022) introduces the Starfall Islands, a mysterious
open-zone archipelago where Sonic, separated from Tails and Amy in a storm,
must free a digitized Amy and Tails, and later Knuckles, from a virtual
prison called Cyberspace while investigating the islands' ancient past.

## The Ancients and the Koco

The islands are revealed to be the former home of a precursor civilization
called the Ancients, who are shown (through memory sequences accessed via
"Portal Gears" and Sonic's psychic connection to a helper being called Sage)
to have possessed advanced technology and to have discovered a method of
digitizing living beings, including small rock-like spirit creatures called
the Koco.

## The End and the Ancients' fate

The Ancients' downfall is tied to a cataclysmic being referred to as "The
End," an entity of near-total destructive power that the Ancients
inadvertently drew toward their world through their own technological
advancement and expansion. In the game's backstory, the Ancients used the
Chaos Emeralds — depicted here as tied to their civilization's technology —
in a desperate attempt to seal or repel The End, an effort that ultimately
failed to save their civilization even as fragments of their knowledge and
consciousness persisted within the islands' systems.

## Sage and Dr. Eggman

Sage, an AI who guides and gradually befriends Sonic through the game, is
revealed to have been created by Dr. Eggman, who was pulled into the
Starfall Islands' systems himself and, following his own investigation of
the Ancients' history, apparently begins to develop a more complex,
protective relationship toward Sage — a notable softening of his typical
antagonist role, expanded on in the game's "Final Horizon" update and
subsequent side content.

## Chaos Emeralds' role in Frontiers

Sonic collects Chaos Emeralds scattered across the islands throughout the
game (each guarded by a powerful Guardian construct/boss) to eventually
achieve Super Sonic and confront The End directly, tying the islands' ancient
catastrophe to the same Emerald mythology established in earlier titles like
Sonic Adventure, while also expanding what the Emeralds' origin and
significance might be beyond Angel Island specifically.
