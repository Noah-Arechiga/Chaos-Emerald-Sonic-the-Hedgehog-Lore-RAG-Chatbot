The Chao (チャオ, Chao?) are creatures that appear in the Sonic the Hedgehog series. They are a mystical species of fairy-like creatures that inhabit various areas on Earth.


## Description



## Anatomy


While there is not a defined appearance for Chao, most of them share various common features. Described as cute, they are small creatures, about the size of Wisps, with gelatinous and pudding-like bodies each. In general, Chao have round and droplet-like heads with oval-shaped eyes but no noses, round torsos smaller than their heads, smooth feet with no legs, and arms with no visible hands. Neither their arms or feet have digits. They also have a short tail, small wings and a floating sphere above their heads. It has been noted that, when they grow up, adult Chao do not show many differences compared to younger Chao.

While Chao are hatched from eggs, they appear to be immediately mature upon the egg's creation, though when they want to emerge is random and can be determined by a caretaker.


## Characteristics and culture


Like humans and other anthropomorphic animals, Chao are highly social and sapient creatures, have very flexible facial expressions, and possess self-awareness. Additional feelings can be expressed by the floating dot above their head; a heart indicates they enjoy the activity, a question mark indicates it is thinking, an exclamation point means the Chao is interested in something, and a swirl is generally a sign of being hurt or too tired.

They also behave closely like human infants, and possess delightful, pleasant, and lively mannerisms. Chao enjoy being petted. If a Chao likes a caretaker, they will often greet them and may even run to them for attention. They likewise react positively when picked up, smiling, clapping and rocking around. They also enjoy fruit, which is their favorite food,, though they do have their preferences.

Conversely, Chao do not like to be attacked, thrown, or punished. If mistreated by being attacked or thrown, Chao will shiver in fear. When food is taken from them while they are eating, or in more severe cases of mistreatment, Chao will begin to cry, either with short sniffles or outright crying. They do not like being picked up by those who frequently mistreat them and trying to do so will result in the Chao squirming to escape their grasp. In severe cases, Chao who have an intense fear of a caretaker may try to run away from them, screaming in terror and flailing around wildly until they are far enough from them.

Chao live in certain areas of Earth, known as the Chao Gardens. These gardens are paradise for Chao, and have a nice, clean and shallow pool for them and palm trees with the fruit they eat. Nevertheless, various Chao have demonstrated that they can live in other environments, as long as these have pools of fresh water. The best-known case of this is Cheese, who lives with Cream the Rabbit and her family. Conversely, Chao dislike dried out places like Sandopolis, and are also scared of Dr. Eggman's bases, including the Final Fortress. While Chao prefer to live in groups, they do not like overcrowded gardens. The Chao are also notorious on the planet they have a dedicated amusement park towards them called Chao Park.

When it comes to biology, all Chao are genderless. They also appear to be oviparous creatures, since they are born from special eggs known as Chao Eggs, which Chao can create through breeding. Only adults are capable of breeding, and generally once in their lifetime. When a Chao is ready to mate, it will sit down, their emotion dot turns into a heart and a bed of flowers will surround it. If another Chao who is also ready to mate is nearby, they will go over to that Chao and perform what appears to be a small nuzzle before an egg appears from where they were standing as they cheer in happiness. Chao that eat Heart Fruit will have an instinct to mate even if they weren't interested or already did so before. The resulting offspring will share traits from its parents.

Chao are born as Normal Chao, and from there can grow into either Hero Chao, Dark Chao, or Neutral Chao, depending on how they are raised. Those aligned towards Hero Chao have whiter skin, two tuffs, a normal tail, and blue emotion dot. Those aligned to Dark Chao have darker skin, tan emotion dot, a spiky-looking tail, and have a zig-zagged shaped point at the top of their head. Finally, those aligned to Neutral Chao simply look the same as they were upon hatching. Most Chao born from colored eggs do not have their skin affected by influence. Further changes to the Chao's appearance are dependent on their alignment towards specific skill(s), assuming the caretaker had influenced its skill growth.

Chao age quite fast compared to other species. When one "Chao year" has passed (roughly one to three hours), the Chao will create a blue-green cocoon and evolve into a more mature form. They do not change much in body size, though a Chao that is aligned towards a Hero or Dark Chao will have their emotion dot changed to a halo and spiky dot respectively. Their skin, emotion dot and wings are no longer affected by influence and remain the same until the end of their lifetime. Adult Chao act similarly as their younger counterparts, but are able to show signs of dissatisfaction when mistreated by throwing tantrums or hissing at the caretaker. Mistreated Dark Chao take this a step further, potentially attacking them with punches, kicks and chops.

As with all living beings, Chao will eventually die at the end of their life. When this occurs, the Chao's emotion dot will turn into a swirl, and it will appear as if it is upset or weary; it may also sit down. A cocoon will soon form, of which there are two potential outcomes. If the Chao was content in its life, it will enter a process known as reincarnation, entering a pink-yellow cocoon. Once the cocoon fades, an egg will appear from the cocoon. The Chao inside the egg will retain its original appearance and some knowledge of its past live(s). Essentially, Chao that is well cared for becomes pseudo-immortal. If the Chao was not content in its life, it will enter a gray cocoon; no egg will appear from it, indicating that the Chao has died for good.

When a Chao spots the cocoon of a Chao that is evolving, the Chao will walk over and cheer on the evolving Chao. They will also do the same to a Chao in the process of reincarnation. On the other hand, if the adult Chao is not reincarnating, nearby Chao who spot the cocoon will initially be happy, only to cry and mourn the Chao's passing. This implies that the Chao are able to understand the concepts of rebirth and death.

Besides the normal evolutions, a special Chao called a "Chaos Chao" can be created. This can be done once the selected Chao has transformed at least two times. During that third or higher cycle of its life, if it is happy enough, given at least one of each animal in the game, and all its stats are around equal, it will become a Chaos Chao. Besides looking different than a normal Chao, the Chaos Chao has an endless lifespan. There are three types of Chaos Chao: "Light Chaos", "Hero Chaos" and "Dark Chaos". To get a Hero Chaos or a Dark Chaos, the player should give them one animal from either the Hero or Dark characters respectively, and that all of their stats are around equal. The Chaos Chao is notably unable to reproduce.


## Powers and abilities


Chao are capable of flight. As shown during various incidents, some Chao are able to deal great damage to their targets. During Dodon Pa's Grand Prix, Chao have also demonstrated to be able to drive racing vehicles like the Chao Pod, and in other instances, they could drive mechs like the Chao Walker as well. However, they are said to be weak life forms; as noted by Tikal, Chao are too vulnerable without the protection of a guardian.

By giving Chao other lifeforms such as Animals, they will absorb and learn some of the given beings' skills and traits. Besides a physical change to the Chao, this will also cause a vast change in behavior, abilities, and personality. Similarly, Chaos Drives can be used to raise Chao's abilities, and lean their alignment accordingly. On a similar note, as demonstrated by Chaos, Chao can harness the power of at least the Master Emerald to gain deity-like abilities, though the effects of these on another Chao are unknown.

Thanks to their wings, the Chao are capable of flight, although not all of them possess the skill to use them, and they do not generally fly for a long time.


## Background


The Chao are former Ancients. After a majority of the race was wiped out due to various threats, the few that physically survived eventually evolved into the present-day Chao.

At least one Chao colony has existed on Earth millennia before the Chaos incident. On what is now Angel Island, a small community of Chao was known to live around the Altar of Emerald. Eventually, however, contact with the Master Emerald caused one of those Chao to turn into Chaos, a liquified being with deity-like abilities akin in appearance with the Ancients, the Chao's genetic ancestors. With its new powers, Chaos protected its friends from foreign invaders and kept the water around the altar pure. Thanks to its protection, the local Chao lived singing in peace.

Around 3,000 years ago, the Chao living in the Altar of Emerald met and befriended Tikal, a young echidna from the Knuckles Clan. Shortly after her arrival, Chaos appeared before her. Though the female echidna was surprised by this, Chaos assured her it trusted her. The two soon became friends, with Tikal having become so close to the Chao that she would allegedly go to the Altar of Emerald to play with her Chao friends and Chaos.

Unfortunately, the Chao from the Altar of Emerald were threatened when Pachacamac, the chief of the Knuckles Clan and Tikal's power-hungry father, sought to obtain the seven Chaos Emeralds on the altar for his people. After learning of this, Tikal immediately went to the Altar of Emerald and warned Chaos about the future invasion, begging it to take the local Chao away from there. However, it told her it could not do that, leading to the female echidna promising to try to change her father's mind. Sadly, however, Pachacamac ultimately went along with his plans. On a night, he launched a full-scale assault on the altar and had his troops scramble over Tikal and the Chao. Seeing this horrible treatment towards its friends, Chaos gave in into its anger and used the power of the seven Chaos Emeralds to turn into Perfect Chaos, slaughtering Pachacamac and his men. Now fueled by thoughts of destruction, however, Chaos did not stop its rampage right there, and instead sought to destroy the world. Fortunately, Tikal used an ancient prayer to seal herself and Chaos in the Master Emerald, which would then be guarded by the few remaining people of the Knuckles Clan for the next millennia.

After this incident, the Chao that lived in the Altar of Emerald were endangered, and forced to leave their original home and look for a new place to live elsewhere. A few were able to barely survive, and some had new, artificial homes built by humans.

In addition, a Chao Race trainer named Clutch the Opossum would acquire a number of Chao. However, those that failed to win Chao Races for him would be mistreated by being locked up in a cage.


## History



## Sonic Adventure


In Sonic Adventure, Chao are a prominent feature. One of those Chao, Chaos, plays a major role in the games as their main villain until the end, where it gets neutralized by Super Sonic and reunites with its fellow Chao. Ordinary Chao, alongside Chaos, are also found in Tikal's visions of the Knuckles Clan's attack on the Altar of Emerald 3,000 years in the past.

In gameplay, Chao can be raised as virtual pets in the Chao Garden. The games have three of these Gardens in each Adventure Field: one is found in Station Square, the other in the Mystic Ruins, and there is an island-like Chao Garden connected to the Egg Carrier. However, all of these work basically the same. In order to raise a Chao, the player must first get one from Chao Eggs, which are obtained in many ways. Each Chao Garden comes with two Chao Eggs when it is visited for the first time.

While only normal Chao are present in the base game, the enhanced port allows players to raise Hero and Dark Chao. Since characters do not have an alignment, they must be fed their respective fruit in order to acquire them.

Eggs can be hatched by letting them do so naturally, cradling it for a short time and then dropping it, or throwing it against a wall to immediately hatch the Chao. There is no notable difference in the Chao with these methods.

Chao can have multiple personality traits and appearances, which cannot be altered without the use of mods as these are completely randomized until they hatch from an egg.

When a Chao is newly born, all of its stats will be at level 0 (level 01 if hatched in a Tiny Chao Garden), but can be leveled up to level 99. The gained stats also depend on the Chao's "rank" on them. A Chao with an E rank in a stat will have a lower growth compared to one with a B rank. Breeding Chao can improve the overall ranking of the offspring. The first four of these stats can be raised by giving the Chao animals. When an animal is given to a Chao, gauges representing certain stats will either be filled up or emptied, though it will never drop a level if the gauge is empty. The Chao may also exhibit some traits and features of the given animal. Stamina, on the other hand, is leveled up when a Chao is given food to eat. When those gauges are filled, their respective stats will fill up by one. Additionally, a second gauge will also fill up and improve the Chao's performance in its respective stat.

The player can take their Chao to compete in a Chao Race. This is where stats come in; the higher levels the Chao has, the more likely are they to win in races. The player's Chao will race against seven other Chao to get to the finish line first. Taking Chao to Chao Races is also important for the player to collect five Emblems.

When a Chao reaches the end of its life, the Chao will envelop themselves inside of a cocoon. If the Chao's happiness level is below a specific threshold, it will die at the end of its life. Chao will only enter the cocoon when on ground and not interacted with. In the Dreamcast version of the game, Chao can be "killed" if their HP counter reaches 0, caused by either attacking it repeatedly or mating constantly. Conversely, if they have received enough care from the player, they will reincarnate in the form of a Chao Egg. When the reincarnated Chao is hatched from the egg, it will be reborn as a baby with their stats' levels reset to 1, but it will retain 10% of their stat points it had in its previous life, allowing the player to further raise the stats to higher values.

In the Dreamcast versions of Sonic Adventure, the Chao will always envelop itself in a gray cocoon regardless if it would die or reincarnate. In every other release (Sonic Adventure DX: Director's Cut, Sonic Adventure 2: Battle and their remasters), Chao will envelop themselves in a gray or a pink cocoon if they would die or reincarnate respectively.

The Chao Gardens in the Sonic Adventure series have the ability to send Chao to an attached VMU (Visual Memory Unit) using the Transporter Machine (renamed to the Chao Transporter in Sonic Adventure 2). This results in the ability to play with the Chao away from the Dreamcast in a mini-games called Chao Adventure and Chao Adventure 2 respectively. The Chao walks on a path and is given a task. If done correctly, the player is rewarded with a fruit or seed which can be taken off the VMU and back onto Sonic Adventure 2 for planting. Seeds can be planted by a Chao with a watering can and a shovel, the latter which can be won in races in Sonic Adventure 2. In the Chao Adventure games, the player can also attach two VMUs to breed Chao, check the stats of a Chao, and name them. In Chao Adventure, the Chao does not age like it does in gardens.

In a manner similar to that of animals, giving Chao Chaos Drives will also increase their bond and happiness.

In a similar vein, the Nintendo GameCube version of Sonic Adventure 2: Battle can communicate with the Game Boy Advance versions of Sonic Advance and Sonic Advance 2 to allow a two-way transfer of Chao gameplay data.

The Chao Kindergarten provides various facilities for Chao, though the only one of interest is the classroom, where Chao can take lessons for drawing, singing, dancing and playing instruments.

Reincarnation is the same as in the previous game, though reincarnating Chao will keep prizes and awards from Chao Race, along with lessons, their stat grade and any hats they had. As with Sonic Adventure, Chao will envelop themselves in a gray cocoon regardless if they would die or reincarnate in the Dreamcast version.

Chao can be given accessories to wear, which requires them to have been given a Skeleton Dog, or they will throw it to the side. The Chao will keep the accessory on them unless attacked, jumped on, trips, or at the end of their life.

Rarely, a Chao can get sick, potentially getting one of six different illnesses. The illness is more of a visual indicator and does not appear to affect the Chao's values, though it tends to override most of their normal animations. If this occurs, the player can bring the Chao to the Chao Doctor to immediately cure them of the illness. Otherwise, they will need to wait a very long time for the illness to subside. The Chao cannot be given the same illness if they already have it until they return to normal.

Chao Races are also present, and completing them will give the Chao various items which they can use, such as growing trees or improving their leisure time.

Chao now have an alignment, which scales on various factors, such as placing them in the Hero or Dark Garden, interactions with "Hero" characters (Sonic, Knuckles or Tails) or "Dark" characters (Shadow, Rogue and Eggman), which determines their adult form when they evolve. Hero Chao are raised by taking care of them with Hero characters or abusing them with Dark, vice versa for Dark Chao, and Neutral Chao require equal interactions with both sides. Various visual indicators on the Chao (such as the shape of their heads, color of their wings or skin) indicate its alignment. Apart from these three alignments, players can also raise Chaos Chao through more stringent requirements.

Character-like Chao are also available; by default, players can create Sonic Chao, Shadow Chao, and NiGHTS Chao by raising a Neutral Run/Dark Run/Neutral Fly Chao respectively. Tails, Knuckles and Amy-themed Chaos are much harder to get through legitimate means, as these were released as part of collaboration events.

## Sonic Rivals 2


The Chao play an important role in Sonic Rivals 2. At the beginning of the story, the Chao appeared to have gone missing and Sonic and Tails suspect that Eggman Nega (masquerading as Eggman himself) is behind the disappearance. In the meantime, Silver and Espio try to hide every Chao he can find from Eggman Nega's sight because of the doctor's intentions with the small creatures: he plans to feed them to a powerful beast named the 'Ifrit' which grants it invincibility and to be unleashed into Sonic's world, conquering the world with it. Later, Eggman Nega reveals to Espio and Silver that he has acquired more than enough Chao kept in a secret room of a mysterious mansion. Despite of this, Sonic and Tails discovered the secret room before Eggman Nega does and escorted the Chao away to safety.

If the player acquires a Chao Detector (which each is unlocked by receiving a card) for a Zone, it can be used to find them easily in each zone.

## Sonic Speed Simulator


In Sonic Speed Simulator, Chao appear as both non-playable characters and notable gameplay elements. In the latter case, they serve to increase the amount of Experience Points and Rings that the player collects over the course of the game.

In gameplay, the player can buy Chao from vending machines, earn them from gifts, buy them directly from limited time shops in Green Hill, receive them as rewards when completing specific objectives during an event, receive them from Spin Wheels, or redeem them from codes. There are as well Chao that are only available for a limited time. These Chao tend to appear during different events where they can be earned by completing event objectives or buying them from stands in Green Hill.

Once obtained, Chao can be equipped to the playable character, whom the Chao will appear behind of in an semi-circle formations. The game offers several different Chao to choose from. Each Chao has a unique color and a name that is more or less associated with its look. In addition, each Chao has different attributes. These include the classification of their rarity, the most common of which are "Common", "Uncommon", "Rare", "Epic", and "Legendary", while the rest are exclusive to limited-edition Chao, which include "Chaos", "Exotic" and "Special". Other unique traits each Chao has are the benefits they grant when equipped.

The player can equip the playable character with three of any Chao to accompany them during gameplay, although that limit can be increased to a maximum of five with the +2 Pet Slots gamepass. Also, while equipped to the playable character, the Chao will gain Experience Points to level up with whenever the player collects Chaos Orbs, touches Sky Rings, or buys Experience Points from vending machines. Leveling up a Chao will improve the benefits they grant. The maximum level for all Chao is 99. The Experience Points gained for Chao is also not affected by Rebirth multipliers or the effects of other Chao or Trails.

While the player can carry up to twenty Chao by default, they can expand their number of holding shots for Chao by purchasing additional slots with a large amount of Robux. Additionally, the player can fuse a Chao by collecting five level 25 Chao of the same type and pressing the "Fuse" button at a Fusion Machine, which will fuse these five Chao into one. Evolving a Chao will combine the base stats of all the Chao involved. Before the Reborn update, the player could simply fuse their Chao from the Chao inventory by selecting 5 of the same type and pressing the "Evolve" button, and the player could also "enchant" a Chao in Enchantment Shrines, which would grant it one of six (originally seven) buffs. There, the player could, for the price of one million Rings, grant a Chao one of the following buffs: speed enhancement, power enhancement, XP heightening, Ring heightening, Magnet enhancement, and increased lucky draws.

## Animation


In the Sonic X anime series and its comic series published by Archie Comics, Chao are a species native to both Sonic's world and Earth. In this media, only Normal Chao appear (the comic introduced Dark Chao as well) and there is no mention of their lifecycles or their trait-borrowing skills.

In the old days, the Knuckles Clan would injure some Chao during an attack, incurring the wrath of their guardian, Chaos. Generations later, several Chao would appear to make Chaos happy following its defeat at the hands of Super Sonic.

The Chao In Space animated short is based on the fictional, in-universe Chao in Space films. It features a Normal Chao dreaming about fighting a space battle, and contains many Chao-related references and Easter eggs.


## Books and comics


In the Sonic the Hedgehog comic series and its spin-offs published by Archie Comics, the Chao are a species native to Mobius and the offspring of Chaos. In this media, their lifecycles and ability to borrow the traits of those exposed to them are fully adapted into the comic's canon. After the cast encounter the Chao during the incident with Chaos, they slowly moved into New Mobotropolis.

After the Super Genesis Wave re-wrote history, the Chao have become virtually identical to their game counterparts.


## Films


In Sonic the Hedgehog 3, Chao appear as mascots of the Chao Garden café at Tokyo. Chao mascots can be seen performing onstage as well as simply roaming the café.


## Reception and impact


Chao and the Chao-raising system have received mixed to positive reviews. In a review of Sonic Adventure, GameSpot's Peter Bartholow stated that with "the Chao-breeding simulation and the minigames, Sonic offers much more beyond the completion of its story." IGN stated that Sonic Adventure "more than has the extras department covered." This enthusiasm was extended to IGN's reviews of Sonic Adventure 2, Sonic Advance, and Sonic Adventure DX: Director's Cut. GameSpot's Shane Satterfield was critical of the Chao raising feature in Sonic Adventure 2: Battle, stating that "there's little in the way of interactivity" and that "the [C]hao training aspect using the Game Boy Advance is little more than a novelty." However, GameSpy's Shane Bettenhausen praised the feature in the game, comparing Chao to Tamagotchi digital pets. GameSpot's Frank Provo noted the appeal to "those people who might only enjoy pinball in passing" that Chao brought to Sonic Pinball Party. 1UP.com's Chris Baker called the Chao feature in Sega Superstars "ultimately worthless... ...but some might find it amusing." In a review of Sonic Chronicles: The Dark Brotherhood, GameSpot's Shiva Stella praised the level of strategy that the game's Chao system added. Destructoid's Jim Sterling voiced a similar opinion, although saying that Chao had been "rubbish" in previous games. In an official Sonic character poll by Sonic Team, the Chao were voted the sixth most popular characters in the series.

A Chao keychain was released as part of a Sonic X character keychain series. Between 27 July and 8 September 2002, Sega hosted events at Tokyo-based department stores to promote upcoming Sega video games. Attendees could download a "Chao Mini Garden" and exclusive Chao to their copies of Sonic Advance. Chao clothing clips, along with accessories based on other Sega characters, were available.

Chao's notability extends beyond the Sonic series and related media; Mary Jane Irwin of IGN used "Chao program" to describe Pokémon Channel in her review of the game.
