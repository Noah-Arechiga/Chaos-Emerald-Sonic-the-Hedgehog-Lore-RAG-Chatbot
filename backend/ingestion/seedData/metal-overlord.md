Metal Overlord (メタルオーバーロード , Metaru Ōbārōdo?) is a powerful transformation that appears in the Sonic the Hedgehog series. It is the completed transformation of Metal Madness, which Metal Sonic attained in order to annihilate his arch-rival, Sonic the Hedgehog, and conquer the world. However, the metallic tyrant was defeated by Team Sonic and stripped of his power. It makes its first appearance in Sonic Heroes.


## Concept and creation


In early concept artwork, Metal Overlord is referred to as Metal Sonic Final. However, in an early recording script for Sonic Heroes, Metal Overlord is instead referred to as Super Metal Sonic, a name which the script also refers to Metal Madness by. As Metal Madness's transformation into Metal Overlord is also absent from the early script, this implies that Metal Overlord and Metal Madness were originally written as the same transformation of Neo Metal Sonic.


## Appearance


Metal Overlord's physical appearance is nearly identical to that of Metal Madness, resembling a large, blue and heavily-armed mechanical dragon-like abomination, with a pair of eyes lined up vertically on his head. He has several long spikes on his upper body and many smaller ones running down his back and his long and curved tail. He has a flamethrower as his right hand (though he is not seen using it) and his left hand has a quartet of fingers with claws that can be launched, but he is legless. The only physical difference between Metal Overlord and Metal Madness is that he has grown a pair of mechanical wings on his lower body and detaches himself from the fleet, leaving several cables hanging from the bottom of his body.


## History



## Sonic Heroes


When deciding to take the matter of defeating his arch-rival, Sonic, and conquering the world into his own hands, Metal Sonic had the idea of becoming Metal Overlord, a form with the power necessary for both tasks. To achieve this form, Metal Sonic needed data from sufficiently strong people, and as such lured Team Sonic to the Egg Fleet to distract Sonic for good while he would copy the team's data along the way. Additionally, he needed Chaos' data and kidnapped Froggy and Chocola. When Team Dark, Team Rose and Team Chaotix joined the adventure, Metal Sonic copied their data as well.

After getting the data he needed, Neo Metal Sonic transformed into the early stage of Metal Overlord, Metal Madness, by using debris from the Final Fortress. Despite interference from Team Rose, Team Dark and Team Chaotix, Metal Madness completed his transformation and finally became Metal Overlord, who then flew into the skies, intending to make his ambitions reality, but was pursued by a super transformed Team Sonic. Confident, however, that even Super Sonic was no match for him, Metal Overlord fought the team without hesitation. During the battle, Metal Overlord ranted about his intention to rule over a robotic kingdom and his belief that the real Sonic was his copy. Eventually though, Metal Overlord was ultimately defeated and he fell down from the sky in agony, unable to understand how he could lose, before landing on the Final Fortress and reverting back to Metal Sonic.


## Shadow Generations


Metal Overlord reappears as a boss in Shadow Generations, where an incarnation of Metal Overlord from somewhere in spacetime was fought by Shadow the Hedgehog in the ruins of the flooded Final Fortress. Notably, he has a much more menacing voice in this game compared to his original battle in Sonic Heroes.

## Powers and abilities


Being one of, if not the strongest of Metal Sonic's transformations, Metal Overlord is one of the most powerful foes Sonic and his friends have ever faced. In this form, Metal Overlord is strong enough to fight on par with Super Sonic, Super Knuckles, and Super Tails teaming up, and even then, the trio only had a slim chance of defeating him, if not even less.

Due to him having grown wings, Metal Overlord is capable of flight and can fly at incredible speeds likely due to copying the data of Tails and other Flight Type characters, but is still slower than Super Sonic. He is also capable of enormous feats of strength due to copying the data of Knuckles and other Power Type characters, as he can easily pick up ships from the Egg Fleet with just one hand and throw them. Additionally, Metal Overlord has proven himself to be highly physically durable in this form, nearing that of invincibility. Normal attacks from even Super State characters cannot hurt him, and he does not appear to have any weak points in this form. The only known attack capable enough to inflict damage on him is Team Sonic's Team Blast, Super Sonic Power, though in the iteration of this fight in Shadow Generations, he can be harmed through other means, such as using Chaos Snap to strike him at high speed.

Metal Overlord can fire rapid barrages of crystal pillars and launch the spikes on his back as homing missiles, much like in his previous form. This time around, however, he can fire them at a much faster rate. He can also fire his left claws as missiles that can suddenly trap his target in a crystal cage which is strong enough even a Super State user cannot escape it.

Metal Overlord can also perform Chaos Control, due to him copying Shadow's and Sonic's data, which allows him to freeze time temporarily (20 seconds in Sonic Heroes). In Shadow Generations, it also allowed him to resist Chaos Control used by other users, being unaffected when Shadow used it to freeze time, likely due to two abilities cancelling each other out.


## Battle


Metal Overlord is the second phase of the boss of the Last story of Sonic Heroes, and the actual final boss of the game. He can be fought directly after defeating Metal Madness, and is fought with Team Sonic under Super Transformation.


## Boss guide


Metal Overlord attacks by shooting out crystal pillars, which Super Sonic can destroy with Homing Attack, Rocket Accel, Tornado Jump, or Light Attack (the Light Attack will consume five Rings when used, and as such, is not recommended), shooting out missiles that can trap the player's teammates with crystal cages (which Tails can Thunder Shoot for Team Blast charge), and by throwing large ships at the player, which can be destroyed by Knuckles (done by using his Volcanic Dunk to create fireballs and directing them at the craft which also fills the Team Blast Gauge faster), though the player can simply maneuver around the craft instead.

The player should repeatedly counter his attacks to fill up and use the Team Blast, Super Sonic Power, which is the only way to damage Metal Overlord as regular attacks are useless in damaging him. Upon using Super Sonic Power, Team Sonic will unleash a series of mighty Homing Attacks all across Metal Overlord's body. After five Team Blasts, he will be defeated. Should the player stall too long, Metal Overlord will use Chaos Control to freeze time for twenty seconds and gain some time to launch a counterattack. Rings are still consumed while time is frozen, but the effect can be countered using Team Blast.

The player will lose a life if their Ring count reaches zero or if Metal Overlord successfully traps all three characters, including Super Sonic, under the crystal cages.

## Books and comics


In the Sonic the Hedgehog comic series and its spin-offs published by Archie Comics, Metal Overlord is mentioned several times, although he does not make an appearance. He first surfaced in the reality created by the second Genesis Wave where his history was identical to his game counterpart. After the universe was rebooted by the Super Genesis Wave, Metal Overlord became a part of the main timeline, although his history remained the same as his game counterpart.

In the Sonic the Hedgehog comic series and its spin-offs published by IDW Publishing, Metal Overlord is only mentioned by Duo the Cat in Sonic the Hedgehog #63 when he mistake for Neo Metal Sonic's secondary final form, although he does not make an appearance.
