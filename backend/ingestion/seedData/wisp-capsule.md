The Wisp Capsule also known as Deployment Pods is an object that appears in the Sonic the Hedgehog series. It is a type of floating capsule created by Dr. Eggman that is used for containing Wisps, like a prison.


## Description


Wisp Capsules are differently-sized capsules for holding Wisps, which always float in one place. Regular Wisp Capsules have a red-colored chassis at the bottom with a transparent top cover. On top of the covers are a red disk with a transparent spike.

Each Wisp Capsule contains only one specific type of Wisp within it. Usually, Wisp Capsules only hold one individual Wisp each, although there are exceptions.

To open a Wisp Capsule in gameplay, the player simply has to touch it. The player will then automatically obtain its Wisp. Also, after opening a Wisp Capsule, another one with the same type of Wisp in it will soon after appear in the same spot although this may not occur with some Wisp Capsules in certain levels.


## Game appearances



## Sonic Colors


In the Wii version and Nintendo DS version of Sonic Colors, and the remaster Sonic Colors: Ultimate, Wisp Capsules can be found in every Act. For most types of Wisps, Wisp Capsules only hold one Wisp each. In the case of White Wisps, however, the Wisp Capsules can hold about four or five of them.

In gameplay, Wisp Capsules for Wisps that the player has yet to encounter will be empty, transparent and cannot be interacted with. Also, these Wisp Capsules have a certain color shade that signifies which Wisp it is supposed to contain. In the Wii version and Sonic Colors: Ultimate, Sonic cannot target Wisp Capsules with the Homing Attack, although he can in the DS version.

While many Acts in the game end with a Goal Ring, there are some that have a giant Wisp Capsule, called the "Prison Capsule", instead. These capsules are placed on the ground and contain numerous White Wisps in two long tubes. The Prison Capsules act as Goals that, once opened, means the player has cleared the Act, much like Capsules.

In some of the Acts in Game Land, there exists a special type of Wisp Capsule that has a color-changing question mark inside it instead of Wisps. On most occasions, this one cannot be used manually. Instead, it deploys a balloon shaped like Dr. Eggman's face, surrounded by hearts, that will track the player for a limited time and inflict damage when touched. If the player uses one of the Color Powers against it, it is possible to destroy this balloon with one hit. On a few occasions, this type of Wisp Capsule can give a random type of Wisp instead.


## Sonic Generations


In the console/PC version and Nintendo 3DS version of Sonic Generations, the Wisp Capsules make a return. In these games, the Wisp Capsules are more detailed than in Sonic Colors, with a yellow eye having been added on the middle, similar to ones on the Prison Capsules in Sonic Colors.

In the console/PC versions, Wisp Capsules only appear in Planet Wisp, and in the Nintendo 3DS version, they only appear in Tropical Resort and can be targeted with a Homing Attack. In both versions, the Wisp Capsules only contain one type of Wisp for each Act.


## Sonic Forces


In Sonic Forces, the Wisp Capsules look much like their Sonic Colors counterparts, although they do not possess a yellow eye on their middle. Like in Sonic Colors, most Wisp Capsules hold only one Wisp, except for those holding White Wisps, which can contain more. They also cannot be targeted with the Homing Attack or Wire Attack.

In gameplay, Wisp Capsules can be found in the Sonic Stages, the Avatar Stages, the Tag Stages and some of the secret stages and extra stages. In the Stages where Modern Sonic/Shadow is playable, the Wisp Capsules will only contain White Wisps. In the stages where the Avatar is playable on the other hand, the Wisp Capsules can contain any type of Wisp available in the game, except for the White Wisps. Finally, the Tag Stages' Wisp Capsules can contain any kind of Wisp.

Much like in Sonic Colors, any Wisp Capsule in the Avatar's stages that contain Wisps that do not match any of the Wispons the player has currently obtained will be rendered intangible and transparent until the player obtains their Wisp's associated Wispon. The color of the transparent Wisp Capsules indicate which Wisp is supposed to be in it.


## Other game appearances



## Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games


In Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games, the Wisp Capsules has the same design they had in the console/PC version and Nintendo 3DS version of Sonic Generations, complete with a yellow eye on the front.

In gameplay, the Wisp Capsules appear in the Sonic's Figure Skating Spectacular event. During the routine for this event, the playable characters will come across Wisp Capsules with a different Wisp inside each of them that they will open, thus releasing the Wisp inside them.


## In other media



## Books and comics


In the Sonic the Hedgehog comic series and its spin-offs published by IDW Publishing they are known as Deployment Pods, during the Eggman War, the Deployment Pods were used by the Resistance to send Wisps into enemy areas where they would wait for Resistance soldiers to arrive and free them from the pods. Despite the accommodations, the Wisps did not mind the pods. Shortly before the war ended, the mercenaries Rough and Tumble took over Barricade Town and used Deployment Pods to imprison the Wisps they had hoarded from all over town.

After Sonic and Knuckles came to Barricade Town, the two discover the Wisps trapped Deployment Pods. Thanks to Sonic's speech however, the Wisp managed to break free of the Deployment Pods and turn against Rough and Tumble. Afterward, the townspeople helped free the remaining Wisps from their Deployment Pods.
