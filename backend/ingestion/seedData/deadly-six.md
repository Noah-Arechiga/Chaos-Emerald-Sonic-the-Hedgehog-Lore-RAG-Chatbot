The Deadly Six (六鬼衆, Rokkishū?, lit. "Six Demons") are a group that appears in the Sonic the Hedgehog series. They are six villainous Zeti who originally formed many years ago, Dr. Eggman discovered the Deadly Six and tried to harness their species' ability to manipulate magnetic fields in an attempt to defeat Sonic the Hedgehog and conquer the world. However, due to Sonic's recklessness, the Deadly Six broke free and threatened to destroy the world on their own, forcing Sonic and Eggman to team up to stop them. In the end, the Deadly Six were defeated by the former, though not for good.


## Concept and creation


According to Takashi Iizuka, the Deadly Six were meant to bring "a sense of danger and humor" to the story of Sonic Lost World. According to another interview with Iizuka at the Summer of Sonic 2013, the Deadly Six are based off of oni, Japanese demons, and were each designed around a particular keyword to make their characteristics obvious at a glance.


## History



## Past


The Deadly Six were formed several years prior by Master Zik. However, it was Master Zik's student Zavok who would assume leadership over the group, as his demeanor and intelligence made him well-suited for the position.


## Sonic Lost World


In the Wii U version and PC version of Sonic Lost World, the Deadly Six were enslaved by Dr. Eggman through his usage of a Cacophonic Conch, which the doctor used to cause them pain if they disobey or offend him. Zazz, always eager for a fight, volunteered to fight Sonic first but was defeated twice. Dr. Eggman sent Zomom after Sonic next, but Sonic defeated him twice. During a confrontation between the Deadly Six and Dr. Eggman, Sonic appeared and recklessly knocked the Cacophonic Conch away. Now free from Dr. Eggman's control, the Deadly Six rebelled against him, commanding all of the Badniks they took control of to attack everyone present. Despite this, Sonic, along with Tails, Dr. Eggman and his two robot henchmen Orbot and Cubot escaped safely from the danger.

Master Zik was the third to fight Sonic. The Deadly Six then used Dr. Eggman's unstable Extractor to absorb the Earth's life, growing stronger over time. Zeena fought Sonic fourth. Later on, the Deadly Six decided to capture Sonic and turn him into a robot under their control, but they captured Tails instead. Zor fought Sonic fifth and Zavok fought him last.

After each of the Deadly Six had been defeated twice by Sonic, Zavok proceeded to transform Tails into a robot as an act of revenge. Zazz, Zomom and Master Zik attacked Sonic and Dr. Eggman at Lava Mountain, seemingly murdering Dr. Eggman, Orbot and Cubot as they fell towards a river of lava they were standing over while Sonic survived and defeated the trio. Eventually, the hedgehog confronted the rest of the Deadly Six, who tried to force Sonic to surrender by sending in the roboticized Tails and ordered him to attack Sonic. Tails, however, was merely feigning his roboticization and soon assaulted the Zeti, causing them to flee as Sonic pursued and defeated them one by one.

The fate of the Deadly Six (aside from Zavok and Zazz) is left unclear, as each of them disappeared after their defeat leaving Dr. Eggman as the final boss. However, Dr. Eggman vowed to try and control the Deadly Six again.


## Other game appearances



## Sonic Dash


In Sonic Dash, Zazz is featured as one of the two bosses fought randomly while in a run. All members of the Deadly Six appeared as cameos in the game's downloadable wallpapers of Sonic Lost World.


## Super Smash Bros. for Wii U


In Super Smash Bros. for Wii U, the Deadly Six makes a cameo appearance as one of the collectible trophies representing the Sonic the Hedgehog series.


## Sonic Runners


In Sonic Runners, during the "Zazz Raid" event, the Deadly Six made their reappearance. This time around, Zazz returned to get his revenge on Sonic by using Power Rings to boost his powers. Each time he confronted Team Sonic however, he was defeated. Between Zazz's battles, the Zeti would go to each of the other Deadly Six for advice on how to beat the heroes, though their advice either did not work or the others would just tease him. However, Zavok told Zazz to keep on fighting, knowing full well that Zazz could not hope to beat Sonic; however, Zazz's constant failures allowed the Zeti to gather the information needed to destroy Sonic one day.


## In other media



## Archie Comics


In the Sonic the Hedgehog comic series and its spin-offs published by Archie Comics, the Deadly Six were enslaved by Dr. Eggman with the Cacophonic Conch, but got liberated by Sigma, a villainous robot from an alternate world. The group briefly teamed up with Sigma to aid with his plans, only to betray him when they received power-enhancing armor from him. However, Sigma took control over the Deadly Six using their armor and instated them as his new commanders. They were eventually freed by the Unified Army, and their involvement with Sigma would be erased from history by Xander Payne.

At some point in the not-so-distant future, the Deadly Six would be enslaved by Dr. Eggman once more.
