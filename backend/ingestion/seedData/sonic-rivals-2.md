Sonic Rivals 2 is a racing video game in the Sonic the Hedgehog series that was developed by Backbone Entertainment under the supervision of Sega Studio USA. It is the sequel to Sonic Rivals. The game was released exclusively for the PlayStation Portable handheld video game console in North America on 13 November 2007 and in Europe and Australia in the following month and like its prequel, the game was not released in Japan.

The game was then re-released as a digital downloadable game from the PlayStation Store on October 2009 in North America, August 2010 in Europe and October 2010 in Australia. The digital version is also compatible with the PlayStation Vita.

## Storylines


Sonic meets up with Tails, who recently told Sonic that he had something important to tell him: a huge amount of Chao have gone missing. Tails believes that Dr. Eggman is behind the disappearances and that he is currently located on Blue Coast Zone, the island where Sonic and Tails are. Sonic agrees to go after him, but wonders why he would want Chao.

During their search, Sonic comes across Knuckles and Rouge, who have teamed up to hunt down the Master Emerald. Sonic jokingly enquires if they are on a date. Knuckles, telling Sonic to knock it off, asks if he has seen Dr. Eggman. Sonic seems peeved by Knuckles trying to pry into his business, at which point Knuckles awkwardly admits to Sonic that the Master Emerald has gone missing again. Sonic is not surprised by this but insists that he must talk to Dr. Eggman before Knuckles does. Knuckles angrily shouts after him to wait up but to no avail. Upset that Sonic wants to get to Dr. Eggman first, Knuckles tries to race him there. Afterward, Knuckles suggests they engage in some physical combat instead of racing each other. Sonic thinks that means Knuckles is getting worn out, which only makes Knuckles angrier and causes him to fight Sonic.

Meanwhile, Tails speaks to Rouge, who tells him she is "looking for treasure", meaning the Master Emerald. Like Knuckles, she wonders if Tails knows where Dr. Eggman is. Tails tells her he is on the island, and that he and Sonic were about to go and search for him, but before he can finish, Rouge leaves, saying her thanks and assuring Tails she will tell Dr. Eggman they're looking for him. Tails tells her she cannot do that, or they'll never find him. He begs her to wait up, but it's too late.

After Sonic beats Knuckles, he runs into Dr. Eggman, who has been told by Rouge that he is looking for him. Sonic affirms this, then asks what he has done with the Chao. ## Multiplayer

## Collectables



## Card Collection


Sonic Rivals 2 features a card collecting system. There are a total number of 150 different cards to collect, and each is collected by completing an achievement. For example, getting an S Rank in Knockout Mode unlocks a card of Mephiles the Dark, and collecting 200 rings in a race unlocks Tails Icon. The cards themselves contain art from various older Sonic games and can unlock things such as alternate suits for the characters. For example, the Blue World Ring card will unlock Rouge's race suit. Unlike the first game, only one card is needed to unlock a suit, and each character has four suits instead of the original three. The cards also unlock the Cup Circuits and Chao Detectors for the Zones. For instance, collecting Nebula card will unlock the first Zone's Chao detector.


## Suits


Various Character Outfits that can be unlocked in the game by meeting select challenges. Each character has four unlockable skins, making a total of thirty two outfits that can be unlocked for all eight characters.

Unlike the first game, the player cannot choose one part of a costume and other parts of other costumes, only being able to select the complete set.


## Soundtrack


The main theme song for the game is "Race to Win" by Ted Poley and Tony Harnell, which also plays in Blue Coast Zone: Act 1. The rest of the game's music is composed by Chris Rezanson.


## Cast


Sonic Rivals 2 is one of the few voiced Sonic the Hedgehog game to lack a Japanese dub.

Like it's predecessor, Sonic Rivals 2 received mixed to positive reviews. GameRankings currently scores it 63.05%, and the game sits at a 60% on Metacritic.
