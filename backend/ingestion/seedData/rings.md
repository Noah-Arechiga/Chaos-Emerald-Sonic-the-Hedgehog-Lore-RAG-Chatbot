Rings are the series' core collectible and survival resource, present since
the original Sonic the Hedgehog (1991). Scattered throughout levels, Rings
serve as a buffer against damage: characters holding at least one Ring who
are hit by an enemy or hazard survive by scattering their Rings instead of
losing a life directly, with the Rings becoming briefly collectible again
before disappearing.

## Relationship to Chaos Emeralds and Super forms

Rings are also the resource that fuels Super transformations. A character
with all seven Chaos Emeralds needs a minimum threshold of Rings (commonly
50 in many games) to initiate a Super form, and the transformation then
continuously drains Rings over time until they run out, at which point the
character automatically reverts to normal. This ties Ring collection
directly into the risk/reward loop of using Super forms during regular
gameplay, rather than only in scripted story sequences.

## Special stages

Collecting a sufficient number of Rings by the end of a level (or finding
hidden "Giant Rings") is the traditional gateway into Special Stages, bonus
levels historically used across the series as the primary method for
obtaining Chaos Emeralds during actual gameplay, reinforcing Rings as the
mechanical link between everyday platforming and the series' central Emerald
mythology.
