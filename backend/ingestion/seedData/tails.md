Miles "Tails" Prower is a young two-tailed fox and Sonic's closest friend and
sidekick, first appearing in Sonic the Hedgehog 2 (1992). His nickname
"Tails" comes from his titular trait: he has two tails instead of one, which
he can spin like a helicopter rotor to achieve short bursts of flight.

## Personality and role

Tails is characterized as intelligent, mechanically gifted, and initially
shy, idolizing Sonic and often serving as his engineer and pilot. He builds
and maintains the Tornado biplane and, in later games, the Tornado-derived
mechs and gadgets used across the series. Over the course of the franchise
he's shown growing in confidence, taking a more active, independent role in
several games (notably having his own campaign in Sonic Adventure and being
playable solo in various spin-offs).

## Powers and abilities

Beyond flight via his twin tails, Tails is a capable swimmer, an expert
pilot, and — in some games with all seven Chaos Emeralds — can achieve a
Super Tails transformation (notably in Sonic 3 & Knuckles). He's generally
the team's technical specialist, contrasted with Sonic's raw speed and
Knuckles's raw strength.
