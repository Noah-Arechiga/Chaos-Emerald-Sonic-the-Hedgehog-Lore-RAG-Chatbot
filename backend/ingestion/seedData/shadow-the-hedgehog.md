Shadow the Hedgehog is a black-and-red hedgehog introduced in Sonic Adventure
2 (2001) as "the Ultimate Lifeform," a genetically engineered being created
aboard the space colony ARK roughly fifty years before the main story. He is
one of the few characters in the franchise with power roughly comparable to
Sonic's, and is frequently portrayed as a rival or foil to him.

## Creation and Maria Robotnik

Shadow was created by Professor Gerald Robotnik (grandfather of Dr. Eggman)
in a joint military/scientific project, partly using genetic material from
an alien species called Black Doom, whose Black Arms would later reappear as
antagonists in Shadow's own spin-off game. Gerald's terminally ill
granddaughter, Maria Robotnik, cared for Shadow and became his closest
companion aboard the ARK; Shadow was originally intended, in part, to help
find a cure for Maria's illness.

## The ARK incident

Sonic Adventure 2 reveals that government forces (GUN) raided the ARK,
believing Shadow to be a dangerous weapon, and that Maria was shot and
killed while helping Shadow escape to a stasis pod, with her final act being
to ask Shadow to protect humanity and give them a chance at happiness.
Shadow was captured and placed in suspended animation for decades. For most
of Sonic Adventure 2, Shadow's memory of these events is distorted — Eggman
misleads him into believing his mission is one of revenge against humanity
for Maria's death — until Shadow recovers the true memory of Maria's actual
last wish near the game's climax, which becomes the turning point that
leads him to help stop the space colony ARK from crashing into Earth using
Chaos Control alongside Sonic.

## Ambiguous status and recurring role

Sonic Adventure 2 ends with Shadow's fate left deliberately ambiguous after
the Chaos Control sequence, and he's absent for a time before returning in
Sonic Heroes, with amnesia regarding the ARK's final moments, working to
piece together his own past. He later stars in his own game, Shadow the
Hedgehog (2005), which deals directly with his identity and the Black Arms'
invasion, and he continues to reappear across the series (Sonic '06, Sonic
Forces, Sonic Frontiers, and others) generally as a powerful ally with a
complicated, guilt- and loyalty-driven relationship to his own origin.

## Powers

Shadow shares Sonic's speed and Chaos Emerald compatibility, is capable of
Super Shadow transformations with all seven Emeralds, and is specifically
associated with Chaos Control (space-time manipulation) and Chaos Spear (a
projectile energy attack), reflecting his artificial, Chaos-energy-infused
origin.
