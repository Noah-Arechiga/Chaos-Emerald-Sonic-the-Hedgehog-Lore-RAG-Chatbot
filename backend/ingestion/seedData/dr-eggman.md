Dr. Eggman, also known by his birth name Dr. Ivo Robotnik, is the recurring
primary antagonist of the Sonic the Hedgehog series, first appearing in the
original Sonic the Hedgehog (1991). He is a brilliant, eccentric scientist
and engineer obsessed with world domination, most often pursuing this goal
by capturing animals and converting them into robotic soldiers (Badniks),
building elaborate mechanical fortresses, and seeking out the Chaos
Emeralds' power for his machines.

## Family and the Robotnik lineage

Later games establish Eggman as the grandson of Professor Gerald Robotnik, a
brilliant scientist who worked aboard the space colony ARK decades earlier
on Project Shadow and other advanced research (see: Shadow the Hedgehog,
Sonic Adventure 2). This lineage becomes a major plot thread — Eggman's
discovery of his grandfather's old notes and creations (including Shadow
himself) repeatedly drives major story arcs, tying Eggman's modern-day
schemes back to Gerald's earlier work and tragedy.

## Recurring role

Across the series, Eggman's goals vary from game to game but commonly
involve: collecting all seven Chaos Emeralds to power a superweapon or
transformation, building an "Eggman Empire" over a captured city or region,
reactivating or repurposing ancient technology (Echidna ruins, the ARK,
Eggmanland), or allying with (and usually eventually being betrayed by or
betraying) other powerful antagonists such as the Chaos creature, the
Deadly Six, or Dr. Eggman Nega, a future/alternate version of himself.
Despite his consistent defeats, he is portrayed as a genuine scientific
genius, responsible for a huge amount of the franchise's advanced
technology, and one of the few recurring characters with narrative and
technological connections across nearly every major saga.
