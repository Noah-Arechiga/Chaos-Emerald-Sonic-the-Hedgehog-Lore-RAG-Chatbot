Sonic the Hedgehog 3 is a live action film based on the Sonic the Hedgehog series and the sequel to the 2022 film Sonic the Hedgehog 2. It is the third entry in the Sonic the Hedgehog film series produced by Paramount Pictures, and was released on 20 December 2024.

Being an adaptation of the video game Sonic Adventure 2 (2001) and taking some inspiration from Shadow the Hedgehog (2005), the film follows Team Sonic as they face the return and latest scheme of Dr. Robotnik, all while a mysterious adversary emerges.


## Plot


On Prison Island in Tokyo Bay, Japan, an anthropomorphic red-and-black hedgehog named Shadow is being kept in suspended animation while being monitored by Guardian Units of Nations. One of the guards expresses concern for the project, but another guard assures him that Shadow has not moved in fifty years. In his capsule, Shadow recalls old memories, such as listening to a young girl play on a guitar, watching the stars with her, and a tragedy that occurred long ago. Suddenly, Shadow's heart rate begins to spike, then one of the guards notices that their system has been hacked and the containment tank is destabilizing. As the heartrate monitor glitches, the guards realize that Shadow is waking up and immediately calls for security. Multiple G.U.N. soldiers surround and aim at the capsule containing Shadow. Despite his heartrate flatlining for a brief moment, Shadow suddenly awakens and shatters the glass keeping him contained before teleporting out of sight. He then knocks out the soldiers one by one, using his ability to disappear and reappear in between each attack. One of the guards' requests backup, only to realize that there is nobody left. Shadow breaks through the compound and teleports onto the water, skating away from Prison Island and toward Tokyo.

Elsewhere, in a forest near Green Hills, Sonic the Hedgehog, Miles "Tails" Prower, Knuckles the Echidna, Tom Wachowski, and Maddie Wachowski are all out enjoying a camping trip. While Tails and Knuckles start to prepare for a race, Sonic walks up to them dressed in swimming gear, under the assumption that they were going tubing. Realizing that they are actually having a race, Sonic discards his swimming gear and asks Tom and Maddie to start the countdown. Tom assures Maddie that a bit of sibling rivalry is healthy and that he and his brothers were similar, to which Maddie responds that his brothers were lunatics and none of them were born with the ability to trigger a global cataclysmic event. Tom concedes her point and asks the three to keep the race "cataclysmic-less", which they all agree to. Once Maddie finishes counting down, the race begins. Sonic is confident that he is in the lead, but is surprised to see both Tails and Knuckles ahead of him. Sonic quickly takes back the lead, using his Spin Dash to gain more speed until he reaches the goal. His celebration is cut short when he sees that Tails and Knuckles were somehow already there, meaning he has lost, but he soon realizes that they are actually holograms. Sonic is initially excited to know that he is still the fastest of the group, but then becomes curious as to what is going on. He rushes back to the starting point to find Maddie, Tails, Tom, Ozzy, and Knuckles surprising him with a party. Tom explains that today is the anniversary of the day that Sonic came to Earth, dubbing it "Happy B(earth)day". Knuckles states that today is the day that they honor Sonic, while the others comment on how Sonic has improved the group's lives and brought them together as a family (except for Ozzy, who envies Sonic, due to the fact that he "stole [his] hoomans").

After the party, Sonic thanks Tom for setting it up. He then notices a familiar location nearby and immediately rushes toward it, slightly annoying Tom by going too fast as usual. After arriving where Sonic went to, Tom quickly realizes that it is the cave where Sonic used to live. Tom is surprised that Sonic lived in such a poor environment for so long while Sonic plays with the nunchucks that he refers to as his "state-of-the-art security system". Tom then notices a mural on the wall, which Sonic explains he drew on his first night on Earth to remember his origins and time with Longclaw. Sonic wonders if Longclaw would be proud of him, and Tom assures him that she would due to the fact that despite losing her at such a young age, he did not let his pain change who he is in his heart. Tom goes on to explain that life is about the choices people make, both good and bad, and that as long as Sonic listens to his heart, he will make the right choice when it matters most. Sonic thanks Tom for the advice and wonders how different his life would be without him, believing he would probably be a completely different hedgehog, being just like Shadow.

In Tokyo, Shadow is walking the streets, gradually growing accustomed to his new modern surroundings. Then he turns to see a promotional ad and discovers that it's the year 2024, leaving him shocked to discover that he was trapped for 50 years (1974 - 2024). All of a sudden, a horde of G.U.N. soldiers and vehicles surround the area and rally around Shadow. Annoyed by G.U.N.'s continued pursuit of him, Shadow prepares for a fight as his eyes glow with red and orange energy. Back at the campsite, Sonic's group is relaxing and roasting marshmallows, with Tom and Maddie appreciating the opportunity to have some peace and quiet. However, they are interrupted by the sudden arrival of a G.U.N. helicopter, from which Director Rockwell emerges. She explains to them that Commander Walters is requesting Team Sonic's immediate assistance in dealing with the situation in Tokyo. In response to this, Knuckles questions the team name while Sonic immediately agrees to it given his nature. As they set off on the helicopter, Maddie calls out to Team Sonic, reminding them to make good choices.

After using a Ring to reach Tokyo, Sonic asks Tails, who is piloting the helicopter, how to find Shadow. At that moment, a giant explosion erupts, providing them with a clear sign of where to start looking. Tails proudly displays his newly made titanium handcuffs, which Knuckles dismissively ignores, claiming he is "one million percent muscle." This assertion slightly annoys Sonic and Tails, as they have heard it numerous times before. The co-pilot informs Team Sonic that they are over the drop zone, and the trio agrees to stick together, regardless of who or what is down there. Director Rockwell approaches them, making a quip before Team Sonic exits the helicopter. Upon landing at the scene, they observe that G.U.N. has been defeated and wonder what transpired. As Tails picks up strong energy readings on the Miles Electric, a truck is suddenly hurled toward them. Knuckles catches it and tosses it aside, once again boasting about his strength. Then, Shadow appears before Team Sonic, surprising them with his similar appearance to Sonic. Both hedgehogs question why the other looks like him, but Shadow quickly dismisses the conversation as a waste of time. He warns the trio to walk away before they get hurt. Wanting to resolve the situation without conflict, Sonic pulls out the handcuffs that Tails made and asks Shadow to talk with them. However, Shadow refuses, citing their association with G.U.N., and turns to walk away.

Having had enough, Knuckles charges at Shadow to attack him, but Shadow uses his teleportation ability to overwhelm and send Knuckles crashing into a nearby shop. After helping Knuckles up, Team Sonic prepares to fight Shadow together, only to be swiftly defeated by the black hedgehog. He then picks up a energy gun and drives off on a motorbike, warning them not to follow. Tails tries to advise Sonic against pursuing Shadow, calling it a bad idea, but Sonic, believing that has never stopped him in the past, goes after him anyway. After chasing him through the streets of Tokyo and bantering along the way, Sonic and Shadow clash with their respective chaos energies, resulting in a citywide power outage. The city's backup generators turn on shortly after, and Sonic wakes up to find himself handcuffed to a nearby railing. Tails and Knuckles quickly find him, the former of whom is upset that Sonic went off by himself despite them being a team. Sonic apologizes, still not fully accustomed to the concept of teamwork, and decides they need to regroup and get some answers from G.U.N. before continuing.

To this end, they decide to take shelter at a Chao Garden café, where they are later joined by Commander Walters. When Sonic asks who Shadow is, Walters explains that Shadow's story is similar to his own, but while Sonic found family and friends on Earth, Shadow only encountered pain and loss. Walters recounts that Shadow's story began over fifty years ago with a meteor crash in a quiet corner of Oklahoma. Inside the meteorite was a lifeform, specifically referred to as the "ultimate" lifeform. Scientists who discovered him experimented continuously with his Chaos energy, believing it could usher in a new era for humanity. However, they eventually deemed it too dangerous. During an attempt by G.U.N. to secure Shadow, a terrible accident occurred that destroyed the lab and resulted in many fatalities. The project leader was blamed for the incident, leading to his imprisonment and the cancellation of the program. Convinced that Shadow was too dangerous to release but also too valuable to destroy, Walters decided to place him in stasis indefinitely on Prison Island.

Sonic realizes that if Shadow spent 50 years in stasis, someone must have helped him escape. Walters agrees, but he points out that only a few people in the world are aware of Shadow's existence. A live show then begins with the Chao mascots dancing on stage, much to Tails' excitement, only for it to be cut short by an attack from several Drone Badniks, who destroy the table Team Sonic and Commander Walters were sitting at. As the civilians flee in terror, Sonic notices that they are the same robots previously used by Dr. Robotnik, who was assumed to be dead after his last defeat. A motorcyclist, whose face is concealed by his helmet, then crashes into the building, skidding to a halt in front of the drones. One of them attempts to shoot him with a missile, which he easily dodges, causing it to blow up part of the restaurant behind him. He then disables the drones using a device on his wrist before riding away. Team Sonic then finds Commander Walters trapped beneath some rubble, which Knuckles lifts, revealing the commander to be badly injured from the attack. Before succumbing to his wounds, Walters gives Sonic a key to what he describes as the most powerful weapon G.U.N. has ever created, trusting only Sonic to keep it safe. Several trucks of G.U.N. troops then arrive at the scene, with Director Rockwell among them. Searching Walters' body, she notices that his launch key is missing and instead finds a photo of Team Sonic, leading her to believe that they had stolen it and that they could not be trusted.

Meanwhile, Team Sonic used parts of Chao costumes to disguise themselves and managed to sneak out undetected. Tails questioned why they were running from G.U.N. if they were supposed to be on the same side, but Sonic replied that they would not trust anyone until they had more information. Team Sonic chases down the mysterious biker, until the trio catch them at the docks. The motorcyclist removed his helmet to reveal himself as Agent Stone, much to Knuckles' surprise, as the echidna had referred to Stone as the "Goat Milker." causing Stone to mock him. Stone informed Team Sonic that he is not here to fight. Sonic and Tails demanded to know why he is here and why he had freed Shadow. However, Stone explained that the last thing they wanted was more super-powered hedgehogs running around, and that Robotnik had nothing to do with the attack. He then summons the Mecha Crab from the ocean and invites Team Sonic inside, where they find an overweight and unshaven Robotnik watching a telenovela, listening to music, and eating junk food. Stone and Team Sonic are here, with the team watching in disgust at how much Robotnik had let himself go. Sonic and Tails express their surprise at seeing Robotnik—who Sonic still refers to as "Eggman"—alive while also being disturbed by his disheveled appearance. However, the doctor dismisses their comments, not caring what they think he looks like. Stone informs Robotnik of an impostor using his technology, showing footage from the attack at the Chao Garden. Annoyed at having his persona and "precious babies" stolen, Robotnik cross-references the coordinates of each drone's energy spikes in order to track down the impostor's base of operations. In order to get closer to finding Shadow, Team Sonic reluctantly decides to team up with Robotnik, but not before he makes Sonic hand over one of his quills, reinvigorating him with a burst of its Chaos energy. With the alliance formed, Robotnik has Stone shave his head and help him make his suit fit.

Meanwhile, Shadow is seen walking through an abandoned G.U.N. facility in the mountainside when he comes across an old roller skate, causing him to reminisce on his late friend, Maria Robotnik. The scene shifts to a period from 1974, during which she is seen roller skating through the then-active facility while carrying a box of her personal belongings. A guard tried to stop her as she approached the laboratory, but Walters, who was a captain at the time, allowed her to enter due to her being granddaughter of Professor Gerald. Walters told Maria where her room was while asking her to take her skates off. Shortly after, Maria came across a room containing the capsule where Shadow was being held for research. Hesitantly approaching the capsule, Maria tried to touch it, only for Shadow to suddenly turn around and glare at her. Although taken aback at first, Maria playfully glared back before drawing a funny face on the capsule to amuse herself. Upon seeing Shadow calm down a little, Maria put her hand on the capsule, and Shadow put his hand up to meet hers. From this point, the two formed a close friendship, enjoying various activities together such as dancing, skating, and watching a movie. However, during the latter, Shadow became upset due to the appearance of a monster that was referred to as an "alien freak". Later on, Maria took Shadow up to a field outside the facility, where they watched the stars. Maria commented on their beauty while explaining that it can take hundreds of years for the light of a star can to reach Earth, and that the star itself may no longer exist by the time people are able to see it. This prompted Shadow to wonderingly comment that "the light shines, even though the star is gone". Maria wondered which star Shadow came from, although he had no memory of his home. Maria responded by telling Shadow that Earth is his home now. Shadow asked Maria if she thought he was dangerous like that movie monster was, as he could tell that the scientists thought so from the way they looked at him. Maria told Shadow that he was her friend, and that he could be whoever he wanted to despite his powers and origins. Shadow, now reassured, took her hand gratefully and they both continued gazing at the stars.

The scene returns to 2024, Shadow arrives at the now-destroyed room where he first met Maria, silently grieving until he notices Gerald behind him. The professor greets Shadow, having known he would come "home". Shadow tells Gerald he is unable to stop thinking about Maria as the pain from losing her is overwhelming, which Gerald states is the reason they must punish those who took Maria's life. Around the same time, Robotnik, Stone, and Team Sonic arrive at the base via a Ring. Robotnik tries to figure out a way to open the door, only for Knuckles to break it down with a punch. Hearing their arrival, Shadow prepares for a fight, but Gerald tells him these are the guests he was expecting and that they should give them a "warm welcome". As Team Sonic, Robotnik, and Stone walk into the base, Stone wonders what this place is. Sonic replies that it looks like a military base, but nobody has been here for years, leading him to wonder if it's haunted. This thought frightens Knuckles, who is uneasy at the mention of ghosts. Stone then asks Knuckles if he is afraid of ghosts, but Knuckles insists that nothing scares an echidna warrior. However, Sonic recalls Team Sonic's last movie night, where Knuckles was scared so badly while they were watching Casper that he grabbed Sonic and Tails' hands tightly in panic, causing both the hedgehog and the fox to spill their bowls of popcorn and scream in pain while trying to break free from Knuckles' grip, begging him to let go. Back in the present, Knuckles says that there is no such thing as a friendly ghost. Sonic and the others come across three separate paths and realize they need to split up to cover more ground. Not wanting to let Robotnik out of his sight, Sonic chooses the right pathway with him. Meanwhile, Tails and Stone head down the left, while Knuckles takes the middle alone. Knuckles attempts to keep his cool while navigating the tunnel, but is quickly caught by Shadow after the latter startles him. Meanwhile, Stone and Tails are walking through another tunnel, with the former admitting that he finds Tails adorable. Stone asks Tails what it is like being Sonic's assistant, wondering if he has a daily routine or any benefits, but Tails tells him that he and Sonic are actually teammates, with Sonic as the leader, Knuckles as the muscle, and Tails as the inventor. Tails explains that their team is made special by their unique roles and their bond as best friends, and asks Stone if he and Robotnik are the same. Despite not truly believing it himself, Stone tells him that they are indeed best friends. Shadow then appears from behind them and kidnaps them. Elsewhere, Sonic and Robotnik enter a room, and Sonic realizes from the items scattered about, including a photo of Shadow and Maria, that this was an actual home where people lived and that Shadow had a family. Sonic asks Robotnik if he has ever wondered what he would be like if he had a family to keep him from turning into a villain, although the doctor rejects this idea, stating that he never had a family and believing himself to be "undesirable to all possible genders". Sonic enters the room where Shadow used to be kept, only for the latter to ambush and knock him out.

While wondering where Sonic went, Robotnik comes across several of his stolen drones, prompting him to joyfully hug them. However, he is interrupted by the appearance of the "impostor" and activates the drones to threaten him. Despite this, the man simply praises Robotnik for his "magnificent creations" and explains that he borrowed them to lure Robotnik here and reunite their family. When Robotnik does not respond, the man removes his glasses and properly introduces himself as Gerald Robotnik, Ivo's grandfather. Despite noticing the uncanny resemblance between them, Ivo does not yet believe they are relatives and begins interrogating Gerald asking him many hard questions. After Gerald answers each question correctly, Ivo is convinced and hugs him, asking where he has been his entire life. Gerald tells him he has spent the last fifty years imprisoned, preventing him from contacting anyone. Gerald is about to tell Ivo why he brought him here, but Ivo retorts that he used his unparalleled intellect to track him down. Gerald counters that it was for the plan he concocted with his own unparalleled intellect, and leads Ivo to the spot where Team Sonic and Agent Stone are tied up in chains. As Sonic regains consciousness, he is horrified to see "two Eggmen" approaching him. Shadow confronts Ivo, referring to him as "the long-lost grandson." In response, Ivo calls Shadow "another stinking hedgehog." Team Sonic realizes that Gerald is the one responsible for Shadow's escape and the attack on Walters. Shadow then takes the key to G.U.N.'s superweapon from Team Sonic, claiming that he and Gerald intend to use it to reclaim everything G.U.N. has taken from them. Gerald presents a hologram of the superweapon, an orbital satellite called the Eclipse Cannon. He explains that he provided G.U.N. with this powerful weapon, the most advanced mankind has ever seen, in exchange for his freedom. The Eclipse Cannon is capable of delivering a precision strike anywhere on Earth. Gerald decides that their first target will be G.U.N. Headquarters (which is located in London), callously dismissing Sonic's warning about the innocent lives that will be threatened. He then informs Ivo that in order to fire the Eclipse Cannon, they need two keys, with the second key being locked away in G.U.N. Headquarters. Ivo agrees to join him, and they set off, accompanied by Stone aboard the Mecha Crab. Before they depart, Ivo sarcastically thanks Sonic for "inspiring" their adventure, as it ultimately worked out in his favor, further justifying Sonic's lack of trust in him.

Sonic pleads with Shadow not to allow his pain to change who he is inside. However, Shadow coldly replies that he has spent fifty years reliving what G.U.N. did to Maria, and that this pain has shaped him into who he is today. He drops a small, marble-like device before teleporting away. Sonic is initially unimpressed by it, but it turns out to be a miniature black hole that is consuming all the matter in the base. It starts pulling Team Sonic in, but Knuckles grabs a rope with his teeth, holding them in place while Sonic uses one of Tails' rings to open a portal. Although it takes a while for the portal to actually open due to the ring bouncing off the surrounding debris, they manage to narrowly escape, ending up on a nearby cliff where they watch the last of the base being swallowed up by the black hole. Aboard the Death Crab, Ivo, believing Sonic to be dead, is impressed by how quickly Gerald managed to dispose of him. Gerald, in turn, assures him that there is nothing they cannot destroy together. Stone charts a route to G.U.N. Headquarters in London, informing the Robotniks that they will arrive in about four hours. Realizing that they are ahead of schedule, Gerald decides to take the opportunity to spend quality time with his grandson, much to Ivo's joy. Using virtual reality headsets, they enjoy a variety of recreational activities together, with Stone trying but failing to join in. He prepares lattes for them but ends up discarding them after seeing how much fun they are already having.

Back at the Wachowski household, Tom and Maddie are enjoying their new hobbies and interests, geeting used to this. Suddenly, the place shake, as a ring portal opens in front of them and Team Sonic suddenly appears in the living room, asking for their help on a mission. Eager for excitement, Tom and Maddie immediately agree, having become bored without the rest of their family. Tails uses the Miles Electric to create a holographic simulation of G.U.N. Headquarters so they can devise a plan. Sonic initially thinks he can run across the water and into the base, but he quickly realizes this would get him incinerated by the energy shield. Tails explains that to disable the shield, they need to gain control of it by planting a USB drive in the server room, a task that will fall to Tom and Maddie. After that, Sonic will need to navigate through a laser hall to access the vault. However, G.U.N. has wiped all images and schematics of the vault from their database, leaving them unaware of its design and preventing them from using a ring to access it. Maddie suggests that the plan would work a lot better if Sonic could use the Master Emerald to transform into Super Sonic and obtain the key. Sonic, however, refuses, stating that the Master Emerald is far too powerful to be used again and that they made a promise to keep it hidden. As the only one who knows the Master Emerald's location, Knuckles asserts that as its guardian, anyone who attempts to take it for any reason will become a sworn enemy. Sonic reassures Tom and Maddie that this is the right decision, and they set off for London.

Around the same time, Team Robotnik arrives right outside G.U.N. Headquarters. Shadow, fueled by his desire for revenge, wants to quickly grab the key and destroy anyone in his way, but Gerald tells him to be patient and let him and Ivo handle it, promising the hedgehog that he will soon have his revenge on an unimaginable scale. Agent Stone provides the Robotniks with their bodysuits for the mission, and is instructed by Ivo to watch Shadow in the meantime while keeping the Mecha Crab hidden. While Ivo and Gerald set off, Stone invites Shadow into the Mecha Crab to have guac, to which Shadow responds, "revenge guac". Meanwhile, Sonic impatiently waits for his team to start the operation, complaining about waiting all day, though Tails points out they have only just begun. Tails asks for Tom, Maddie, and Knuckles' status, with all three in position and ready to proceed. Knuckles, who has taken position atop the Big Ben, is tasked with "breaking glass in case of an emergency", although he initially ignores the emergency part out of eagerness for the chance to punch something. In order to gain access into the headquarters, Tom and Maddie use Tails' hologram technology to assume the likeness of G.U.N. agents, with Maddie taking on Rachel's appearance. Tom critiques Maddie's initial enthusiasm as Rachel for being out of character, causing Maddie to immediately display the more aggressive attitude Rachel is known for. Tom responds that it is perfect and takes on the appearance of her husband Randall.

As Tom and Maddie approached the front desk of the headquarters, Tom, disguised as Randall, requested to have lunch upstairs with his wife. The agent at the front desk informed him that the facility was currently locked down and that only those with command-level clearance were allowed access. Tom quietly contacted Tails through the intercom, prompting the fox to realize that security had tightened following Shadow's escape. At that moment, Maddie, disguised as Rachel, stepped in and delivered an intimidating speech to the agent, threatening to have him fired if he did not comply. Yielding to her pressure, the agent allowed them to proceed. Once upstairs, they switched out of their holograms. Maddie transformed into Captain Rockwell and attempted to gain access to the server room using facial recognition. However, Tom noticed the real Rockwell approaching with several guards. After a tense moment, Rockwell walked past them, and they seized the opportunity to enter the server room. Tails guided Tom and Maddie to the location of the security server, while Sonic and Knuckles grew impatient, ready to carry out their part of the mission. Maddie inserted the drive, but it had no immediate effect, leading Sonic to question whether she had inserted it correctly. In a moment of inspiration, Tom suggested blowing on the drive before reinserting it, which successfully activated it. As Tails was about to shut down the outside sensors, Tom and Maddie noticed another USB drive next to theirs, this one with a giant mustache. Meanwhile, Tails detected two heat signatures moving toward the vault. It turned out to be the Robotniks, who were using the camouflage feature in their suits to sneak past the guards undetected. In the Mecha Crab, Shadow and Stone are watching La Última Pasión, the same telenovela Ivo was watching earlier. Shadow believes that Gabriella, the show's female lead, should kill Juan and, Pablo, the two men, competing for her love as she is not a prize to be won. Stone tells Shadow to lighten up, reminding him that they are about to rule the world. Shadow, however, replies that there will be nothing left to rule once they are done. This comment concerns Stone, causing him to ask what he and Gerald are up to. Meanwhile, Tails detects an outside signal from the Mecha Crab. He destroys it by flooding the sewer where it is concealed, resulting in both the Mecha Crab and Stone being washed away.

Meanwhile, the Robotniks arrive at the laser hall, where Gerald asks Ivo what their next move is. When Ivo unexpectedly pushes him into the lasers, Gerald initially panics and flails around. However, he soon realizes that his bodysuit is protecting him from the lasers. Ivo explains that he designed the suits to be impenetrable to lasers, but he was previously unsure if the design would work. After Gerald compliments Ivo on his cruelty and heartlessness, they begin to dance their way through the laser hall, demonstrating perfect synchronization and coordination in their movements. Outside, Tails notices that the Robotniks are nearly at the vault, prompting Sonic to take action despite Tails' warnings about the active traps. After quickly taking in the sights of London, Sonic approaches G.U.N. Headquarters, with his friends telling him that he isn’t going to make it. At the last moment, just before he collides with the barrier, Tails grabs him and gives him a boost, allowing Sonic to get inside unharmed. Having arrived at the vault, the Robotniks watch as the doors open, revealing to them a room with the Eclipse Cannon key at the center. When Sonic arrives behind them, they make a run for the key, only to be pinned to the floor by an artificial gravity trap. Sonic taunts them and attempts to grab the key himself, but is caught in a similar trap that causes him to float in the air. Director Rockwell arrives and explains that the vault is armed with variable gravity, with green panels containing reduced gravity while the red panels have the opposite effect. Sonic attempts to warn Rockwell about the Robotniks' plan but she disregards him, still under the impression that Sonic wants to steal the key. A group of G.U.N. soldiers then enter the vault, having captured Tom, Maddie, and Tails. Tom and Ivo briefly exchange insults but are interrupted by Rockwell, who plans to arrest all of them. However, Sonic takes the opportunity to call in Knuckles, who glides toward the headquarters at high speed, using the Flames of Disaster to break through the barrier and into the vault, the impact of which knocks the gravity control device out of Rockwell's hands. Sonic tells Knuckles to disable the gravity trap, but he breaks the device instead, causing the gravity in the vault to go haywire and send everyone in different directions. Sonic is pinned to the floor while a large piece of debris starts to collapse above Tom, Maddie, and Tails, who are also stuck on the other side of the vault. Rockwell attempts to reach the key while floating in the air, pushing her way past soldiers until she and the key suddenly fall to the ground, with Ivo right across from her. As they both slowly inch toward the key, Sonic, still under the effects of the red gravity panel, uses his Chaos energy to build up enough strength to launch a Spin Dash, destroying the debris and saving Tom, Maddie, and Tails. At the same time, the gravity in the vault returns to normal, causing everyone who was floating, including Knuckles, to fall back down. Rockwell grabs the key before Ivo can and walks out of the vault, prompting Tom to follow her.

As Rockwell retreats down the hallway, she encounters Tom, who is disguised as Commander Walters and wearing an arm cast. He thanks Rockwell for keeping the key safe and asks her to hand it over. Although she is initially suspicious, Rockwell does not question his authority and gives him the key. Tom then asks her to organize the reinforcements. After getting out of sight, Tom reverts to his normal self and informs the others that he has the key before taking on Walters' appearance again. At that moment, Shadow suddenly breaks in through the roof. Enraged at the sight of Walters, Shadow strikes him with a Chaos energy-fueled punch, immediately knocking him out. As he picks up the Eclipse Cannon key, Shadow discovers that Walters was actually Tom in disguise. Sonic then arrives to inform Tom that the others have cleared the building, only to see Tom's unconscious body lying in front of Shadow. Horrified, Sonic rushes to him and desperately pleads for him to wake up. This scene reminds Shadow of his own reaction to Maria's death. Sonic then turns to Shadow, angrily demanding to know what he did. In response, Shadow simply says he did what he had to before teleporting away. Maddie, Tails, and Knuckles soon arrive, all crouching worriedly over Tom's body.

Back in the vault, Ivo and Gerald find themselves surrounded by G.U.N. soldiers as they try to get back on their feet. Suddenly, Shadow appears and swiftly defeats all the soldiers. He hands Gerald the second key before teleporting the three of them aboard the Eclipse Cannon. While Ivo admires the design of the station, Gerald reveals a pair of new suits he has crafted for both of them, causing Ivo to squeal with excitement. Ivo crawls over and begins to kiss Gerald's feet affectionately. Meanwhile, Agent Stone slowly emerges from the river as the Mecha Crab sinks behind him. He calls Ivo to inform him about Shadow's earlier comments, implying that Gerald may not have been completely honest with them. Ivo interprets Stone's remarks as jealousy toward the bond between him and Gerald, leading him to fire Stone for his insolence. However, Ivo decides to keep Stone around until he is "successfully cloned." Stone pleads with Robotnik, expressing his reluctance to lose him again, but Ivo hangs up and blocks Stone, leaving him to sadly give up. Gerald then hands Ivo the two keys. Ivo inserts them into the slot on the computer of the Eclipse Cannon, causing it to rise from the River Thames. Team Sonic, Maddie, Agent Stone, and the people of London all watch in horror.

Outside G.U.N. Headquarters, multiple police cars and ambulances arrive to assess the situation, accompanied by reinforcements from G.U.N. Tom is taken to an ambulance while Maddie follows closely behind. Sonic, Tails, and Knuckles watch sadly as the ambulance doors close and it drives away. Maddie turns to Sonic and reassures him that it’s not his fault. Despite Tails trying to comfort him by saying that Tom will be okay, an enraged Sonic responds that comfort is not what he needs right now. He insists that he needs to stop them, by any means necessary, brushes his and Knuckles' concerns off and decides to use the power of the Master Emerald. ## Production



## Development


Prior to release of the second Sonic film, Patrick Casey and Josh Miller revealed in an episode of Kellen's Petty Talk Show on 5 April 2021 that they expressed interest in making a third Sonic movie, going as far as to mention that there was an Eggman scene cut from the second film for the sake of flow that they would want to re-add to the third movie. The film was eventually announced on 15 February 2022 at the ViacomCBS Investors Connect day, two months before the release of Sonic the Hedgehog 2.

On 13 May 2022, writers Pat Casey and Josh Miller made guest appearances at a comic book convention hosted by Klamath Community College, where they answered questions from fans about the movies they had written for and been involved in. On the topic of Sonic the Hedgehog 3, Casey and Miller were asked on what plotlines they were interested in adapting into it, and they expressed interest in potentially drawing inspiration from Sonic Adventure 2 and Shadow the Hedgehog. In response to a question on Shadow the Hedgehog's planned voice actor for the film, Casey and Miller would state that they intended to have a big movie star voice Shadow, and had a specific person planned for the role, although they had not heard back from him in regards to his acceptance of the role. In response to whenever Amy would be in the film, Casey and Miller said that her inclusion "was in the works". Another question asked whether "Live and Learn" could potentially be used in the climax of Sonic the Hedgehog 3, to which Casey and Miller replied that there was a "decent chance" of such a thing, adding that the producers were aware of fan support for Crush 40 to be featured in the film, and that the band's involvement had been discussed.

In response to a question on the release date of Sonic the Hedgehog 3, Casey and Miller stated they were expecting for it to be released in roughly two years. On 8 August 2022, the film was eventually announced for an official release on 20 December 2024.

On 25 May 2023, Pat Casey confirmed that the writing for the film had been finished at some point prior to the 2023 Writers Guild of America strike, an event which started on 2 May 2023.

Johnny Gioeli, lead singer of Crush 40, has teased a bit about the band's involvement within the movie. It was later revealed by him on 4 February 2024 that Paramount Pictures has secured the legal rights to use "Live and Learn" in the movie.

On 17 April 2024, Idris Elba revealed in an interview with the site Collider that he can tease nothing absolutely nothing and that the film would have Easter eggs "for all of the die-hard fans".Additionally, he said that Jim Carrey's an incredible actor.


## Casting


Shortly after the release of Sonic the Hedgehog 2, Jim Carrey announced that he is considering retirement from acting, but comments that he may return, "if the angels bring some sort of script that's written in gold ink that says to me that it's going to be really important for people to see." In response to a question on Jim Carrey's involvement in Sonic the Hedgehog 3, Patrick Casey and Josh Miller replied that the movie was currently still in its "blue sky" phase of development, where they were considering all the possible ways the movie could be, while trying to find how it should be, and that Carrey's involvement was unknown at the time. Ben Schwartz, the voice actor for Sonic the Hedgehog, had hoped for Carrey to reprise his role in the third film, while producers Neal Moritz and Toby Ascher expressed that they would not recast Dr. Robotnik should Carrey proceed with his retirement plan.

On 2 February 2024, it was revealed that Jim Carrey will reprise his role as Dr. Robotnik. In addition to other reprising actors (Ben Schwartz, Colleen O'Shaughnessey, Idris Elba, James Marsden, Tika Sumpter, Lee Majdoub and Tom Butler), new cast members were also revealed to appear in the film with undisclosed roles: Krysten Ritter, Alyla Browne, James Wolk, Sofia Pernas, Cristo Fernández and Jorma Taccone.

On 16 February 2024, Alyla Browne revealed on her Instagram that she would be portraying Maria Robotnik, which many fans had speculated this prior to the announcement.

On 15 April 2024, it was reported that Keanu Reeves would be voicing Shadow the Hedgehog in the movie, many fans had speculated this prior to the announcement.

## Reception



## Box Office


As of 3 March 2025, has grossed $235.6 million in the United States and Canada, and $250.4 million in other countries the world over, giving a total of $486 million worldwide.

Within a month of the film's release, as of 20 January 2025, Sonic the Hedgehog 3 grossed more than $422 million globally, quickly surpassing its predecessor's.

In the United States, Sonic the Hedgehog 3 released the same day as Mufasa: The Lion King, and alongside Kraven the Hunter. Despite the competition, Sonic the Hedgehog 3 was projected to run $55 million to $60 million at the box office from 3,800 North American theaters, surpassing Mufasa domestically, but not globally. The film managed to earn over over $62 million domestically on its opening weekend, as projected surpassing Mufasa. Sonic the Hedgehog 3 continued to remain the higher grossing film domestically up until the middle of February, but not internationally.

The film reached exactly 3,761 locations on its opening weekend, with it estimated $16,485 per screen. The film was over-indexed in the major markets of NY, Houston, Balitmore, LA, Orlando, Montreal San Antonio, Las Vegas, Detroit, and Fresno; with its #1 location being AMC Burbank. But, it ended up under-indexing in Boston, Toronto, Minneapolis, Denver, and Montreal. Despite the $62 million opening weekend, it was notably shorter than Sonic 2's start of $72 million, the reason speculated to be due to the severe cold weather. 59% of the viewers were male, whereas 41% were female, with 46% of the people attending being in a family (Sonic 2 had 59% families); overall the film had 54% of ticket buyers made up of general audiences. The film's demographics showed 39% of viewers were Caucasian, 34% Hispanic, 17% African American, 5% Asian, and 5% other; being comprised 61% of multi-culture.


## Critical Response


The film received mixed to high praise depending on the outlet. Review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes reported an 86% approval rating based off 138 reviews, becoming the first film in the series to receive the "certified fresh" label. The site's critical consensus reads, "With a double helping of Jim Carrey's antics and a quicksilver pace befitting its hero, Sonic the Hedgehog 3 is the best entry in this amiable series yet." Metacritic gave the film the weighted average score of 56% based on 26 critical reviews, giving it an average score.

Many critics praised the choice to give Jim Carrey double roles. A.A. Dowd of IGN gave the film an average score, but praised Jim Carrey's double roles, "In Sonic the Hedgehog 3, Carrey has finally found a scene partner who can match his freak. It’s… Jim Carrey." Glenn Kenny of The New York Times also praises the film's inclusion of Jim Carrey playing two roles, "It’s hard to settle on what’s more bombastic: Carrey’s admittedly virtuoso double act, or the teeming computer graphics gadgetry of death and destruction spilling out of every corner of the screen. However you choose to categorize this movie, you have to admit it’s a lot — up to and including a post-credits tease for the next picture in the franchise." Christy Lemire of Rogerbert.com gave the film 2 and a half out of 4 stars, "The visual effects are impressively seamless, for the most part, in this live action-animation hybrid. Cinematographer Brandon Trost gives everything from forest chases to dance numbers a colorful sheen, befitting the lively material. (He also recently shot Marielle Heller’s “Nightbitch,” how’s that for range?) And composer Tom Holkenborg, better known as Junkie XL, returns with a score that’s knowingly melodramatic to punctuate all these adventures."

Some critiqued the film's inconsistent tone. Brandon Collins of Medium Popcorn gives the film a 2/5, adding that, "This is a significant step down from the previous two films. I was surprised how uneven the comedy was and how dark in tone the film ended up being." Phil Owen of Gamespot gave the film a 7/10 and criticized the plot, "The plot is messy and doesn't quite add up. For example: One major character, GUN director Rockwell (Krysten Ritter), positions herself as an antagonist but then bows out of the movie halfway through, and it's hard to tell what point there was in including her. But it's easier to overlook potential plot holes like that one this time out because the movie's tone is more controlled than the last one was." Jesse Hassenger of Polygon labelled the film "Sloppy, speedy, great", going on to state, "Looking at it on paper, Sonic 3 depends on a series of ill-advised tonal swings. On one hand, the newly formed team of CG-animated aliens Sonic (Ben Schwartz), Tails (Colleen O’Shaughnessey), and Knuckles (Idris Elba) tees up some more half-assed Saturday-morning-ready lessons about the importance of teamwork when they’re recruited by a shadowy government agency for a world-saving mission. On the other hand, their dangerous opponent is Shadow (Keanu Reeves), essentially a dark version of Sonic, who has recently awakened from a 50-year stasis, ready to channel his grief and rage over the loss of a loved one into the full destruction of planet Earth. Then, on an inexplicable third hand, there is Jim Carrey, returning as the villainous Dr. Robotnik and also debuting Robotnik’s flinty grandfather, Gerald, acting like two maniacs for the price of one."
