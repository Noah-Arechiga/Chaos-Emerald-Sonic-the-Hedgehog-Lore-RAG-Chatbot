Metal Sonic is a robotic doppelgänger of Sonic created by Dr. Eggman,
first appearing in Sonic CD (1993). Unlike most of Eggman's Badnik
creations, Metal Sonic is built specifically to match and, in Eggman's
intent, surpass Sonic's speed and combat ability, making him one of the
series' most direct rivals to Sonic in a purely physical sense.

## Recurring motivations

Metal Sonic is frequently portrayed with a degree of independent will and a
recurring obsession with proving itself superior to the original Sonic,
sometimes acting outside or even against Eggman's direct orders in pursuit
of this goal. This is most explicit in Sonic Heroes, where Metal Sonic
copies the abilities of Sonic's entire team (not just Sonic himself) to
create an enhanced form, Metal Overlord, in a bid to defeat Sonic decisively
and independently of Eggman's schemes.

## Notable forms and appearances

Beyond his standard form, Metal Sonic has appeared in enhanced states across
different games, including Neo Metal Sonic (capable of shapeshifting and
absorbing other robots' data, as seen in Sonic Heroes) and Metal Overlord.
He remains one of the most consistently recurring antagonists/rivals in the
franchise outside of Eggman himself.
