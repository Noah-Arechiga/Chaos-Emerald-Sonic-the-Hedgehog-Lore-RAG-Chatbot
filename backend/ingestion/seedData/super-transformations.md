"Super" forms are transformations granted to certain characters when they
collect all seven Chaos Emeralds simultaneously while holding a sufficient
number of Rings. The best known is Super Sonic, first introduced in Sonic
the Hedgehog 2 (1992), in which Sonic's fur turns golden-yellow, his eyes
turn red, and he gains flight, drastically increased speed, and near
invulnerability to standard attacks (though he can still be harmed by
bottomless pits or crushing in most games).

## Ring drain mechanic

Super forms are typically balanced by continuously draining the character's
collected Rings over time; when Rings run out, the character automatically
reverts to normal. This creates a resource-management element in games where
Super forms are player-usable (as opposed to story-only cutscene
transformations).

## Other characters' Super forms

Besides Sonic, characters shown achieving seven-Emerald Super transformations
in mainline games or spinoffs include Shadow (Super Shadow), Knuckles (Super
Knuckles), Tails (Super Tails, primarily in Sonic 3 & Knuckles/Sonic the
Hedgehog 3), Amy Rose, Silver the Hedgehog, and Sonic's ally-turned-doppelgänger
characters in various later titles. Perfect Chaos, the antagonist's final
form in Sonic Adventure, is not a "Super" transformation in the same
mechanical sense but is likewise fueled by absorbing all seven Emeralds.

## Hyper forms

"Hyper" forms are a rarer, more powerful tier introduced in Sonic 3 &
Knuckles, achieved with the seven Chaos Emeralds plus the seven Super
Emeralds (an upgraded set unique to that game, obtained by combining the
Chaos Emeralds with the Master Emerald's power at special stages). Hyper
Sonic and Hyper Knuckles have expanded abilities beyond their Super
counterparts, such as screen-clearing attacks, but the Super Emeralds and
Hyper forms have rarely reappeared in later mainline titles, making them
something of a one-game curiosity beloved by long-time fans.

## Narrative use vs. gameplay use

In story-heavy contexts (cutscenes, boss fights), Super transformations are
often used as a climax device — the "final push" needed to defeat an
antagonist who has become too powerful through other means (e.g. Chaos
absorbing the Emeralds itself, or Eggman piloting an Emerald-powered
superweapon). This makes the Chaos Emeralds simultaneously a core gameplay
collectible and the series' primary symbol of "ultimate power," fought over
by nearly every major character at some point in the franchise's history.
