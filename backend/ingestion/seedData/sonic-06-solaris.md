Sonic the Hedgehog (2006), commonly called "Sonic '06," centers on Princess
Elise of Soleanna and a being called Solaris, a "god of time" created by
Soleanna's royal family research project. The game is infamous for its
troubled development and bugs, but its plot is often discussed separately as
a notable (if divisive) piece of series lore involving time travel.

## Mephiles and Iblis

During an incident at Soleanna's Flame of Judgment ceremony, the experimental
being Solaris is destabilized and split into two separate entities: Iblis, a
being of destructive fire representing chaos and destruction, and Mephiles,
a being of shadow and manipulation representing hatred. Mephiles is sealed
away in crystal form for roughly 200 years (in-universe) while Iblis is
sealed within Elise, whose tears are established as the only thing capable
of extinguishing Iblis's flame — and, consequently, the only thing that can
prevent Iblis from being fully unleashed.

## Time travel and Silver the Hedgehog

Silver the Hedgehog, introduced in this game, arrives from roughly 200 years
in the future — a future rendered a scorched wasteland by Iblis's unchecked
rampage — attempting to travel back and kill the person he's told is
responsible ("the Iblis Trigger"), initially believing this to be Sonic.
Silver is accompanied by Blaze the Cat, who helps contain Iblis's flame using
her own fire-related powers.

## Mephiles/Iblis reunification as Solaris

The overarching plot across the game's three interwoven character
storylines (Sonic, Shadow, and Silver) converges on preventing Mephiles and
Iblis from reuniting into their original combined form, Solaris — an entity
whose existence across all of time is depicted as having caused the entire
unstable time-loop the characters are trapped in to begin with. The
resolution involves the three hedgehogs' groups combining efforts (with all
three achieving Super forms) to erase Solaris from time itself, which also
erases the tragic events that caused the story's central character death
earlier in the game, resetting the timeline and restoring peace, with most
characters left without memory of the events that occurred.

## Note on canonicity

Sonic '06's story elements, particularly Elise and the specifics of the
Solaris arc, have not been substantially revisited in mainline games since,
and fan and official framing of this story's canonical status has varied;
Silver, Blaze, and Mephiles' broad character concepts have persisted in the
franchise, but the Elise/Solaris storyline specifically is treated by many
fans as a self-contained, rarely-referenced arc.
