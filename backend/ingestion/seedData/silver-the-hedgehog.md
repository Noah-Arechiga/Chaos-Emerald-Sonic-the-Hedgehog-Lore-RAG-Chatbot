Silver the Hedgehog is a white/silver hedgehog with psychokinetic (telekinetic)
powers, introduced in Sonic the Hedgehog (2006) as a visitor from roughly
200 years in the future. Unlike Sonic, Shadow, or most other cast members,
Silver's primary combat style relies on lifting and throwing objects and
enemies using psychic energy rather than direct speed-based attacks,
though he retains hedgehog-standard running and jumping abilities.

## Origin and motivation

In his debut, Silver hails from a devastated future ravaged by the fire
being Iblis, and travels back in time seeking to kill the person responsible
for that future ("the Iblis Trigger"), initially and mistakenly believing
this to be Sonic. He's accompanied for much of the story by Blaze the Cat,
a similarly time/dimension-displaced character with fire-related powers who
helps contain Iblis.

## Recurring role

Following his introduction, Silver has reappeared across multiple
games (Sonic Rivals, Sonic Generations, Sonic Forces, Sonic Frontiers among
others) generally as a well-intentioned but sometimes naively idealistic
ally, often involved in plots relating to time travel, alternate futures, or
dimensional travel, reflecting his original introduction's themes.
