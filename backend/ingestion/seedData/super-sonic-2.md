Super Sonic 2 (スパソニ2, Supa Soni Tsū?), also known as Starfall Super Sonic, is a power-up that appears in the Sonic the Hedgehog series. It is the advanced Super State of Super Sonic, achieved by completing Master King Koco's trial to temper his cyber corruption into newfound power. After The End possessed the defeated Supreme, Sonic empowered his Super form even further into this state in order to more effectively fight it. After using it in this one occasion, Sonic lost the power-up permanently after using the full extent of his cyber corruption as Super Sonic Cyber, in order to destroy The End's physical form.


## Appearance


When Sonic uses his cyber corruption to empower Super Sonic into Super Sonic 2, Super Sonic remains largely physically unchanged, but his golden aura is accompanied by a secondary crimson aura, his body gains a crimson outline, and his red eyes change to cyan. The golden sparkles emanating from Sonic's body are accompanied by blue and black sparkles, red wisps, and general sparks of energy.


## History



## Sonic Frontiers


In Sonic Frontiers, Super Sonic 2 is the advanced state of Super Sonic that Sonic achieved by harnessing and partially mastering the cyber corruption within his body. It only appears in The Final Horizon.

During his battle against the Titan Supreme, possessed by The End, Sonic empowered further beyond his Super State, with cyberspace energy, to become Super Sonic 2 in order to easily inflict more damage to it. After sending it to the air, via his attacks, Dr. Eggman told Sonic to go into Supreme's repurposed rifle for a final attack. Inside the rifle, Sonic had no choice but to fully utilize all his cyber corruption to achieve the full potential of the form.

After using it to annihilate The End for good, Sonic sustained battle damage and immediately reverted back to Super Sonic, left unconscious and drifting in the Earth's orbit, covered in bruises and scrapes.


## Power and abilities


In how much the successor to Sonic's transformation into Super Sonic, Super Sonic 2 possesses drastically increased power and speed. Whereas Super Sonic was unable to inflict significant damage to Supreme when corrupted by The End, Super Sonic 2 is able to damage and overwhelm the empowered Titan with ease. Most importantly, Super Sonic 2 gains the ability to Perfect Parry any attack, including cyber corruption attacks that Super Sonic is normally unable to deflect, with a simple wave of his hand.

Super Sonic 2 is able to perform stronger versions of his attacks, such as using Sonic Boom against The End's avatar and being able to forcefully launch its rifle out of its socket (and later dozens of feet into the air) with a single Cyloop. Super Sonic 2 also is able to move insanely fast, capable of relentlessly attacking The End multiple times near-instantaneously, in a manner akin to the Light Speed Attack.


## Weaknesses


As with its previous state, Super Sonic 2 uses tremendous amounts of energy and is maintained by Ring Energy. Upon running out of Rings, Sonic will fully revert back to normal. Despite being able to Perfect Parry cyber corruption attacks, his maximum Ring count will still decrease if he fails to do so. It is also possible for Sonic to be forced out of this form by extraordinary external force, such as attempting to deflect The End's largest cyber corruption attack.
