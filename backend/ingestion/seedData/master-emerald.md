The Master Emerald is a large, green, gemstone-like artifact distinct from
(though related to) the seven Chaos Emeralds. It is most closely associated
with Angel Island, a landmass that floats in the sky because of the Master
Emerald's power, and with its hereditary guardian, Knuckles the Echidna,
whose ancestors were tasked with protecting it.

## Relationship to the Chaos Emeralds

Unlike the seven Chaos Emeralds, which are usually depicted as roughly
fist-sized, the Master Emerald is much larger — closer to the size of a
boulder or small monument. Its primary established role is to regulate and
stabilize the power of the Chaos Emeralds: when the Master Emerald is intact
and properly placed (traditionally on its altar on Angel Island), the Chaos
Emeralds behave more predictably. When it is removed, shattered, or its
power disrupted, the Chaos Emeralds can go inert, lose power, or behave
erratically.

The Master Emerald is generally not shown granting Super transformations the
way the seven Chaos Emeralds do; instead it functions as a stabilizer,
amplifier, and, in some stories, a containment mechanism for other dangerous
forces (such as the creature Chaos).

## Shattering and restoration

Several games' plots involve the Master Emerald being shattered into pieces
(sometimes by Eggman's manipulation, sometimes by Knuckles being tricked into
breaking it himself), sending Knuckles on a quest to recover the shards and
restore it. A shattered or destabilized Master Emerald is frequently the
inciting event that allows an antagonist — most notably the creature Chaos —
to grow more powerful, since without the Master Emerald's regulating
influence, ambient chaos energy runs wild.

## Guardianship

The role of Master Emerald guardian is hereditary among the Knuckles Clan /
Echidna tribe, passed down for generations. Knuckles takes this duty
extremely seriously, which is a recurring source of both his gruff,
suspicious personality toward outsiders (including Sonic, initially) and of
plots where he's manipulated by a villain (classically Eggman) into
believing Sonic's group is trying to steal the Emerald, when in fact they're
usually trying to help stop a bigger threat.
