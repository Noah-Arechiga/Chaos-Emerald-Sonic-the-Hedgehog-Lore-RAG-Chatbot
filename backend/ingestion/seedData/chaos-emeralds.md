The Chaos Emeralds are a set of seven gemstones that appear throughout the
Sonic the Hedgehog series as the setting's primary source of near-limitless
power. Each Emerald is a different color — commonly depicted as red, orange,
yellow, green, cyan, blue, and purple/violet across various games and media —
though exact color assignments have varied between titles.

The Emeralds are usually described as having been formed on the Floating
Island (or Angel Island, depending on the game) alongside the larger Master
Emerald, which acts as a stabilizing counterpart to the seven smaller stones.
When separated from the Master Emerald's influence, the Chaos Emeralds can
destabilize and cause chaotic energy surges; when gathered together, they
resonate and amplify each other's power dramatically.

## Powers granted

Individually, a Chaos Emerald can power small technological devices or grant
minor boosts. When all seven are gathered by a compatible being, they enable
a "Super" transformation — most famously Super Sonic, in which Sonic's fur
turns gold/yellow, his eyes turn red, and he gains flight, near-invulnerability,
and vastly increased speed and strength. Other characters shown to achieve
similar seven-Emerald transformations include Shadow, Knuckles, Tails, Silver,
and Amy in various games.

Using all seven Emeralds for a Super transformation typically requires the
user to be holding a sufficient number of Rings, which the transformation
then drains over time; running out of Rings reverts the transformation.

## Chaos Control and Chaos Blast

Characters attuned to the Emeralds — notably Shadow the Hedgehog and Sonic —
can use a single Emerald (or sometimes just their innate connection to Chaos
energy) to perform "Chaos Control," a technique that warps space-time to
slow, stop, or skip through time, or teleport across short or long distances.
"Chaos Blast" is a related but distinct technique, an area-of-effect energy
explosion used primarily by Shadow and, in some appearances, by the creature
Chaos itself.

## Recurring role in plots

The Emeralds are the object of pursuit for antagonists across the series,
most often Dr. Eggman, who seeks them to power his machines (such as the
Death Egg or Eclipse Cannon) or to unlock ancient, dangerous forces. Multiple
games' plots revolve around a race between Sonic's group and a villain to
collect all seven Emeralds first, or around preventing a villain who already
has them from completing a transformation or activating a superweapon.

Because the Emeralds are tied to "Chaos energy," a broader in-universe force
associated with life, emotion, and destruction, they are frequently linked
narratively to the entity called Chaos and to the ancient Echidna civilization
that once worshipped and safeguarded them on Angel Island.
