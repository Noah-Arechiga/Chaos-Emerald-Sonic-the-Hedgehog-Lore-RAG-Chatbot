The Knuckles Clan (also called the Echidna Tribe) is an ancient civilization
of echidnas established, in Sonic Adventure's backstory, as the original
guardians of the Chaos Emeralds and the Master Emerald, living on the
Floating Island (later called Angel Island) alongside a race of small,
gentle creatures called the Chao.

## Tikal and Pachacamac

The two most significant named members of the ancient clan are Tikal, a
young priestess devoted to peace with the Chao, and her father Pachacamac,
the clan's chief, who leads a military campaign against the Chao's sanctuary
to seize the Chaos Emeralds' power for the tribe. This attack is what
provokes the guardian creature Chaos into its destructive Perfect Chaos
transformation, and it is Tikal who ultimately seals Chaos within the Master
Emerald, sacrificing her own freedom (her spirit remains bound within the
Emerald for millennia) to stop the devastation.

## Knuckles's lineage

Knuckles the Echidna, introduced in Sonic the Hedgehog 3, is established as
the last known member of this once-larger civilization still living on
Angel Island, and inherits the role of Master Emerald guardian as a
generations-long family duty. Later games (such as Sonic Adventure) reveal
more of this deep backstory, explaining why Knuckles takes threats to the
Master Emerald so seriously and why he's so easily manipulated by anyone
(especially Eggman) claiming Sonic's group intends to steal it — protecting
it isn't just a personal responsibility to Knuckles, it's the last surviving
duty of his people.

## Angel Island

Angel Island itself is depicted as a landmass that floats via the Master
Emerald's power, a physical legacy of the ancient civilization's connection
to Chaos energy. Its isolation is part of why Knuckles, prior to meeting
Sonic and friends, has limited contact with the outside world and why so
much of his people's history had been forgotten or mythologized even within
the story's present day, until Sonic Adventure's flashbacks reveal it
directly to the player (and, narratively, to Knuckles himself).
