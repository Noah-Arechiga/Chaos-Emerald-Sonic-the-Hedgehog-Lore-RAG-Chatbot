Knuckles the Echidna is a red echidna introduced in Sonic the Hedgehog 3
(1994), initially as an antagonist manipulated by Dr. Eggman into believing
Sonic and Tails intend to steal the Master Emerald, before becoming a
recurring ally. He is characterized by immense physical strength, the
ability to glide and climb walls, and a serious, sometimes gullible,
hot-tempered personality shaped by his isolated upbringing as the Master
Emerald's sole guardian.

## Guardianship and gullibility

Knuckles's defining character trait across the series is his devotion to
guarding the Master Emerald, a hereditary duty passed down through his
echidna lineage. This devotion is also a recurring narrative device: because
Knuckles is isolated on Angel Island and has limited outside contact, he's
repeatedly shown being tricked by antagonists (most often Eggman, but also
other characters like Rouge the Bat) into distrusting Sonic's group or
handing over pieces of the Emerald, a running joke acknowledged even within
the games themselves.

## Abilities

Knuckles's signature abilities include gliding long distances, climbing
vertical surfaces, and delivering powerful punches capable of breaking
through terrain that other characters can't. Like Sonic and Shadow, he can
achieve a Super Knuckles transformation with all seven Chaos Emeralds, and a
Hyper Knuckles transformation in Sonic 3 & Knuckles specifically with the
Super Emeralds.
