Sage (セージ, Sēji?) is a character that appears in the Sonic the Hedgehog series. She is a humanoid artificial intelligence program created by Dr. Eggman, whom he used in an attempt to harness the technology of the Ancients during the Starfall Islands incident. Calm, analytical, and observant, Sage evaluates threats and simulates countermeasures with remarkable precision in order to fulfill her primary directive: protect the doctor at all costs. She is known as the doctor's "daughter" and has full access to the Eggnet and is set to be the future heir of the Eggman Empire.


## Concept and creation


Sage has been described by Takashi Iizuka to be one of the many mysterious elements in Sonic Frontiers. With so much of the concept of the game being focused upon Sonic unearthing the mysteries of the Starfall Islands and the fate of his friends, "Sage is kind of one of these other elements of... 'This mysterious character that I don't know... why is this character the way this character is? What this character is doing, or why the character wants Sonic to leave and get out of there?' [...] But all these mysterious things the team wanted to put into the design of the character, and that's why you kind of see Sage as this mysterious young girl who has these abilities or these things that she can do that we don't know that much about."

According to Ian Flynn, he didn't know what Sage looked like when writing the script of Sonic Frontiers.


## Appearance


Sage resembles a young human girl. She possesses a slim body and limbs with white skin and a large, round head with a tall forehead. Notably, she possesses glowing red eyes and a red energy structure on the left side of her face. She also has short white hair with a swirl in it on her forehead and a single red energy highlight inside said swirl. The rest of her body however, especially her arms and legs, are completely black and seem to have outlines of red computer code around them. For attire, she wears a black cloak-like dress and black Mary Jane shoes. However, when Sage becomes sympathetic or emotional, her design inverts to a white cloak and shoes with cyan wireframing and eyes.

In The Final Horizon, she remains in her white and blue design, now with her arms and legs white, like her skin, after The End was destroyed.


## History



## Past


At some point prior to the Starfall Islands incident, Dr. Eggman formulated his latest plan to harness the technology and resources of the Ancients found on the islands. To that end, he created his most advanced artificial intelligence, intended for the purpose to protect him and access the technology for him. Like many creations that came before, Eggman gave the newly made AI a name: “Sage”.


## Sonic Frontiers Prologue


While the doctor traveled to the Starfall Islands, Sage was stationed to watch over his network, such as informing him on the status of the supplies heading to the islands and the delay regarding Orbot and Cubot's report of D3-COY's destruction. Eggman became dissatisfied with the two robots and mumbled to himself he wish one of his creations could fill him with joy instead of disappointment.

Once Dr. Eggman arrived on Kronos Island, Sage downloaded herself to the Egg Device by his command, warning the doctor that her integration systems were untested, and advising him not to use them. Eggman, however, brushed off these precautions, and upon locating a Portal to Cyber Space, attached Sage's device to it, hoping she would be able to hijack the ancient systems through it.

However, Sage's integration with Cyber Space caused her coding to be rewritten by the advanced technology, changing her greatly. Her abilities were significantly increased, and Sage's personality became more lifelike. In addition, Sage swiftly learned a massive amount of information and assumed a certain level of control over the islands' robots, but also immediately detected The End, a danger of cosmic proportions trapped within Cyber Space. With new power and information at her disposal, Sage ran millions of simulations, and determined that the only way Eggman could survive The End's inevitable escape would be if he was contained within Cyber Space at the time. Making an executive decision without asking for approval from her creator, Sage enacted protective measures, absorbing a confused Eggman into the database. In addition, Sage also drew the Chaos Emeralds to the Starfall Islands in order to restore the functionality of the ancient's technology and prepare for the looming threat.

Sometime after Dr. Eggman's invasion of the Starfall Islands, Knuckles the Echidna found an old portal in the ruins of Sky Sanctuary and accidentally arrived at the islands. Knuckles fought and defeated the Soldiers that attacked him, until Sage herself intervened by summoning an Asura. After the Asura defeated Knuckles, he found himself inside a metallic prison on Ares Island.


## Sonic Frontiers


Sage kept watch over the Doctor in Cyber Space, shielding him from phantom G.U.N. helicopters and updating him on Sonic’s progress across the islands, but struggled to devise a safe way to extract him from the digital database.

Despite being irked about Sage trapping him in Cyber Space, Eggman was comfortably confident in his brilliance behind her coding and could only grumble. Though progress was slow, Sage continued to relay information about Sonic's actions and his battles to the doctor in a way Eggman's creations never had before. Gradually, her control over Cyber Space and the efficiency behind her actions impressed her creator. After one report of Sonic in a fight, Eggman bellowed in laughter. Joyfully, he informed Sage that he had plans to give her control over the Eggnet once the situation was resolved. Sage was taken by surprise by this, and was intrigued further when Eggman mentioned Orbot and Cubot. She rethought her existing relationships and asked if the duo could be considered "brothers" to her, to which Eggman agreed with uncertainty. Sage's face glowed at the thought of meeting them.

On the islands themselves, Sage confronted Sonic the Hedgehog multiple times, claiming that his actions were both futile and dangerous, but refusing to elaborate when Sonic would inquire about her or her knowledge. While initially cold and combative towards him, seeing Sonic's varied interactions with Amy, Knuckles and Tails taught Sage much about interpersonal relationships, causing her to cry upon realizing that the hedgehog had fostered a family born of love and not genetics, something she had desired with her own "father." Additionally, Sonic's courage in the face of the Titans and his own corruption consuming him on Rhea Island as a result of The End's machinations endeared him towards her, as she finally grew to understand the blue blur's heroic heart.

Upon the completion of Sonic's corruption, Eggman, Amy, Knuckles, and Tails were freed from Cyber Space, seemingly at the cost of Sonic's soul. Sage warned Eggman to return to Cyber Space, and claimed that even the Egg Fleet would not be enough to defeat The End, with no probable scenario of survival. However, Sonic's friends sacrificed their freedom to return him to normal, causing Sage to excitedly posit the possibility of herself and Eggman working together with Sonic, a scenario which Eggman forbade her from simulating. Eggman, faced with no choice, begrudgingly and temporarily inducted Sonic into the Eggman Empire to form an alliance in which Sonic and Sage would split off from Eggman and work together to navigate Ouranos Island and gather the Chaos Emeralds.

Upon gathering the Emeralds, Sonic became Super Sonic and subdued the most powerful titan, Supreme. As it was defeated, Sage noted that The End was fleeing into space to regain its true form and insisted that she and Sonic pursue it. Eggman acquiesced to this, but with knowledge of the danger, warned his "dear daughter" to be careful as she possessed the body of Supreme and with Super Sonic, fought against the deific being.

After enduring its onslaught of attacks, Super Sonic flew through the moon-like being, dealing a critical blow. However, it began to charge a final, powerful blast of its destructive laser. Sage, seeing this, quickly acted, and destroyed The End at the cost of her existence, using her final words to ask Sonic to look after her father.

Eggman himself, upon realizing that Sage would not return to Earth, dropped to his knee in grief, before reaching a hand out to the falling debris and clenching his fist.

An unknown amount of time later, Dr. Eggman was typing away fervently at a computer, until the monitor displayed a mysterious symbol and Sage's voice asked aloud, "Father?" to which Eggman responded, "That's my girl."

During The Final Horizon, after Sonic arrives on Ouranos Island, Eggman stops Sonic before he could begin exploring the island, so that Sage is able to tell Sonic of a scenario that she didn't even consider as a viable option, due to the unlikeliness of it succeeding. She tells Sonic that she could reverse his friend's efforts while also stabilizing Sonic's cyber corruption for a brief period of time, suggesting to him that they could collect the Chaos Emeralds for him while he faces an even greater challenge that would be able to convert his cyber corruption into a useable power and save her father. Sonic agrees to do it, provided it will also save the world, and Sage assures him that, since the world is his to conquer, it will be saved.

Sage uses her powers to release Amy, Knuckles, and Tails, who are at first concerned, but were quickly calmed by Sonic. They part from Sonic and Sage, who begin to climb the four Towers of the Masters and confront the Master Koco, the spirits of those who piloted the Titans. Upon his completion of the four towers, Sage directs him to the one who can convert his cyber corruption into a useable power, at the top of the final tower. Upon arrival, she is barred entry to the last trial by the Master King.

After Sonic completes the the Master King's trial, he meets back up with Sage, who pleased at his success. She informs him that Sonic's friends aren't handling their own cyber corruption well. They meet up with the others, and Sonic receives six of the Chaos Emeralds from his friends, and the seventh from Dr. Eggman.

Sage watches as Super Sonic engages Supreme in battle, and when the Titan is taken over by The End, she joins the battle to protect Sonic and Dr. Eggman from an assault by The End. Although she struggles to maintain the barrier at first, she is supported by Amy, Knuckles, and Tails. Sonic then enters the Titan's rifle, and destroys The End for good.

Afterwards, Eggman and Sage watch a meteor shower together, excited to go home together.

## Personality


Sage is cold and calculating, initially making decisions solely considering projections and logical analysis. She believes that the results of her calculations are absolute and cannot be defied. She holds disdain for Sonic and friends as reckless and irrational beings, preoccupied with notions like love, and argues that they are out of depth and better off giving up.

Her highest priority is ensuring the safety of Dr. Eggman. As she grows closer to Eggman, she gains interest in having a family, seeing him as her father and his creations as her brothers. She also grows impressed by Sonic's defiance of her predictions, and saddened by the thought that his bravery will only lead to ruin. When The End appears, Sage grows frantic to protect Eggman, and is excited as he agrees to collaborate with Sonic, increasing Eggman's chances of survival. She eventually sacrifices herself to stop The End, showing the love she developed for her creator.

Despite developing a begrudging admiration for Sonic and experiencing love for herself, Sage remains beholden to her logical ways and is a staunch supporter of the Eggman Empire's campaign for world domination. She emphasizes to Sonic that their alliance on the Starfall Islands is only to protect the world so that Dr. Eggman may rule it. She belittles those who defy Eggman and his plans, engaging in understated but brutal trash talk with other racers during Dodon Pa's CrossWorld Grand Prix. She is wholly subservient to her creator and just as devoted to taking over the world as he is.


## Powers and abilities


Utilizing abilities while uploaded in Cyber Space, Sage can act as an observer over the Starfall Islands.

Sage is incredibly intelligent. Her processing power can calculate all known factors under 1,700,050 simulations, likely even more. Sage had already run millions of calculations regarding Eggman’s chances of victory against The End, yet even then, every scenario she considered was one where Sonic never assisted them. What Sage does know; All of Dr. Eggman’s accumulated historical knowledge from the beginning of his campaign up to the events of his expedition to the Starfall Islands, which previously had her share sour opinions of Sonic the Hedgehog with her creator, and the Ancients' data, consisting of their very essence. This would include all knowledge the Ancients had on The End.[citation needed]Due to having full access to the EggNet, Sage can utilize its network infrastructure to upload, update, and receive data in real time from Eggman’s servers.

As a being made out of data and thus having no matter, Sage's form is incorporeal, similar to a ghost. This form of existence, along with Cyber Space, has benefited her movements to consist of levitating in midair, phasing through walls, and casually teleporting in the area. While she is mostly shown levitating and teleporting, she isn't incapable of basic flight as she flew away from the Egg Mobile to possess Supreme.

Sage can also manipulate the machines on the Starfall Islands. In addition, she has the ability to project a laser beam out of her hands after gathering energy, along with having a degree of control while in Cyber Space. She also can create shield-like constructs to shield herself and others from attacks, as seen in the final battle with Supreme.

Following her departure from Starfall Islands, Sage's abilities and potential appear to have become greatly limited, although she still retains her full intelligence, she is now required to use hologram and hard light constructs to show herself, her ability to control technology and being able to create shield-like constructs are more limited. However she still retains her flight and teleportation abilities.

As shown during Dodon Pa's CrossWorld Grand Prix, Sage is also an expert at driving cars like the Giganto Liner.


## Color Powers


By harnessing different variants of Hyper-go-on from Wisps, Sage can use specific Color Powers to transform into a certain form, such as a drill, a laser or even a planet, each one possessing its own unique abilities. So far, Sage has been able to transform into the Cyan Laser, Yellow Drill, and Indigo Asteroid. However, these transformations require a steady supply of Hyper-go-on to be maintained.


## Weaknesses


While she is an artificial intelligence and appears to be a hologram, she is in fact a hard light construct, which makes her vulnerable to being damaged.


## Relationships



## Dr. Eggman


When Dr. Eggman had first created Sage, Eggman had seen Sage as nothing more than another pawn for his plans. Even then, Eggman appeared to have a lot of faith in his creation, believing that it would reign supreme all of his other creations, all of which proved to be complete failures. Sage's various exchanges with Eggman, as well as protecting him from the threats found in Cyber Space would begin the creation of a father-daughter-like bond between the two of them. Sage seemed to regard Eggman with a daughter-like respect and concern early on, as she mentioned that keeping Eggman safe was her top priority after Eggman told her to make her top priority finding a way to get him out of Cyber Space. She also looked forward to going home with Eggman to meet Orbot and Cubot, whom she seemed to consider brothers.


## Metal Sonic


The relationship between Sage and Metal Sonic seemingly entails some degree of brother and sister bond, though they have not been shown to interact much at this time.

During the Mirage Express incident, Sage is seen sharing "Let's go, dad!" shirts with Metal Sonic to support Dr. Eggman, seemingly viewing him as a brother. Indeed, promotional material identifies them both as part of the same family. In Sonic Channel art, Sage gifts Metal Sonic his own stocking for Christmas.

However, her perspective of Metal displays several sides during the CrossWorld Grand Prix, where she can be wary and cautious around him, believing he is confrontational and potentially rebellious, much like he was as Neo Metal Sonic in the past. As such, she hopes to maintain his loyalty coding to the Eggman Empire. Despite this caution, she will kindly apologize if she hits him during a race, suggesting a lingering sense of affection.


## Sonic the Hedgehog


Giving that she's a creation of Eggman, despite Sonic the Hedgehog's heroic actions and personality, Sage views him as a threat not just to the Starfall Islands, but the world as a whole. As such, she repeatedly threatens him to leave the Islands through means of violence despite him simply wanting to rescue his friends. She eventually warms up to him, especially after witnessing the lengths he will go to save his friends. She also had some degree of respect for his abilities even before then, as she suggested to Eggman that forming an alliance with Sonic may be Eggman's best option for escaping Cyber Space due to them solving past crises together.

However, at a later point Sonic and Sage had to team up to save Metal Sonic and Sage admits that she respects Sonic's skills to overcome any obstacle but she still sees him as an enemy.


## Knuckles the Echidna


Very little is actually known about what Sage thinks of Knuckles the Echidna, but she does appear to act antagonistic towards him in the same way she was towards Sonic, as evidenced in the Divergence prologue. Knuckles in return sees Sage as an enemy, and decides to attack her when she tries to attack Sonic with a laser. When Sonic tries to prevent him from doing this, Knuckles sees this as an indication that he is too trusting.


## Miles "Tails" Prower


Sage respects Miles "Tails" Prower and sometimes addresses him by his true name, Miles Prower. Although she questions him for his years of being best friends with Sonic, Sonic is a constant help and light source to any situation and always saves his friends using his determination and optimism. Tails acts as he doesn't need to have faith for Sonic, but know Sonic is smart enough to overcome evils to rescue him or his friends when captured. Sage doesn't hate Tails or see him as a threat, but she's very secretive and doesn't tell him anything he should know.


## Shadow the Hedgehog


During the CrossWorld Grand Prix, Sage mentions how they are both creations of the Robotnik lineage, and ask if that they could be considered family relatives. Shadow the Hedgehog responds by stating he has left his past behind and lives only for himself, advising her to do the same. Another interaction between them is more confrontational; Shadow dismisses her as an unworthy challenge, while Sage, confident in her own design, retorts by labelling him a "relic of fifty years ago."


## Belle the Tinkerer


Sage first meets Belle the Tinkerer when Eggman asks her to join Belle and Sonic in finding Metal Sonic. At the beginning, their relationship was quite hostile: Sage only considers her important for finding Metal Sonic, even coldly asking her to leave Motobud during Metal Sonic's attack, while Belle is jealous that Sage is the daughter that Eggman truly cares about and speaks to her in a very sarcastic manner. However, when they tried to protect themselves from Corrupted Metal Sonic, their relationship began to improve: After seeing Sage's distress over the hopelessness of the situation, Belle calms her down and asks her to help her create a blaster out of the wreckage, to which Sage agrees, and they work together to accomplish this task.

Later, when Belle connects to the program to communicate with her and find her, she notices that she considers her to be her sister, which she is interested in. After Metal Sonic was freed from the mysterious villain's control and the villain himself escaped, Sage suggested that she remove the program, but she refused because she wanted to be able to talk to her, to which Sage gently agreed.


## Team Chaotix


Sage comes across Team Chaotix during their investigation of the missing Astroscopic Lens when they came to Eggman's abandoned base. Believing that they came to stop Eggman, Sage immediately sends an Egg Hammer after them. However, after learning their true reason for coming, she helped them by finding the file they had come for. Still, she didn't tell them about Eggman's approach because she wanted to see their shocked faces.
