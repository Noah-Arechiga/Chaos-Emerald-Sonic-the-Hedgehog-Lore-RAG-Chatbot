Professor Gerald Robotnik (プロフェッサー・ジェラルド・ロボトニック, Professā Jerarudo Robotonikku?), referred to in short as Prof. Gerald (Ｐｒｏｆ．プロフェッサージェラルド, Purofessā Jerarudo?), is a character that appears in the Sonic the Hedgehog series. He was a human scientist and the grandfather of Maria and Ivo Robotnik. A world-renowned scientist who is widely acknowledged as one of the greatest scientific minds of all time, Gerald was responsible for several ground-breaking creations, including his greatest achievement, Shadow the Hedgehog, making him a modern day scientific Da Vinci.

Over fifty years ago, Gerald was asked by the President of the United Federation to lead Project Shadow, a medical program for inventing immortality, which he accepted in hopes of finding a cure for a fatal illness afflicting Maria. Eventually, Gerald managed to create Shadow the Hedgehog, though not without drastic measures; he was taken into custody by the military when his work was deemed too dangerous. When learning that Maria had been killed by the military, Gerald slipped into madness and spent his last days plotting revenge against humanity.


## Appearance


Gerald Robotnik bore a resemblance to his grandson. He was skinnier and had more normal body proportions, a bald and slightly pointier head, a long nose, and average-sized ears. Like Eggman, he also had an oversized moustache, except his was whitish-gray and more ragged. He was also not very tall of stature, only being about half a head taller than Maria. His eyes were never seen.

Gerald's general attire consisted of a turtleneck sweater, and a white lab coat with black buttons. He also wore blue pince-nez glasses, much like his grandson's. In Sonic Adventure 2 and Sonic Adventure 2: Battle, he was shown wearing white pants, and a gray turtleneck sweater, while in Sonic X Shadow Generations, Gerald's sweater was dark brown and was also seen wearing brown pants, and light brown shoes.

In Shadow the Hedgehog, Gerald's design was more similar to Ivo. He had broad and elevated shoulders, a round torso (though not as wide as Ivo), slightly over-proportioned arms and legs, and a more voluminous and smooth mustache. His bald head was also dome-shaped. He was seen wearing dark brown pants and blue slippers, and his glasses had a distinct ripple-like pattern on the lenses.


## History



## Past


It is implied that Gerald came from a long line of brilliant scientists. Having an interest in robotics and archaeology, Gerald believed his purpose in life was to bring the people of the world happiness and hope through science, and became a scientist to help the world by dedicating his life's work to the people of Earth. Gerald also had two sons, one of whom studied robotics and went on to father Ivo Robotnik, while the other studied archaeology and fathered Maria Robotnik, whom Gerald came to cherish dearly.

During his career, Gerald accomplished many great things. Upon deciphering an ancient ring found at the Mystic Ruins, Gerald discovered Angel Island and visited it, though he felt the place was too sacred to defile with experiments and departed with newfound knowledge of the Master Emerald.

Soon afterwards, he helped to design the world's first space colony: the Space Colony ARK. After completing the ARK, Gerald began living and working there with other scientists, eventually obtaining a Chaos Emerald and bringing it aboard. However, he remained skeptical of G.U.N., who contributed funding to the ARK and urged him to devote his genius towards weapons development. Gerald's research on the Ifrit likely occurred around this time, which he wrote several files about; though the exact context of this work is a mystery, he appears to have been in a state of distress at the time.


## The Gizoid


In a warehouse one day, Gerald discovered the Gizoid, which had been previously excavated by his archaeologist son. Gerald accidentally activated it while working with Chaos Emeralds, and began to study it himself. However, he quickly learned that the Gizoid held great power and that it destroyed the Fourth Great Civilization long ago. Theorizing the Gizoid's full potential should it be fully restored, Gerald realized he had uncovered a horrible weapon.

As Gerald continued to research the Gizoid, he discovered that the Gizoid would swear loyalty to those with great power by establishing a "Link" with them. To keep the Gizoid under his control and from being used for evil, Gerald successfully made a Link with it by demonstrating his model weapon collection to it. However, in fear of someone with evil intentions forming a Link with the Gizoid, Gerald researched how to re-establish a Link with the Gizoid should it be lost.


## Project Shadow


Gerald's granddaughter Maria was eventually diagnosed with NIDS. Gerald made the controversial decision to bring her aboard the ARK, so he could care for her personally and give her constant medical attention, with the low-gravity environment helping manage her symptoms. This choice may have led to his strained relationship with his sons.

Over fifty years prior to the Black Arms incident, Gerald received a request from the then-President of the United Federation to help with "Project Shadow", a medical program meant to capture mankind's ultimate dream: immortality. Initially, Gerald stubbornly refused this request, believing they were crossing the limits of man and that this research could ultimately be used for war. However, upon realizing that the immortality research could also develop a cure for Maria's illness, Gerald accepted the President's request and led the project on the ARK in secrecy. At the same time, G.U.N. continued to urge Gerald to develop weapons, leading him to use previous research on Chaos to invent Artificial Chaos to satiate them.

Initially, Gerald's fellow researchers doubted Project Shadow, even with his brilliant mind on their side. In the first stages of the project, Gerald researched the Chaos Emeralds and invented Chaos Drives in an attempt to apply Chaos Emerald energy to living beings. When those tests proved successful, Gerald and his colleagues moved on to creating an immortal creature: the "ultimate life form". They eventually succeeded in producing a prototype, dubbed the Biolizard. While it lived up to many of their expectations, the creature proved too flawed as it grew larger. In addition to a bestial and unruly nature, weak limbs and reliance on machinery for survival, Gerald noticed the Biolizard grew too fast and requested that the control part on his vital organs was reinforced, storing it within Cannon's Core.

As Project Shadow stalled, Gerald grew desperate. When the Black Comet made its fifty-year orbit by the Earth, Black Doom, the leader of the Black Arms, took notice of Gerald's work and reached out to make a deal: Black Doom would provide his immortal, Chaos energy-compatible DNA to Project Shadow, and in return, the final product would deliver the seven Chaos Emeralds to him in fifty years when the Black Comet returned. Gerald understood the danger of dealing with the marauding alien, so he secretly plotted against Black Doom by developing the Eclipse Cannon within the ARK, a weapon capable of destroying the Black Comet upon its return.

Using the DNA of Black Doom, Gerald created a different model of of the Biolizard, who gained the name Shadow the Hedgehog from Maria. Unwilling to weaponize Shadow though, Gerald gave him a "heart" and "soul" similar to Maria's in hopes that he would not become a tool of mass-destruction. He also secretly changed Shadow to be the savior of mankind by helping to stop the Black Arms upon their return, believing that only someone with their power could stop them. Shadow proved to be everything Gerald had hoped for, possessing both immortality, high intelligence, his own identity, and independence. In addition, Shadow became very close to the residents of the ARK, especially Gerald's granddaughter, Maria.

As Shadow neared completion, Gerald learned the government had become uneasy about the outcome of Project Shadow following his contact with the Black Arms. With Gerald's invention beginning to be perceived as a potential danger to humanity, the government considered freezing the project's funding and shutting down the ARK. Unwilling to let his studies get ceased now that he was so close to finding a cure for Maria, Gerald made a big risk and offered the Gizoid as a bribe to the government to buy his research more time. While Gerald ordered the Gizoid not to absorb dangerous technologies, other researchers tried to form a new Link with the Gizoid by demonstrating great power. Eventually, Gerald's fears were confirmed when the Gizoid went on a rampage that destroyed most of the ARK, even taking Gerald hostage before it was subdued with Shadow's help. Deeming the Gizoid too dangerous, Gerald tried to destroy its core but failed to circumvent its advanced technology. Instead, he planted a code-based "heart" and "soul" based on Maria's in the Gizoid that would grant it free will and emotions. Gerald hoped that these qualities would prevent the Gizoid from becoming a weapon of mass-destruction. In case the Gizoid went out of control, however, Gerald implanted a final program in it that would prompt it to self-destruct should that time come.

Upon suspecting that the government planned to cease his research after deeming Shadow a threat to mankind, Gerald prepared a contingency video for Shadow. In it, Gerald discussed the government's plans to close Project Shadow, while also explaining how Shadow was meant to stop the Black Arms when the Black Comet returned in fifty years. During the time Shadow was raised, Gerald unintentionally had an accident with his Artificial Chaos which caused them to run loose. Gerald himself dictated this mistake to his old age. Fortunately, Shadow and G.U.N. ensured the situation was brought under control, though the damage was done.


## Imprisonment


Gerald's suspicions were confirmed when G.U.N. attacked the Space Colony ARK around the time he had almost completed Shadow to eliminate everything and everyone related to the project and shut the ARK down. When GUN's Special Forces rushed into the ARK's laboratory, Gerald entrusted Shadow to Maria and left them to flee in the ARK's escape pods. Eventually, Gerald assumed that Maria and Shadow had escaped to safety.

Gerald was soon after captured under the guise of disappearing under mysterious circumstances and taken to Prison Island where he was forced to continue his experiments under military control. Meanwhile, when it was released to the public that the ARK was shut down due to an accident, Gerald was labeled as the cause of it. When the military later found Shadow, who got placed in suspended animation, and brought him to Prison Island for Gerald to continue his research, Gerald saw Maria among the list of casualties when the ARK was shut down and was grief-stricken. Unable to cope with the loss of his granddaughter, Gerald began to lose control of his mind as he could only think of avenging Maria and ending everything, something which terrified him. As a result, he would unwillingly scribble calculations he made for a doomsday plan in his cell. However, during the period where he could still control his mind, Gerald got a hold of the Gizoid known as Emerl again and used this opportunity to make Maria's dying words the keywords that would activate the Gizoid's free will once all seven Chaos Emeralds were inserted into it.

In the end, Gerald lost his grip on reality and went insane, devoting himself to avenging Maria by destroying humanity. While it was thought that he was continuing his research with Shadow, Gerald secretly created a program that would cause the Space Colony ARK to fall to Earth and destroy the planet once the seven Chaos Emeralds were placed into the Eclipse Cannon, and set the Biolizard up as well to ensure this. Gerald also biologically implanted his own will for revenge into Shadow, altering his memories of what Maria wanted of him as her final wish so that he would carry out his plan once he was awoken. Gerald then left a note behind in his diary that encouraged the reader to release Shadow if they wanted to cause global destruction. Soon after, Gerald was incarcerated on Prison Island by the military when they began to fear of how he could retaliate. Just before he was executed, Gerald's last action was leaving behind a recording where he condemned the ungrateful world to destruction for the fate of Maria by giving his estimate for how long it would take for the ARK to collide with Earth.


## Legacy


Because of the tragic deaths Gerald's research indirectly caused and the potential dangers to humanity his discoveries posed, many would view Gerald with contempt. Commander Abraham Tower himself came to consider Gerald insane and held a life-long grudge against him for creating Shadow, which indirectly led to the death of Maria. Many of Gerald's late creations, research and, discoveries which would form his legacy were either sealed or placed under tight security due to their potential for calamity. Gerald's Chaos Drives continued to be used by G.U.N. as power sources for their machines. Prior to his execution, he also conducted several mathematical equations within his prison cell (implied to be calculations regarding how long it will take between ARK's deorbiting and impact of Earth), which would later be found by Amy Rose shortly after the latter freed Sonic.

Despite Gerald's bad reputation, Gerald's grandson Dr. Eggman looked up to him and admired him for the great things he accomplished. This inspired him to become a scientist, though Eggman would use his intellect to try achieving world domination. Gerald's forbidden research would also be uncovered over time by his descendants, namely Eggman and Eggman Nega, who would use it for their own nefarious purposes. It is implied that the reason that Dr. Eggman has developed notions of world domination was because he saw Gerald’s death as a great injustice, and was embittered by his demise wanting his legacy to be noticed.

In Sonic Adventure 2, Shadow was awakened by Dr. Eggman after the latter found his grandfather's diary. After awakening, Shadow unknowingly carried out Gerald's plan to destroy the world, believing that he was fulfilling Maria's false wish for revenge. Shadow subsequently deceived Eggman into activating Gerald's program that put the Space Colony ARK on a collision course with the Earth, causing a recording of Gerald condemning the world to destruction in his final moments to be played across the world. However, Shadow would eventually remember the real promise he made to Maria, which made him seek to stop the ARK's collision with the planet. Together with Sonic the Hedgehog, they stopped the ARK and foiled Gerald's revenge plot. Eggman, in turn, began to doubt his grandfather, seeing as he wished to destroy the world in the end.

In Sonic Battle, Dr. Eggman recovered the same Gizoid Gerald found, thus putting the world in danger. In an effort to keep the Gizoid from becoming a weapon of mass-destruction, Shadow and Rouge discovered from Gerald's notes that they could reset the Gizoid and turn it into an ordinary robot with the program Gerald had inserted into it if they gave it the seven Chaos Emeralds and the keywords "Bring hope to humanity." After the duo accomplished this though, Eggman managed to lure the Gizoid to the Death Egg. However, his attempt to make it forge a Link with him backfired when it caused the Gizoid to attempt to destroy the Earth with the Final Egg Blaster. In the end, he was stopped by Sonic. Since the Gizoid had grown too powerful as well, it enacted Gerald's final program, which was making itself self-destruct.

In Shadow the Hedgehog, the Black Arms returned to invade the Earth and collect the Chaos Emeralds Gerald had promised them. Shadow, who was suffering from amnesia following the Space Colony ARK incident, agreed to help Black Doom collect the Chaos Emeralds in hopes that the alien would reveal the secrets of his past to him, which threw a wrench into Gerald's original plans. However, when Black Doom was on the cusp of total victory, Team Chaotix managed to activate one of Gerald's recordings onboard the Space Colony ARK, which was broadcasted world-wide for everyone to see, including the President, Abraham Tower, Black Doom and Shadow. In the video, Gerald explained the freezing of Project Shadow, his plan to destroy the Black Comet with the Eclipse Cannon, and that Shadow was the only one who could defeat the Black Arms. This recording gave Shadow the resolve to stand against Black Doom and ultimately destroy him. After the Black Comet was destroyed with the Eclipse Cannon, the President and the Commander decided to pay homage to Gerald by working towards a brighter future. On the other hand, Dr. Eggman realized that his grandfather was more brilliant that he initially saw.

In Sonic Rivals 2, his descendant Eggman Nega discovered the Gerald's notes about the Ifrit, which he then sought to release upon the world from the creatures' home dimension. However, his attempt was foiled by various heroes, villains, and anti-heroes.

At some point after Shadow's creation, Gerald makes his only living appearance in Shadow Generations. Due to the time displacement caused by his grandson, Doctor Eggman with the Time Eater's power, both he and Maria were brought into White Space and transported to the present, where the latter is attacked by Black Warriors. To their surprise, the present-day Shadow from the future rescues Maria near Sunset Heights.

Gerald is quickly able to tell that Shadow had changed much from their time, but the pair are ordered to find a safe place to hide from Black Doom's minions while Shadow would continue to pursue their master. To avoid creating a time paradox, Gerald also refuses to listen to any reference towards their future. Following this, the pair remain in the hub world to speak with Shadow as he progresses on his journey.

As Shadow continued to accumulate more Doom Powers, the group notices that the Black Moon grew with his power; Gerald explained how Shadow, as an extension of Black Doom, was strengthening both of them. Gerald recounts how he was tempted by a cure for Maria's disease, and helped manufacture Shadow to be Black Doom's perfect warrior with the latter's DNA. He warns Shadow thusly that he would lose control and either be a vessel to Black Doom, or have his mind controlled as his warrior. In response, Shadow assures the professor that he would take these powers for himself and would not be controlled.

After defeating Mephiles, the Black Moon would grow once again, causing Black Doom to arrive, boasting that he had learned from their "previous" encounter. Gerald believes that Black Doom had used the temporal anomaly to grow a new body in White Space to take revenge. As Shadow's rage increased, he is calmed by Maria, who reminds him to remain true to the light of his own heart.

Once the Black Moon is complete, members of the Black Arms emerge as Black Doom finally challenges Shadow for control of his body. Though saddened that Shadow would have to fix his mistakes, Gerald remained proud, and he and Maria would see Shadow off for his final battle against Devil Doom. Following Shadow's final victory, the time anomaly would begin to heal; parts of the world fading away to return to their respective times.

Realizing this, Shadow arrives to find both Gerald and Maria disappearing as well. Although Shadow wishes to use Chaos Control to stop the pair in time to prevent them from fading away, they both assure him that they did not wish to be trapped in time, and that his friends still needed him. Shedding tears, Shadow accepted their decision as both Gerald and Maria bade him farewell.

## Personality


Before his descent into madness, Gerald was a good man and a profound humanitarian, the very much opposite of his psychotic grandson Eggman. He was devoted to using his intellect to help mankind and bring them happiness and hope through the power of science, which he believed was the reason for his existence. While he was much more serious about his work than Eggman, Gerald remained a caring person towards his family. As old age began to catch up to him, though, Gerald would become more forgetful. While far more benevolent than his grandson, he was not perfect. Gerald held a considerable amount of pride in his genius, at times even lapsing into vanity and hubris, though he was somewhat aware of this and usually tried to act with humility. He would knowingly make a number of risky deals with people and organizations that he did not morally agree with or that intended to exploit his abilities for the sake of his research, always rationalizing that through his genius he would somehow manipulate the situation to avoid consequences down the line. He also had a fondness for puns, as evident by the passages in his journal.

Gerald was a man of strong ethical standards, refusing to use his genius for the wrong reasons. He initially refused to lead Project: Shadow since he thought obtaining immortality was against nature and because he had no interest in aiding with war. That said, he was not above breaking his ethics for certain reasons, like when being forced by the higher-ups or when he thought it was for the greater good, such as joining Project Shadow or handing over the dangerous Emerl just to find a cure for his dying granddaughter. Even when in such situations, however, Gerald went out of his way to make sure his research would have the potential for good as well, like how he gave both Shadow and Emerl genuine feelings and planned to share Shadow with the world to help mankind, as well as wanting to use the data borne from the Artificial Chaos development to develop search-and-rescue units. He also held the strong belief that power born from the desire to protect rather than the desire to conquer would make all conflicts cease to exist. Also, although Gerald was willing to make a deal with Black Doom in order to complete Project Shadow, upon realizing the alien's ulterior motives of conquering Earth, Gerald set to retaliate against his benefactor despite promising to give the seven Chaos Emeralds to him, showing that Gerald is willing to undermine a deal for the sake of protecting Earth and humanity.

When Maria was killed, Gerald went through a profound change, as he was consumed by grief and slowly went insane. He eventually became a rage-filled psychopath, developing a deep hatred for humanity who he blamed for Maria's death, and grew obsessed with revenge by committing global genocide. To this end, he turned Shadow and the Eclipse Cannon — both of which he originally conceived to defend Earth from the Black Arms — into weapons of mass destruction, completely in contrast of his original humanitarian and peaceful ideals. He showed at least some awareness of his change in character, expressing fear at how he was losing control of his thoughts in his diary entry. Even in his last moments, all Gerald had to express was his contempt for humanity, and he died a broken man. Prior to Maria's death, he may have also come close to losing his sanity when encountering Ifrit, due to his description of it resembling a rant.


## Powers and abilities


Having presumably inherited a tremendous genius from his family of brilliant scientists, Gerald was by far the most knowledgeable and gifted scientist of all time, boasting an incredible intellect, even when compared to present-day geniuses. As a testament to his intelligence, he was regarded by his grandson, Dr. Eggman himself, who possesses an IQ of 300, as the greatest scientific mind in history.

Gerald was an evident polymath, having mastered several areas of study, including architecture, astrology and foremost biology, along with genetics to astrophysics to robotics. He was the one who designed the world's first Bernal sphere space colony, the ARK, and developed incredible devices and machinery such as the Eclipse Cannon, which has the power to destroy planets and pierce stars, the Chaos Drives, which could contain the Chaos Emeralds' mystical energies, the Artificial Chaos units, the Heal Units, and the Bomb Units. He was also behind the creation of incredible creatures such as the Biolizard and Shadow the Hedgehog (the latter of which used alien DNA), both of whom are powerful, nearly unstoppable and capable of harnessing the power of the Chaos Emeralds. Additionally, he was a skilled archaeologist as demonstrated in his research of the Gizoid and the Fourth Great Civilization, where he uncovered many of their secrets from ancient stone tablets and papers.


## Relationships



## Maria Robotnik


Maria Robotnik was Gerald's granddaughter and undoubtedly the most important person in his life. Gerald was extremely fond of Maria and loved her dearly. Overall, she meant everything to him and was the jewel of his eye. In addition, Gerald also made Maria's first name the password for many of his secret files and devices in return for Maria naming Shadow the Hedgehog, indicating that she was very prominent to Gerald.

After Maria was diagnosed with Neuro-Immuno Deficiency Syndrome, Gerald's main priority was her well-being, and nearly all his late research was dedicated to saving her life. When Maria's condition was doubted by his fellow researchers, Gerald found that all of his reason and restraint vanished because of this insult towards her. Because of Maria, Gerald would break his own ethical standards and take enormous risks to find her a cure, such as leading Project Shadow to develop a cure despite finding the research unethical, handing over Emerl to the government to get more time to find a cure for her despite Emerl posing an imminent danger, and contacting Black Doom for help. He also held great trust in her, as he entrusted Shadow to her when the military attacked.

When the ARK was shut down, Gerald's only concern was Maria's safety. When he later learned that Maria had been killed, Gerald lost all reason to live and felt responsible for her demise since his research led to the terrible accident that killed her. Eventually, he went mad with grief. Gerald would then devote the remainder of his life to avenge Maria by wiping out humanity, whom he blamed for his loss.


## Shadow the Hedgehog


Shadow the Hedgehog is Gerald's greatest achievement of his scientific career, an immortal and intelligent being. Gerald was the one who created Shadow, but while the United Federation wanted to use Shadow for their own desires, Gerald planned for him to provide a way to develop a cure for the fatal sickness afflicting his granddaughter, Maria. However, Gerald wished not for Shadow to become the government's weapon and so gave him a "soul" similar to Maria's so he could choose not to fight and even help cease conflict.

While Shadow was created to help him save Maria, Gerald cared a great deal about Shadow as an individual and considered him his own son. Gerald also held great faith in him as he entrusted him to correct his past mistakes by defeating the Black Arms and wholeheartedly hoped he could help mankind. When Gerald later went mad with grief over losing Maria, however, Gerald used Shadow as his tool to get revenge at humanity, going as far as to alter his memories to ensure he would carry out his plans.


## Black Doom


Desperate to save Maria, Gerald made a "Faustian deal" with Black Doom. While Black Doom saw Gerald as a means to create a herald for his invasion, Gerald secretly planned to betray him, intending to use Shadow to destroy the Black Comet instead with the Eclipse Cannon. Gerald later called their contact a "terrible mistake," and Black Doom dismissed Gerald as a "fool" upon discovering his plans.

Their relationship was purely pragmatic, built on mutual exploitation rather than trust. Neither had any intention of honoring their pact, and both ultimately failed to achieve their goals through Shadow. Black Doom dismissed the "unassuming form" Gerald gave Shadow, insisting that his true destructive nature came from Black Arms blood, not Gerald's design.. Gerald's final act of programming the ARK to crash into Earth, could be interpreted as one last attempt to thwart Black Doom, ensuring that even in death, his plans would be for nothing.

During the Time Eater incident, Black Doom's influence brought Gerald and Maria into White Space. Doom sent his spawn to kill them, but Shadow intervened. After Shadow defeated Black Doom, Gerald and Maria returned to their own time.


## Family


Gerald took great pride in his sons' educational achievements, as one inherited his passion for archaeology and the other for robotics. However, a rift grew between them, with Gerald lamenting that his love for his sons "shouldn't be a divisive issue." He later admitted they were "very bright young men, just... not on my level," and that they "don't talk much."

Gerald became the grandfather to Maria Robotnik, whom he described as his true legacy. When Maria was diagnosed with a terminal illness, Gerald had her brought aboard the ARK for treatment, a decision her family was "less than enthusiastic" about. Over time, he became estranged from Maria's parents, referring to them only as "Maria's parents" rather than "my son and his wife" in his journal.

It is unknown if Gerald knew of his grandson's existence, Doctor Eggman, as his journal never acknowledges him, only his sons and grandaughters, but he was transported to the White Space in part because of him and he never knew the harm his descendants do using the genius they got from him.

By the end of his life, mad with grief over the loss of his beloved granddaughter, Gerald programmed the ARK to crash into Earth, killing the "ungrateful humans who took everything away from him," including the rest of his living family.

## In other media



## Animation


In the Sonic X anime series and its comic series published by Archie Comics, Gerald Robotnik, much like in the games, was a human from Earth and the grandfather of Maria Robotnik and Dr. Eggman.

Like in the games, Gerald was a scientific genius who worked on Project Shadow. Eventually, Gerald's research led to the creation of Shadow the Hedgehog who became close to Maria, but G.U.N. arrived sometime after to forcefully shut down the project when it was deemed too dangerous. Gerald himself was incarcerated on Prison Island where he learned that Maria had been killed by the military. Consumed by thoughts of revenge, Gerald created a program that would destroy the Earth with the Space Colony ARK before he was executed.


## Books and comics


In the Sonic the Hedgehog comic series and its spin-offs published by Archie Comics, Gerald Robotnik was an Overlander, the maternal grandfather of Dr. Robotnik, and a member of the House of Ivo. Much like in the games, Gerald worked on Project: Shadow in hopes of finding a cure for the Neuro-Immuno Deficiency Syndrome afflicting his granddaughter, Maria Robotnik. Gerald created Shadow the Hedgehog but was captured by G.U.N. and forced to work for them. When Gerald learned that the military had killed Maria, he secretly made a program that would destroy Mobius as revenge before he was executed. A year after his awakening, Shadow encountered a digital version of Gerald who helped him uncover his purpose.

Following the events of Worlds Collide, Gerald became virtually indistinguishable from his game counterpart.

Gerald Robotnik is a minor, unnammed character in the The Jet Black Hedgehog: Shadow the Hedgehog manga. He is the grandfather of Maria and the creator of Shadow the Hedgehog.

Fifty years prior to the manga's events, Gerald made a deal with Black Doom: in exchange for providing his cells to be used in Shadow's creation, Black Doom would return for Shadow fifty years later for his conquering schemes. Gerald's research was destroyed during the raid on the Space Colony ARK.

Unlike his mainline counterpart in Shadow Generations, Gerald is not present during Shadow's adventure in White Space.


## Films


In the Sonic the Hedgehog film series produced by Paramount Pictures, Gerald Robotnik, much like in the games, was a human, and worked on Project Shadow, and the grandfather of Maria Robotnik and Dr. Ivo Robotnik. However, instead of searching the cure for Neuro-Immune Deficiency Syndrome, as Maria wasn't diagnosed with it, he was experimenting with Shadow's Chaos energy. G.U.N. later then raided the lab to retrieve Shadow, however, ending up causing an explosion that killed Maria. Because of his refusal to follow orders, Gerald was imprisoned for 50 years. In contrast to the games, Gerald wasn't executed in jail, but was rather released by making a deal with the government to create the Eclipse Cannon, only to use it by himself, planning to destroy the Earth. It's also implied that Gerald lived as long as he did due to occasionally licking on Shadow's quills to extend his life. Gerald was eventually killed by his own grandson after a murder attempt on the latter (after Eggman decided against blowing up the Earth due to viewing it as too extreme even for him).


## Voice


When the then 37-40 year old Mike Pollock took over the role of Gerald Robotnik for both Sonic X and Shadow the Hedgehog, he voiced Gerald with a high-pitched, stereotypical old man voice.

However, his performance took a significant departure by the time of Shadow Generations. At 59 years old, Pollock voiced Gerald using his more natural sounding voice, making Gerald sound similar to Pollock's interpretation of Doctor Eggman, but in a more subdued, natural manner.
