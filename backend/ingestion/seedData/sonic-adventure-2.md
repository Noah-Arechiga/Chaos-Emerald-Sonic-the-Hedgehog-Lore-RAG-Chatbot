Sonic Adventure 2 (2001) structures its story as two competing campaigns —
"Hero" and "Dark" — that run in parallel before converging in a shared "Last
Story."

## Hero story

Sonic is framed for a bank robbery by an impostor who turns out to be
Shadow, and is pursued by GUN (a global military organization) throughout
much of the game. Sonic, Tails, and Knuckles work independently and
eventually together to stop Dr. Eggman, who has discovered his grandfather
Gerald Robotnik's old research and is trying to use it, and to prevent
Eggman/Shadow's plan involving the space colony ARK's orbital superweapon,
the Eclipse Cannon (later renamed the "Space Colony ARK" cannon, colloquially
similar to earlier games' "Eclipse Cannon" concept), which threatens Earth.

## Dark story

Dr. Eggman breaks into GUN's high-security prison to free Shadow, whom he
believes can help him achieve his grandfather's unfinished goals. Eggman and
Shadow (with Rouge the Bat, a GUN spy/treasure hunter, alternately helping
and pursuing her own agenda) search for the Chaos Emeralds and investigate
Gerald Robotnik's research on the ARK, uncovering the truth of Gerald's
history and Project Shadow.

## Gerald Robotnik's revenge plot

The story reveals that after Maria Robotnik's death during GUN's raid on the
ARK, Gerald Robotnik — devastated and enraged — secretly programmed the ARK's
Eclipse Cannon and a self-destruct/"Eclipse" protocol as a posthumous act of
revenge against humanity, intending for the cannon to devastate Earth and,
failing that, for the ARK itself to collide with the planet. Gerald had
altered Shadow's own memories/programming so that Shadow would believe he
was fulfilling Maria's wish by carrying out this plan, when in fact Gerald
had twisted Maria's real last request (for Shadow to protect humanity) into
its opposite.

## Climax

In the Last Story, Sonic and Shadow set aside their rivalry to work together,
using Chaos Control (which requires both to use Chaos Emeralds
simultaneously) to teleport the falling ARK away from Earth at the last
moment, saving the planet. The game is a major turning point for Shadow's
characterization, transforming him from a straightforward antagonist into a
recurring anti-hero across later titles.
