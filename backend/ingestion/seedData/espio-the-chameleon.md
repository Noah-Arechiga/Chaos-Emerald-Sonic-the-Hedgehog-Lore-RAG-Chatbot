Espio the Chameleon (エスピオ・ザ・カメレオン, Esupio za Kamereon?) is a character that appears in the Sonic the Hedgehog series. He is an anthropomorphic chameleon, who mainly serves as an intelligent ninja warrior poet and a member of the Chaotix Detective Agency alongside Vector the Crocodile and Charmy Bee. Together, the trio solves whatever cases they are given, where Espio is able to put his skills to good use. He also possesses the ability to blend in with his environment as well.

Espio is a calm and quiet chameleon, but also overly cautious and disciplined. However, his wariness and soulful character makes him a vital member of Team Chaotix, where his presence plays an important role as a restraint among his coworkers, who tend to be optimistic and energetic. Espio likewise thrives on the dangers of his missions, and although he is quite confident in his ninjutsu, he feels it is aesthetically pleasing to stay in the shadows as a ninja should.


## Appearance


Espio is a fuchsia-skinned anthropomorphic chameleon with a yellow horn between his gold eyes. His muzzle and the heart-shaped pattern on his torso are peach in color. He has a ridge of three black spikes going down his spine and a coiled tail. For attire, he wears purple shoes with black accents, metal-reinforced arm bracers, and white gloves with purple pads with white trims on the backs. Around his wrists and ankles are black bandage-wrapped cuffs with gold studs on the lower bands.

During his early years, up until the Neo Metal Sonic incident, Espio was shorter and stubbier. His purple skin color was also somewhat darker, and he had black eyes. He wore simple white gloves and green shoes with black toes, yellow cuffs, and gray soles, each with two black belt straps held by silver buckles just above the ankles. In addition, his back spikes were teal/green in color and his tail was not coiled and was shorter.


## Personality


A single-minded, straight-laced detective committed to his job, Espio is serious, practical, intelligent and wise. He has a militaristic discipline despite being quiet, calm, and laid-back. Espio is additionally portrayed as being stubborn, opinionated and self-obsessed. Despite this, he has an excellent drive to all that he does and flourishes off of peril, getting himself into threatening situations to flex and polish his ninja skills, and, due to his extensive training, he can confront these troubles head-on unconcerned by danger.

Among his colleagues, Espio's opinionated attitude serves a key role in Team Chaotix, allowing him to reign in and balance out the more optimistic and zany behaviors exhibited by Vector and Charmy. Despite his more serious demeanour compared to his colleagues, he can be prone to his own quirks, such as being conflicted on the outcome of a race in order to have some of Vanilla's victory cake, or being intrigued by Blaze's offer to come and work in the Sol Empire purely down to the idea of the pay. He also appears to find small creatures, like Flickies, adorable. It is said Espio's unique charm comes from a combination of his cute and charming - yet cool and stoic appearance. Espio's ability to be swept in his teamamates antics despite this coolness can be described as "adorable".

Beneath Espio's reserved demeanor lies a burning and very strong sense of justice and an absolute refusal to let evil have its way. As such, he gives the impression of a rather ruthless demeanor when facing villains; when encountering Dr. Eggman during the Neo Metal Sonic incident and the Black Arms incident, the usual civil and soft-spoken chameleon would make threats at the doctor's life. This is also exemplified in how he believes that one should not be forgiven for the bad deeds they have already committed, even if the perpetrator does not remember anything and is unable to answer for them. Espio is also seen as a ninja-based character; his battle poses and shuriken stars support that portrayal. Also in the Japanese script, the pronouns he uses to refer to himself are usually jibun, and the more archaic onushi pronoun for referring to others.

Like any ninja, Espio has a soulful side to himself too. He is a lover of traditional ways and can play the shamisen but is left confused by computers and audio-video equipment. He is also fluent in 17 different languages. Additionally, it has been shown he has an affinity for poetry and calligraphy. He has a keen sense for attention to detail.

He is likewise a wise, intelligent, and soulful character, and has an excellent drive to all that he does, always being very thorough with his work. While having a no-nonsense attitude, he also has a bit of an ego which makes him elaborate on stories about his work that go nowhere in order to make his listeners understand how hard he has been working. He is also reluctant to admit his shortcomings.

While Espio appears to have a great deal of confidence and outward ego in his abilities as a stealth expert and ninja, he places his worth in his ninja skills and can become disappointed and angry with himself when he feels that these skills are either stagnating or do not meet the incredibly high expectations he places on himself. This can lead to him feeling like a burden or making him question his ability to be a ninja in the first place. Such instances often have him desire to return to training instantly in an attempt to eliminate these shortcomings. Having others point out his flaws leaves him defensive of his abilities and character.


## Powers and abilities


Using speed and stealth to overcome his enemies, Espio is a powerful adversary (by his own accords), as he says it would take no less than a "mighty foe" to outclass him. He is likewise a fierce foe in direct combat, as he has been able to face skilled opponents like Rouge the Bat and Knuckles the Echidna on even ground during the Ifrit incident.

As a Speed Type, Espio possesses a great deal of speed. As demonstrated during the Ifrit incident, he can run at speeds compatible to Sonic and Shadow's, and his swiftness is potent enough to let him fool his enemies and appear in different spots nigh-instantaneously. Also, while having not demonstrated power on a superhuman level, Espio has impressive physical strength too, enough to shatter obstacles and smash Badniks using only hand chops. Espio also has very impressive durability and endurance, having been able to withstand Sonic's Homing Attacks indefinitely and keep on fighting during their duel amidst the Time Eater incident. Espio also has extreme acrobatic skills, being capable of doing complex and nimble jumps even in midair, leap between walls with ease, pull off Spin Attack techniques at high speed, and grind on Grind Rails without any trouble.

Espio possesses a unique stealth camouflage trick that lets him change the hue of his skin to match his environment. His camouflage is so effective that he is rendered completely invisible to the naked eye, capable of passing through photocells with great efficiency, and able to pass by foes unnoticed. Combined with his keen mind, this ability makes Espio a very sneaky and effective spy. He additionally possesses the skill to lock-pick.

Having undergone extensive ninja training, Espio is a skilled ninja and a master of ninjutsu. With his skills in the latter field having been described as "powerful", he is proficient with several forms of ninja-based weaponry. He is especially a master of throwing kunai, capable of striking targets with pinpoint accuracy while only barely looking at them. His far-range accuracy is so great that Espio notes himself to be able to hit a bottle cap with a throwing spike from 50 feet away, and is able use a kunai to hit a switch 30 feet away through treacherous winds - a feat noted to need near "impossible" expertise to pull off. He can also expertly throw shuriken stars at enemies for offensive purposes. He is also capable of stealth, which combined with his camouflaging abilities makes him virtually undetectable. Espio's signature moves are likewise ninjutsu moves that utilize his camouflage trick. The Leaf Swirl lets Espio combine his camouflage with a Ninja Leaf Swirl maneuver to create a tornado to blow opponents away while he disappears, his Chroma Camo maneuver hides himself completely from his enemies' perception while increasing his speed, and his Ninja Arts: Camouflage hides himself completely from enemies' perception while he "warps" between places to disorient his enemy as he attacks them. As noted by Sage.

Espio also possesses an extremely keen sense of awareness of his surroundings on both the physical and empathic plane, detecting Team Dark nearby despite the group being out of his field of vision, Sonic's presence aboard the Death Egg, and the soul of the Time Eater.

Like a real lizard, Espio can climb along any surface and stick to them for an unprecedented amount of time. As seen during the Death Egg II incident, he also possesses a prehensile tongue which he can use as a short-range projectile attack in combat and as a grappling hook.

As seen during Dodon Pa's CrossWorld Grand Prix, Espio also has excellent skills when it comes to driving and racing in race cars like the Stealth Chaser. Or driving Vector's vehicle the Beat Monster.

Espio is also able to perform the Spin Attack, a technique where he curls into a concussive ball or cutting disk and directs himself at his targets. With it, Espio can shred or burrow through just about any substance given enough speed. Adept in its usage, Espio can utilize several variants of the Spin Attack for both ground- and aerial maneuvers, including the Spin Dash, Spin Jump, and Homing Attack.

Espio has a keen mind and experience in investigation and gathering intel. However, while having experience in solving mysteries alone, he is not as brilliant a detective as Vector. He also has some limited skill in hacking computers as seen during the Black Arms incident. Among other things, Espio can also harness the power of the Chaos Emeralds to empower himself.


## Weapons


As a ninja, Espio favors and employs ninja-based weaponry, such as Kunai and shuriken stars. He uses his shuriken stars as a long-range offensive by throwing them at opponents and can do so in rapid succession. By attaching a rope to his kunai, he can also use it as a grappling hook to latch onto suspended targets and swing between them, make deadly turns, or use it as a makeshift barbed wire. Additionally, he carries pellet-sized smoke bombs that he uses to produce large smoke clouds for confusing opponents or making quick escapes.


## Transformations


Espio is capable of using this mode for a brief time on his own by drawing power from the Chaos Emeralds. In Hyper Mode, Espio's speed and attack power greatly increases.

By harnessing different variants of Hyper-go-on from Wisps, Espio can use specific Color Powers to transform into a certain form, such as a drill, a laser or even a planet, each one possessing its own unique abilities. So far, Espio has been able to transform into the Cyan Laser, Yellow Drill, and Indigo Asteroid. However, these transformations require a steady supply of Hyper-go-on to be maintained.


## Weaknesses


Espio's camouflaging ability, while practical, is not perfect. Its maintenance is tied to Espio's concentration, meaning it will falter should he lose focus due to things such as being blinded by light, sneezing, coughing, or being covered in a visible substance. His stealth is not flawless either as Rouge the Bat was able to detect his movements thanks to her heightened sense of hearing. As demonstrated by Zazz, he can also be sniffed out, even while he is invisible, and as later demonstrated by Clutch, one can still hear the noise he makes in the environment. He is also not mechanically-minded.


## Relationships



## Vector the Crocodile


As a vital member of the Chaotix Detective Agency, Espio respects Vector the Crocodile as a colleague and boss, as well as a close friend, with their bond being close to something "familial". Espio's ninja abilities often come in handy for ongoing investigations and Vector even trusts Espio to handle cases on his own. However, Espio can, at times, not bother as much with payment as Vector does, occasionally leading to conflicts between the two, like during the Ifrit incident, where the chameleon failed to collect evidence to present to their client. Coupled with Vector's free-of-charge charity, this usually leaves the detectives in debt. Espio acts as the voice of reason among the group and may advise Vector against accepting certain jobs, like following their mysterious client's orders during the Neo Metal Sonic incident but was unsuccessful due to Vector's readiness to take any work that pays. Generally, Espio is very loyal to his boss and always follows his orders. This loyalty is noted by others, such as Vanilla the Rabbit, who state that Espio, along with Charmy, would do anything for Vector. Espio holds a deep respect for Vector's detective skills and is more than happy to compliment him on such a fact.

As the calmest and most serious member of Team Chaotix, Espio sometimes goes unnoticed between his louder colleagues. During the Wisp incident, Vector wondered about Espio's whereabouts, and neither he nor Charmy, Sonic and Tails realized that Espio had been standing next to them. Espio has also displayed some frustration with Vector's childish side, such as during the Time Eater incident, where the crocodile gobbled up three chili dogs at once at Sonic's birthday party while the chameleon shook his head in embarrassment.

Though Espio can be sometimes seen as the voice of reason compared to Vector, he still follows his lead on missions and trusts him during his more serious plans. Vector equally sees Espio as a trusted ally, and knows that he can depend on him to get the job done. For example, Vector decided to stay behind with the Zombots in Restoration HQ so Espio could escape and appointed him as the new lead detective of the Chaotix, while Espio could only look on in silence, tearing up over his friend. Eventually, with the combined efforts of Super Sonic and Super Silver, they were able to warp the entirety of the Metal Virus away from the world and to the sun. This act turned Vector and Charmy back to normal, allowing Espio to joyfully reunite with and sing with them.


## Charmy Bee


Espio finds Charmy Bee to be a trusted ally and good friend of his as a member of the Chaotix. Due to his more resolute and serious personality, he does not find Charmy's antics very amusing, though the young bee does not mind this at all. Due to Charmy's young age, Espio has found himself explaining various intricate situations that Charmy does not yet understand due to his young age, and trying to protect him more than quite a few times. He is very protective of Charmy and cares for him a lot as a friend. He also respects him well enough as a fellow detective.

Espio and Charmy have what can be described as a "brother-like" relationship. Charmy has been shown to often tease Espio, such as in the CrossWorld Grand Prix, where Charmy taunts Espio that if he wins, he'll overtake him as the coolest Chaotix member, causing Espio to demand him to tell what the ranking is. Despite Espio's mature demenour, Charmy can bring out a somewhat "childish" and petty side out of Espio, such as the chameleon holding a grudge over Charmy breaking his Super Ninja action figure and refusing to help carry him due to this during the "Case of the Red Specter". Despite this, Espio respects Charmy's quick thinking and considers him a valued member of the team. Charmy, in return, is often amazed by Espio's ninja feats, finding them to be awesome. Espio is quick to console Charmy when he makes mishaps.


## Knuckles the Echidna


When first meeting Knuckles the Echidna, he is envious of him because of his own desire to see the Angel Island. Nevertheless, the two get along as allies during the Chaos Ring incident that took place, and they continue being allies for many years to come, such as working as part of the Resistance together during the War to Take Back the Planet. Espio would even help Knuckles retrieve a relic that was stolen by the Babylon Rogues and shows respect towards the echidna but at times does not admire his sense of pride at times.


## Rouge the Bat


Espio and Rouge the Bat first meet during the Neo Metal Sonic incident where the pair initally are hostile due to Espio believing that Rouge was an adversary of their client. While, from then on, Rouge is an ally, Espio views her as an untrustworthy and sneaky individual. He even would at times call her out on her sneaky behavior as seen when him and Rouge teamed up to sneak into a Clean Sweep Inc. facility in Rainstorm Forest. However, Espio secretly respects Rouge's stealthy abilities but this "pains him to say it", though he has yet to admit it to Rouge herself. Espio even refers to her as a tricky and devious bat who is not very trustworthy but are still allies who help each other on occasion. Espio even expresses relief at her being safe from the storm Infinite created and in turn, Rouge will often save him from the line of fire, should the need arise.

While Rouge likes to poke fun at the idea of Espio being a ninja due to the extent of her own stealth abilities, Espio can frustrate her in return, such as when he refuses to help her reach a control platform under the impression it was just a feat to make off with the Astroscopic Lenses. At the same time, Espio frequently fails to pick up on Rouge's flirtatious advances towards him, much to her frustration. Espio, on the other hand, finds her confusing and struggles to understand her fascination with breaking him away from his team.


## Shadow the Hedgehog


The relationship between Espio and Shadow the Hedgehog can be defined as one of professional acquaintances. While Espio respects Shadow's abilities, Shadow remains largely indifferent toward the chameleon.

During the events of Neo Metal Sonic incident, Team Chaotix and Team Dark initially clashed after mistaking each other for enemies. However, they eventually put their differences aside and joined forces to defeat the common threat of Neo Metal Sonic. Their paths crossed again during the Black Arms invasion. While Shadow paid little direct attention to the Chaotix Crew, not once directly talking to them, their alliance proved pragmatically useful when he helped them access Eggman's database. Throughout their mission in the cyberspace, Espio maintained a professional demeanor. When Eggman attempted to manipulate the amnesiac Shadow by claiming he had no past, Espio urged Shadow to ignore the doctor and focus on the mission.

The two have appeared indirectly during several major events, including the Time Eater incident, the war against Eggman, and the return of Neo Metal Sonic, though they rarely interacted directly.

Their most notable interaction occurred during the CrossWorld Grand Prix, where Espio expressed respect for Shadow's abilities, while Shadow dismissed him, claiming not to barely know who he was.


## Silver the Hedgehog


When Espio first met Silver the Hedgehog, his mission was to find evidence that Silver was behind the kidnapping of several Chao. As such, Silver was a subject of doubt for Espio as he did not seem like a bad guy despite his actions. At first, Espio treated Silver like an enemy, but as he learned that Silver was working to save the world, he decided to follow his instincts and help him with his mission. During their adventure, the two of them built a good relationship as Espio proved his trustworthiness and helped Silver on many occasions. After finishing their mission, they gladly thanked each other, showing the great admiration and faith they had gained for one another.

Espio and Silver are distant allies and fighting partners. They were comrades when Knuckles led the Resistance during the Eggman War, though Silver hints that they know each other for a long time and may have worked together. Though they only meet when their timelines are in danger, Espio and Silver seem quite close to each other. Espio seems at ease around Silver to let his guard down and even open up to him. He is also cautious about Silver's well-being and recognizes when something bothers him. Their relationship reinforced when Espio sees Silver leave and followed him to know what troubled him. Silver denies by saying he is fine, but Espio responds that he is a bad liar. While Silver explains that he is angry, they open up to each other and discover common points, notably their fear of losing. Espio then explains to Silver how he does not give up to his fears by helping and protecting his friends, and encourages Silver to do the same. His words comforted Silver to keep fighting for his principles, but also for the ones he loves and does not want to lose. He then thanks Espio with a hug, to the latter's surprise.


## Sonic the Hedgehog


Espio trusts Sonic the Hedgehog as a close ally and has recognized his skills as a fighter. Sonic similarly trusts Espio and considers both him and the rest of Team Chaotix to be the best detectives he knows, although that is not saying much, as they are also the only detectives he knows.

Sonic sees Espio as a trusted person to fight alongside with and has trusted him to help with several missions and cases. The two of them can harmonize with each other on the battlefield. Overall, the pair get along quite well; even with Espio's serious demeanor, he has actually cracked a joke with Sonic before. However, Espio's patience has been tested with Sonic before; he disagreed with Sonic about how to deal with Eggman, believing that the doctor should be punished. However, his patience was put to the test when, due to Sonic's decision to leave a reformed Eggman be, led to Eggman returning to his old ways and releasing the Metal Virus scourge that ended up claiming Vector and Charmy. Nonetheless, Espio himself still knows that Sonic means well, despite his brashness.


## Zazz


Espio first met Zazz during the Metal Virus pandemic in Riverside to retrieve the Chaos Emerald. Espio, despite not fearing him, was shocked that Zazz was able to find him in his Ninja Arts: Camouflage state. Espio would end up winning but would later fight Zazz during the Calendar Comedy. Espio does not see him as a threat or a strong opponent, as during their first fight, Espio kept his composure and did not panic speak. Espio would meet him again during the CrossWorld Grand Prix saying how he will not take him lightly.
