Amy Rose is a pink hedgehog who has had a persistent romantic interest in
Sonic since her debut (originally as "Princess Sally" in early Japanese
materials before being redesigned and renamed for Sonic CD in 1993). She
wields the Piko Piko Hammer, an oversized mallet, as her primary weapon
across most of her playable appearances.

## Character evolution

Early appearances lean heavily on Amy's crush on Sonic and her tendency to
chase him. Later games, particularly from Sonic Advance onward and more
markedly in modern titles like Sonic Frontiers, develop her into a more
independent, capable fighter with her own motivations beyond pursuing Sonic,
while generally retaining an upbeat, determined, and caring personality.

## Notable appearances

Amy has starred in her own campaigns (Sonic Adventure, where she protects a
bird named Birdie from Dr. Eggman while being pursued by the robot E-102
Gamma), and has been a consistently playable character across spin-offs like
Sonic Heroes (as part of "Team Rose") and Sonic Advance. She's also
associated with Cream the Rabbit, a younger character she frequently mentors
or partners with in several games.
